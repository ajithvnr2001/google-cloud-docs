# Looker Blocks and Dashboards overview

[Cortex Framework](https://docs.cloud.google.com/cortex/docs/v6/overview) offers [Looker Blocks](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/blocks) for some of the [Cortex data sources](https://docs.cloud.google.com/cortex/docs/v6/data-sources-and-workloads). Looker Blocks are prebuilt data models that can be used as a starting point for quick and flexible data modeling in Looker.

Looker Blocks and Dashboards for Cortex Framework are designed to provide immediate insights into your data. These blocks and dashboards are built on top of the pre-configured data models and pipelines within Cortex Framework, allowing you to quickly visualize and analyze key metrics related to your business operation.

## Key benefits

By utilizing these predefined blocks and dashboards, you can extract key insights from your data. These blocks and dashboards can be tailored and modified to align with your unique requirements and get the following benefits:

  * **Accelerate time to value:** Gain insights without extensive dashboard development.
  * **Get the best practice examples:** Learn from well-designed visualizations created by the Cortex team.
  * **Customize according to your needs:** Customize the dashboards to fit your specific needs and preferences.
  * **Extend your insights beyond operations** : Integrate other datasets in Cortex Framework, which provide reporting views for enterprise data sources such as CRM and ERP systems.

## Looker Blocks and Dashboards deployment

While the prebuilt dashboards provide a solid foundation, you can customize them to align with your specific reporting needs. For instructions about deploying the Looker Block from each repository, see [Deploy Looker Blocks for Cortex Framework](https://docs.cloud.google.com/cortex/docs/v6/looker-block-deployment). Consider that each data source might have some [Additional specifications for deployment](https://docs.cloud.google.com/cortex/docs/v6/looker-block-sap#additional_specifications_for_deployment) and you can find them in its corresponding documentation.

For getting the graphical insights from Dun & Bradstreet data source, follow the instructions specified in [Data Studio Dashboard for Dun & Bradstreet](https://docs.cloud.google.com/cortex/docs/v6/looker-dashboard-dun-and-bradstreet).
