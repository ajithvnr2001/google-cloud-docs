#  Manage OS packages Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Linux 

You can store and manage private Debian packages and RPM packages in [Artifact Registry](https://docs.cloud.google.com/artifact-registry/docs) Apt and Yum repositories. Managing your OS packages in Artifact Registry provides: 

  * Control over the Linux packages that your organization installs on both [Compute Engine](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs) VMs and Linux VMs outside of Google Cloud.
  * Access to OS packages when VMs do not have external internet access or when public repositories have a service outage.



## What's next

  * Try the quickstart for [Debian packages](https://docs.cloud.google.com/artifact-registry/docs/os-packages/debian/apt-quickstart) or [RPM packages](https://docs.cloud.google.com/artifact-registry/docs/os-packages/rpm/yum-quickstart).
  * Read the overview for [Debian packages](https://docs.cloud.google.com/artifact-registry/docs/os-packages/debian) or [RPM packages](https://docs.cloud.google.com/artifact-registry/docs/os-packages/rpm).
