#  Feature deprecations Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

The deprecation policy that applies to Compute Engine is defined in Google Cloud's [terms of service (section 1)](https://cloud.google.com/terms/) as _Discontinuation of Services_. The deprecation policy only applies to the [features and services listed here](https://cloud.google.com/terms/deprecation).

After Google deprecates a Compute Engine feature, the feature is available for at least the period of time defined in the [terms of service](https://cloud.google.com/terms/). After this period of time, Google schedules the feature or service for shutdown.

The following table lists Compute Engine's feature deprecations and their shutdown schedule.

Feature | Deprecated | Shutdown date | For more information  
---|---|---|---  
NVIDIA P4 | August 1, 2026 | August 1, 2027 | [NVIDIA P4 end of support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/eol/p4-eos)  
NVIDIA T4 | August 1, 2026 | August 1, 2027 | [NVIDIA T4 end of support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/eol/t4-eos)  
Customer-supplied encryption keys (CSEKs) | June 15, 2026 | June 16, 2027 | [Deprecation of CSEKs in Compute Engine](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/deprecations/csek-deprecation-in-compute-engine)  
Compute Engine Usage Report | January 20, 2026 | March 30, 2026 | [Compute Engine usage report deprecation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/deprecations/usage-export)  
NVIDIA P100 | September 15, 2025 | September 15, 2026 | [NVIDIA P100 end of support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/eol/p100-eos)  
Container startup agent in Compute Engine | July 21, 2025 | July 31, 2026 | [Compute Engine container startup agent deprecation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/deprecations/container-startup-agent-on-compute)  
Serial console server SSH key endpoint | March 31, 2025 | March 31, 2026 | [Serial console server SSH key endpoint deprecation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/deprecations/serial-console-ssh-host-key-endpoint)  
OS Login POSIX groups support | September 26, 2024 | September 26, 2025 | [OS Login POSIX groups support deprecation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/deprecations/oslogin-groups)

Throttling metrics for persistent disks | June 23, 2022 | June 22, 2023 | [Throttling rates](https://docs.cloud.google.com/compute/docs/disks/review-disk-metrics#throttling_metrics)
