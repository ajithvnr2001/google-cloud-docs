#  About our third-party partners Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Google Cloud works with many reputable [suppliers and staffing partners](https://about.google/extended-workforce/) globally that provide specialized services that range from sales, customer service, and engineering functions. In an era of specialization, global supply chains, and project-based work, this approach is widespread among many large companies. Our longstanding [Supplier Code of Conduct](https://about.google/supplier-code-of-conduct/) governs our work with all suppliers and staffing partners.

Employees of our suppliers and staffing partners are members of our extended workforce and have an email address that ends with a unique domain – `@xwf.google.com`. If you receive communications from an email with this domain on behalf of Google Cloud, please be assured that the email address is legitimate.

We thank you for your understanding and your continued support.
