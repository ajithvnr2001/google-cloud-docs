Last reviewed 2026-04-20 UTC

[Google Distributed Cloud air-gapped](https://docs.cloud.google.com/distributed-cloud/hosted/docs/latest/gdcag/overview) services meet demanding US government compliance requirements that are verified through formal third-party authorizations, including the Impact Level 6 (IL6) provisional authorizations (PA) issued by the Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA).

For more information, see the Google [U.S. Department of Defense (DoD)](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/disa) compliance offerings page.

This document provides a detailed list of Distributed Cloud air-gapped services in scope for DoD IL6 provisional authorizations.
