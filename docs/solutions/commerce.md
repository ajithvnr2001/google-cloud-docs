[![The Shopping agent with Gemini for customer experience understands a shopper's personalized needs to manage the entire journey from discovery to checkout.](https://www.gstatic.com/bricks/image/252424aa-26e3-4c78-8a59-73eb68d2b4d2.png)_web_traffic_ Learn more](https://cloud.google.com/products/gemini-enterprise-for-customer-experience)

The Shopping agent with [Gemini for customer experience](https://cloud.google.com/products/gemini-enterprise-for-customer-experience) understands a shopper's personalized needs to manage the entire journey from discovery to checkout. It helps identify products from customer requests, suggest options based on real-time availability, builds a cart, and streamlines purchases. For retailers, the agent increases conversion rates, order sizes, and customer engagement, all while optimizing operational efficiency by handling routine interactions and providing valuable data insights. Coupled with [AI Commerce Search](https://cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/commerce) which leverages Google's query understanding and search expertise, this creates a powerful combination to enhance retail product discovery experiences.

The introduction of [Agentic commerce](https://cloud.google.com/transform/agentic-commerce-retailers-can-prepare-for-the-new-shopping-era-ai) fundamentally changes the shopping paradigm, moving beyond consumer navigation to autonomous AI agent interaction. Consumers experience a highly efficient, personalized, and coherent shopping experience across apps and devices. For retailers, this transformative shift enables new interaction models, allowing personal agents to engage with merchant agents to fulfill requests and source items—creating a powerful new network for collaboration and revenue capture.

The [Agent Payments Protocol](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/announcing-agents-to-payments-ap2-protocol) (AP2), is an open standard designed to enable secure, agent-led payments in AI-driven commerce. Shoppers benefit from a consistent, secure experience when using agents for smarter, personalized, or delegated purchases, while retailers gain opportunities for new sales and increased conversion by building a trusted foundation for agentic commerce.

#### What are the benefits of ecommerce modernization on Google Cloud and how does it help adapt to market changes faster?

Working with Google Cloud on ecommerce modernization means transitioning from older, rigid systems to modular, microservices-based architectures. This allows for faster innovation and agility, which is critical in today's rapidly changing retail environment. This agility means retailers can adapt faster to shifts in customer behavior or economic conditions, staying competitive and responsive. By moving away from legacy platforms to composable architectures, retailers can launch new services or updates without disrupting their entire system, saving time and resources.

#### How does Google Cloud help retail businesses during peak seasons?

Google Cloud provides a reliable, secure, and scalable cloud platform that can handle high traffic volumes and peak demand periods. This ensures a smooth and seamless customer experience across all channels. By having a platform that can easily scale up or down as needed, retailers can avoid website crashes or slowdowns during busy periods like holidays or promotions. This reliability enhances customer satisfaction, as they can shop without frustration, leading to more completed purchases. Ultimately, this results in increased revenue and a positive brand image.

#### How can Google Cloud help retailers optimize their supply chain and reduce costs?

Google Cloud provides tools for forecasting and BigQuery for data warehousing to improve demand forecasting, optimize inventory management, and reduce overall supply chain costs. The value for the retailer is significant: by using AI to predict demand accurately, retailers can avoid overstocking or stockouts, reducing storage costs and lost sales. Data analytics allow for deeper insights into supply chain operations, identifying bottlenecks, inefficiencies, and areas for cost reduction. This leads to a more agile and cost-effective supply chain, ultimately increasing profitability and customer satisfaction through better product availability.

#### How can Google Cloud’s associate productivity solutions empower our store employees and improve customer service?

Google Cloud provides AI-powered tools that equip store associates with real-time information and enable personalized assistance. The value for the retailer is improved employee efficiency and enhanced customer service. With these tools, associates can quickly access inventory information, product details, and customer preferences, allowing them to answer questions accurately and provide tailored recommendations. This not only improves the shopping experience but also reduces the time associates spend on manual tasks, allowing them to focus on direct customer interaction. Empowered and informed associates lead to happier customers and a more productive workforce.

#### How does Google Cloud support retailers in achieving their sustainability goals?

Google Cloud helps retailers achieve their sustainability goals by providing solutions to monitor and optimize energy consumption across their enterprise, stores, and distribution centers. These tools track and measure environmental impact, including carbon footprint, water usage, and waste. Google Cloud also assists in generating ESG reports, and offers 24/7 carbon-free energy, leading to lower operational costs, a sustainable future, and enhanced brand image through demonstrated environmental responsibility. 

#### How does Google Cloud support multicloud strategies?

Google Cloud supports robust multicloud strategies through our commitment to openness and interoperable technologies. We provide consistent platforms like Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) for managing applications across environments, and our Google Distributed Cloud solutions extend our infrastructure where you need it, ensuring you can leverage the best of all clouds without being locked in.

#### How long does it take to move to Google Cloud?

The time it takes to migrate to Google Cloud depends on the scope and complexity of the project. Factors like the amount of data across the organization and the specific migration strategy play a role in determining the overall timeline. Google Cloud and/or your partner of choice will help you scope your project, set priorities, and determine a realistic timeline.
