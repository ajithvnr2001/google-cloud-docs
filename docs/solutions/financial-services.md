#### How does Google Cloud Storage ensure my data privacy with AI?

Customers always own their data. When using Gemini platform, your proprietary data used for fine-tuning is kept private and is not used to train Google's foundation models. Robust controls protect data confidentiality.

AI agents are software systems that understand goals and act autonomously (or semi-autonomously) to complete tasks. They can boost employee productivity, enhance customer experiences, automate complex workflows, and unlock data insights, driving efficiency and growth.
