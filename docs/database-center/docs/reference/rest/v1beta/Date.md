#  Date Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

  * JSON representation



Represents a whole or partial calendar date, such as a birthday. The time of day and time zone are either specified elsewhere or are insignificant. The date is relative to the Gregorian Calendar. This can represent one of the following:

  * A full date, with non-zero year, month, and day values.
  * A month and day, with a zero year (for example, an anniversary).
  * A year on its own, with a zero month and a zero day.
  * A year and month, with a zero day (for example, a credit card expiration date).



Related types:

  * `[google.type.TimeOfDay](https://docs.cloud.google.com/database-center/docs/reference/rest/v1beta/TimeOfDay)`
  * `google.type.DateTime`
  * `[google.protobuf.Timestamp](https://protobuf.dev/reference/protobuf/google.protobuf/#timestamp)`

JSON representation  
---  
      
    
    {
      "year": integer,
      "month": integer,
      "day": integer
    }  
  
Fields  
---  
`year` |  `integer` Year of the date. Must be from 1 to 9999, or 0 to specify a date without a year.  
`month` |  `integer` Month of a year. Must be from 1 to 12, or 0 to specify a year without a month and day.  
`day` |  `integer` Day of a month. Must be from 1 to 31 and valid for the year and month, or 0 to specify a year by itself or a year and month where the day isn't significant.
