**GDC Ready platform qualification**

GDC Ready platform validation ensures that our partners' hardware platforms support the latest version and provide the foundation for Google Distributed Cloud (software only) on bare metal and VMware. This validation includes testing the following:

  * The functions most reliant on the underlying hardware platform such as the ability to deploy and scale a cluster
  * The successful interaction of GDC with the platform partner's compute, network, and storage solution
  * The ability to deploy on-premises and expose a workload through a supported load balancer option on GDC clusters on the partner platform



GDC Ready platform partners have also demonstrated a strong understanding of GDC architectures, its features, and how customers benefit from all components of GDC. Google commits to working with GDC Ready platform partners to ensure that they have the most up-to-date validation regarding GDC and its roadmap, and their hardware works with the latest version of GDC. Google Cloud and the partner will jointly look at ways to improve the customer experience.

You can find out more about the program in [GDC Ready partners](https://docs.cloud.google.com/kubernetes-engine/enterprise/docs/resources/anthos-ready-partners).

**Note:** The platform information on this page, including the Linux operating system (OS) versions for Google Distributed Cloud (software only) on bare metal, is validated by our partners. The OS versions might be out of date and no longer supported. For the latest OS versions validated by our partners, contact them directly. For the latest OS versions qualified by Google, see [Select your operating system](https://docs.cloud.google.com/kubernetes-engine/distributed-cloud/bare-metal/docs/installing/os-reqs) in the Google Distributed Cloud (software only) on bare metal documentation.
