#  Migrating your workloads to other products Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Learn how to migrate your workloads out of Knative serving. The process of migrating your workloads includes individually porting over each of your services to run in another product.

For example, as an alternative to [upgrading your previous "free trial" installation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/kubernetes-engine/enterprise/knative-serving/docs/install/on-gcp/upgrade) to use fleets, you can choose to migrate your workloads to other products.

Use one of the following guides to learn how to migrate your workloads:

  * [Migrating a service to Cloud Run](https://docs.cloud.google.com/anthos/run/docs/migrate/cloud-run)
  * [Migrating a service to Knative on GKE](https://docs.cloud.google.com/anthos/run/docs/migrate/knative)
