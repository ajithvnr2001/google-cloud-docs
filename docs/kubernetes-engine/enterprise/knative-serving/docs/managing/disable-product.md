#  Disabling Knative serving Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

You can disable Knative serving to disable all of your services across all the clusters in your fleet.

To disable Knative serving:

  1. Go to the **Feature Manager** page in the Google Cloud console:

[Go to Feature Manager](https://console.cloud.google.com/kubernetes/features)

  2. In the Knative serving panel, click **Details**.

  3. In the details panel, click **Disable Knative serving**.

  4. Click **Disable Knative serving** again to disable the feature.
