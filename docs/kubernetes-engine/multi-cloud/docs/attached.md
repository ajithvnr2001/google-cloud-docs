#  GKE attached clusters 

With GKE attached clusters you can manage any standard, [ CNCF-compliant](https://www.cncf.io/certification/software-conformance/) Kubernetes installation, including clusters already in production. Then add [GKE](https://docs.cloud.google.com/kubernetes-engine) features to standardize and secure your clusters across multiple cloud environments and Kubernetes vendors. 

### Standardize

GKE attached clusters can automatically install [Config Sync](https://docs.cloud.google.com/anthos-config-management/docs/config-sync-overview) and [Policy Controller](https://docs.cloud.google.com/anthos-config-management/docs/concepts/policy-controller) on your clusters, so your platform team can maintain consistent configurations and security policies across all your Kubernetes environments. App teams are free to use their continuous delivery tool of choice with policies in place to provide necessary safeguards. 

### Secure

You can use [Policy Controller](https://docs.cloud.google.com/anthos-config-management/docs/concepts/policy-controller) and [Cloud Service Mesh](https://docs.cloud.google.com/anthos/service-mesh) to apply security and network configurations across all your cluster configurations. Additionally, you can enable valuable intra-cluster telemetry. 

### Manage

Use the [Connect gateway](https://docs.cloud.google.com/anthos/multicluster-management/gateway#why_use_the_connect_gateway) to connect to clusters across environments without proxies, inbound firewall rules, or bastion hosts.
