# Blockchain Analytics documentation

[Read product documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/blockchain-analytics/docs/overview)

Blockchain Analytics offers indexed blockchain data made available through [BigQuery](https://docs.cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs) for easy analysis through SQL. Starting with [Ethereum](https://ethereum.org), Blockchain Analytics offers you access to reliable data without the overhead of operating nodes or developing and maintaining an indexer.
