For help with Google Cloud products, click **Support** at the top right of the Google Cloud site.

## Get a Google support package

Google Cloud offers different support packages to meet different needs, such as 24/7 coverage, phone support, and access to a technical support manager. For more information, see [Cloud Customer Care](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support).

## Get support from the community

### Discuss Dataproc Metastore

Join the [dataproc-metastore-discuss](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!forum/dataproc-metastore-discuss) Google group to discuss Dataproc Metastore and receive Dataproc Metastore announcements and updates.

## File bugs or feature requests

To provide documentation or product feedback to the Dataproc Metastore team, click **Send feedback** at the top right of the [Dataproc Metastore documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/dataproc-metastore/docs).

You can also send your feedback or support requests to [dataproc-metastore-support@google.com](mailto:dataproc-metastore-support@google.com).
