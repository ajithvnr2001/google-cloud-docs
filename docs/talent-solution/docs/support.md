# Support

## Support package

Google Cloud Platform offers different [support packages](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/role-based) to meet different needs, such as 24/7 coverage, phone support, and access to a technical support manager.

See the [Job Search Troubleshooting Guide](https://cloud.google.com/talent-solution/docs/troubleshooting) for help with common search result problems. 

To purchase and setup support for your organization, your [Organization Administrator](https://cloud.google.com/resource-manager/docs/creating-managing-organization#adding_an_organization_admin) must:

  1. Visit the [support cases](https://console.cloud.google.com/support/cases) area in Google Cloud console.
  2. Enable role-based support for your organization.
  3. [Assign members](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eJJiGfL8vbI) of your organization so they can [create and/or view](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G6DNQdAkWSY) support cases.

If the member of your business is unable to enable Cloud Support, it's likely either: 

  * They are not the Organization Administrator.
  * Your business doesn't have an [organization](https://cloud.google.com/resource-manager/docs/creating-managing-organization) in Google Cloud console, which typically starts with setting up the free version of [Cloud Identity](https://gsuite.google.com/signup/gcpidentity/welcome).



## Submit product or documentation feedback

You can submit feedback about Cloud Talent Solution or its documentation without having a support package. To submit feedback, click the **Send Feedback** button at the top-right of any page of the documentation.
