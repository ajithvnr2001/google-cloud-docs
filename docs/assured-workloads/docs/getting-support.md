# Getting support

Google Cloud offers specific support services to meet specific customer needs, such as 24/7 coverage, multi-channel support, and access to subject-matter experts.

A support subscription is not required to create and use Assured Workloads folders. However, to receive support from personnel that meet your control package's requirements, you must have an active [Enhanced Support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/enhanced) or [Premium Support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/premium) subscription. An Enhanced Support subscription is available for purchase directly in the Google Cloud console. See [Purchasing and setting up Enhanced Support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/purchasing-setting-up-enhanced) for more information. To purchase Premium Support, contact [sales](https://cloud.google.com/contact).

You can receive support for your Assured Workloads folders from either Google or select partners. For more information about each support type, see the Google-led Assured Support and Partner-led Assured Support sections.

## Google-led Assured Support

When you create a support case for projects in Assured Workloads folders with an active Enhanced Support or Premium Support subscription, your support case is routed to personnel that, where applicable, meet requirements for work location, adjudication status, HR verification, data access controls, and more. These requirements are determined by the control package your Assured Workloads folder has been configured for. For select control packages, this includes [Assured Support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/enhanced#assured_support).

**Note:** Some support cases may require escalation handling or technical consultation with Google support personnel who don't meet the work location or adjudication requirements for a given data boundary. However, such personnel don't have access to [Customer Data](https://cloud.google.com/terms).

For control packages that don't include support, the support personnel handling your case are not restricted to a particular work location or adjudication status. FedRAMP Moderate workloads are adjudicated but are not restricted to a specific work location. Support personnel for regional data boundaries included in the Assured Workloads _Free tier_ are not restricted to a specific work location or adjudication status. These support personnel attributes apply regardless of whether or not you have an active Enhanced Support or Premium Support subscription.

If you have [Standard Support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/standard) and you create a new case, the support personnel handling your case are not restricted to a particular work location or adjudication status, regardless of the control package your Assured Workloads folder has been configured for. For example, if your Assured Workloads folder is configured for the US Data Boundary and Support control package, the support personnel handling your case are not limited to only US Persons located in the US.

If you have Basic Support, you cannot create support cases for Assured Workloads.

For more information, read the following sections:

  * Regional data boundaries support
  * Regulatory data boundaries support



For a list of partner-managed offerings, see the [Sovereign Controls by Partners](https://docs.cloud.google.com/sovereign-controls-by-partners/docs/supported-products).

### Open a support case

**Caution:** Failure to select the appropriate project associated with your Assured Workloads workload can result in the case and all details provided within the case request being routed to global support personnel, who are unable to access Assured Workloads project data to provide effective support.

Assured Workloads technical support services are only available for customers who have purchased Assured Support. For questions about the Assured Workloads product, open a support case specifying the control package that is most relevant to your issue when you open a case.

**Field** | **Specification**  
---|---  
Product | **Assured Workloads**  
Category | Select the appropriate control package for your issue  
  
To open a support case, see [Support Procedures](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/procedures). You are responsible for ensuring that sensitive information is not shared within your Support case.

## Partner-led Assured Support

Select partners offer partner-led Assured Support for some Assured Workloads control packages, and are authorized to provide first-level and second-level support. The following partners have met the requirements of the partner-led Assured Support program for the following control packages:

Partner name | Control package | Support description  
---|---|---  
[SADA](https://cloud.google.com/find-a-partner/partner/sada) |  |   
---  
[US Data Boundary and Support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/docs/control-packages/us-data-boundary-support) |  First-level and second-level support is provided by US Persons located in the US, and only in English.   
  
[Operator Chmury Krajowej (OChK)](https://cloud.google.com/find-a-partner/partner/operator-chmury-krajowej) |  |   
---  
[EU Data Boundary and Support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/docs/control-packages/eu-data-boundary-support) |  First-level and second-level support is provided by EU personnel located in the EU, and only in English.   
  
Your partner is your first point of contact for support. They are responsible for upholding the personnel access controls required by the control packages that they're authorized to support. For control packages without built-in personnel access restrictions, partners may offer localized first-level and second-level support.

When a case is escalated to Google for third-level support, Google's commitments are limited to those as described for the applicable control package in the Google-led Assured Support section. If stricter, more granular access controls for Google support personnel are required, we recommend using [Access Approval](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/access-approval/docs).
