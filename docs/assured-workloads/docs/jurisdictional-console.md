# Jurisdictional Google Cloud console

Assured Workloads provides [control packages](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/docs/control-packages) to help you meet regulatory, regional, or sovereign compliance requirements. Some control packages enforce [data residency](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/docs/data-residency), which keeps your data within the jurisdiction you selected.

If your workload requires this control, always access your Google Cloud resources using one of the jurisdictional URLs below. When you do, your experience within the Google Cloud console may be altered when interacting with some Google Cloud services. For example, one or more services may be hidden if they don't fully meet your control package's data residency requirements. Other in-page content and functionality may also be hidden or modified compared to a workload without such requirements.

Don't enter any controlled information into free text fields, such as resource names and search queries.

  * [`console.us.cloud.google.com`](https://console.us.cloud.google.com)

## Kingdom of Saudi Arabia

  * [`console.sa.cloud.google.com`](https://console.sa.cloud.google.com)

**Note:** Depending on the [session length](https://support.google.com/a/answer/9368756) that your Google Workspace administrator has set, you may be prompted to reauthenticate to the jurisdictional Google Cloud console through a pop-up window in your browser. For easier reauthentication, ensure that your browser settings are configured to allow future pop-up windows from `console.sa.cloud.google.com`.
  * For federated identity users: [`console.sa.cloud.google`](https://console.sa.cloud.google)
