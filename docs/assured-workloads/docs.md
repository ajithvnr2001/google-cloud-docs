# Assured Workloads documentation

[Read product documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/docs/overview)

Assured Workloads lets you secure your workloads and accelerate your path to running compliant workloads on Google Cloud. [Go to the Assured Workloads product page for more.](https://cloud.google.com/security/products/assured-workloads)

Google Cloud offers multiple administrative access controls that aim to provide you progressively greater transparency and control over access to your content stored in Google Cloud. The key offerings are Access Transparency, Access Approval, and Key Access Justifications. 

Access Transparency aims to provide you clear and accurate information regarding access by Google employees to your content stored in Google Cloud. For more information, see [Access Transparency documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/access-transparency/docs). 

Access Approval aims to ensure that Google employees require your explicit approval to access your content stored on Google Cloud. For more information, see [Access Approval documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/access-approval/docs)

Key Access Justifications aim to give you visibility into every request for an encryption key, a justification for that request, and a mechanism to approve or deny decryption in the context of that request. For more information, see [Overview of Key Access Justifications](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/key-access-justifications/docs/overview).
