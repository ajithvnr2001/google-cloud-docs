#  Review and export estimation results Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

You can review the cost estimate for every environment that you added for evaluation. You can also export the results for presentation or further analysis.

## Review cloud cost estimate results

The results contain the following:

  * Estimated total cost for Google Cloud.

  * Estimated pricing for all the environments you included in your estimate. To view the cost breakdown for any environment, click **View details** in the results card for that environment.

Further, to see the details of how the cost is calculated for any component, click the component name.

These calculations are based on your inputs and the default assumptions.




## Adjust your results

At this point you can choose to add or remove any environment from your estimation results.

  * To add a new environment, click **Add another environment** and specify the required details for estimating the cost.
  * To remove an environment, click more_vert**More > Remove from estimate**.
  * To start adding details for another environment included in your estimate, click **Start** in the results card for the environment.
  * To edit details in an existing estimate and recalculate your cloud cost, click **Edit** in the results card for the environment.



## Export estimate results summary

  1. To export the estimate results summary to a PDF file or Google Sheets, click file_upload**Export**.

  2. Depending on which results summary you want to export and the export format you want, select the appropriate option.
