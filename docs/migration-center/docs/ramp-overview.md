#  Rapid Migration and Modernization Program (RaMP) overview Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

The Rapid Migration and Modernization Program (RaMP) is a Google Cloud global program for migration and modernization initiatives. RaMP combines Google Cloud products and best practices into a unified approach to help you achieve business goals and mitigate common challenges. RaMP provides a prescriptive framework, resources, and tools to help you execute migrations and modernizations to Google Cloud.

To learn more about RaMP, see the following pages:

  * [RaMP: your fast track to an AI-ready cloud](https://docs.cloud.google.com/solutions/cloud-migration-program)
  * [Fast-track your cloud migration with RaMP](https://cloud.google.com/consulting/ramp-rapid-migration-and-modernization-program)



## How RaMP works

RaMP follows the following phased approach to guide your migration and modernization journey.

### Assess

We work with your team to perform a thorough, data-driven discovery of your existing environment. Using our advanced tools, we help you identify the highest-value opportunities and workloads, perform total cost of ownership calculations, and establish performance benchmarks to start to maximize immediate business value.

In this phase, use Google Cloud Migration Center to discover source infrastructure and assessment to understand your existing environment.

  * [About infrastructure discovery and assessment](https://docs.cloud.google.com/migration-center/docs/discovery-and-assessment-overview)
  * [Start asset discovery](https://docs.cloud.google.com/migration-center/docs/start-asset-discovery)



### Plan

We work with your team to perform a thorough, data-driven discovery of your existing environment. Using our advanced tools, we help you identify the highest-value opportunities and workloads, perform total cost of ownership calculations, and establish performance benchmarks to start to maximize immediate business value.

In this phase, use Google Cloud Migration Center to organize assets into groups, generate TCO reports, and define migration waves:

  * [Start a cost estimation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/migration-center/docs/estimate/start-estimation)
  * [Review and export cost estimation results](https://docs.cloud.google.com/migration-center/docs/estimate/review-and-export-results)
  * [Collect asset data for one-time export](https://docs.cloud.google.com/migration-center/docs/discovery-client-cli-overview)
  * [Collect asset data and automatically upload it to Google Cloud](https://docs.cloud.google.com/migration-center/docs/discovery-client-overview)
  * [Directly import asset inventory data from AWS](https://docs.cloud.google.com/migration-center/docs/aws-import-data)
  * [Manually import data](https://docs.cloud.google.com/migration-center/docs/manual-import-overview)
  * [Detected software workloads](https://docs.cloud.google.com/migration-center/docs/software-workloads-insights)



### Migrate

We work with your team to perform a thorough, data-driven discovery of your existing environment. Using our advanced tools, we help you identify the highest-value opportunities and workloads, perform total cost of ownership calculations, and establish performance benchmarks to start to maximize immediate business value.

In this phase, use Google Cloud Migration Center to specific tools for lift and shift, replatforming, or refactoring applications to Google Cloud:

  * [Migration and modernization tools](https://docs.cloud.google.com/migration-center/docs/migration-modernization-tools)



### Innovate

We work with your team to perform a thorough, data-driven discovery of your existing environment. Using our advanced tools, we help you identify the highest-value opportunities and workloads, perform total cost of ownership calculations, and establish performance benchmarks to start to maximize immediate business value.

In this phase, use Google Cloud Migration Center to optimize workloads and use AI-powered tools like Google Cloud Migration Center App Modernization Assessment (`codmod`):

  * [Create an App Modernization Assessment](https://docs.cloud.google.com/migration-center/docs/app-modernization-assessment)
