This document describes how to unbox Google Distributed Cloud (GDC) air-gapped appliance.

## Crates for shipping the appliance

For secure and consolidated transit, each appliance ships in a wooden crate on a standard sized pallet (~48" x 40"). To ensure the security of each crate, a unique tamper-proof seal is applied to the locking mechanism on the crate doors. Each seal has its own unique ID number, which is tracked by the System Integrator within a secure facility. If any signs of tampering are detected, customers are instructed to immediately open a support case with our support team immediately.

The crate contains the following items:

  * The cabled GDC unit enclosed in a box. These cables are optional and are typically used for rack-mounting the appliance. However, for rack installation, remove the appliance from its ruggedized case.
  * Optional rail kits.
  * A pelican case containing power cables and ancillary materials.



**Crate specifications:**

  * Exterior (L×W×H) - 1.14 m × 1.35 m × 1.14 m
  * Weight - 176.9 kg



![Shipping crate designed for the safe transport of the appliance.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/distributed-cloud/hosted/docs/latest/appliance/admin/images/crate-appliance.png)

## Pelican case

Google recommends using the pelican case to securely transport the appliance, to ensure that these ancillary items and cables remain with their corresponding appliance during transit and whenever the appliance is moved. A tactical carrying case is necessary to safely ship and pair ancillary items like cables and power cords, which cannot be securely transported within the GDC's housing case.

**Pelican case specifications:**

  * Exterior (L×W×D) - 0.62 m × 0.49 m × 0.22 m



![Rugged Pelican case,for protecting sensitive equipment.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/distributed-cloud/hosted/docs/latest/appliance/admin/images/pelican-case.png)
