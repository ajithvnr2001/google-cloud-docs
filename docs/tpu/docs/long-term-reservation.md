# Request a future reservation for one year or longer

If you need exclusive access to TPUs for one year or longer, you can purchase a future reservation which has a committed use discount (CUD) attached. CUDs provide discounted prices for eligible Google Cloud resources in exchange for purchasing a committed use contract. CUDs have a duration of one or three years.

To purchase a future reservation with a CUD for Cloud TPU, contact [Google Cloud Sales](https://cloud.google.com/contact). For information about pricing, see [Cloud TPU pricing](https://cloud.google.com/tpu/pricing).

## Limitations

  * You can't renew, extend, merge, split, or upgrade commitments that you purchased through future reservations for Cloud TPUs.
