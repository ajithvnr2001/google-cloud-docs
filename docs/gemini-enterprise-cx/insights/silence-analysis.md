#  Silence analysis Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

The silence analysis feature detects and measures periods of silence longer than five seconds during voice calls between a human agent and an end user. This feature reports the silence measurement as a percentage of the overall conversation. You can configure silence analysis with [analysis rules](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/insights/analysis-rule).
