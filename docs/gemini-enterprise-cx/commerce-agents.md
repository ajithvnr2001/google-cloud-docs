#  Commerce agents Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Commerce agents provide complete end-to-end solutions for commerce-specific use cases. They connect frontend user interfaces like chat and voice directly to backend tools such as product searches and adding items to a shopping cart.

They expand beyond simple chatbots by handling complex requests using complex reasoning, multimodal interactions and executing consented actions.
