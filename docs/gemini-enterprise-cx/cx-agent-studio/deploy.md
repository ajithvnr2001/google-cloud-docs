#  Deploy agent applications Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Once you have built an agent application and tested it, you can deploy agent application [versions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/cx-agent-studio/version) in a variety of ways:

  * [Web widget](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/cx-agent-studio/deploy/web-widget): Provides a configurable, ready to use web interface widget.
  * Platform connections: 
    * [AudioCodes](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/cx-agent-studio/deploy/audiocodes): Connect to your AudioCodes platform.
    * [Five9](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/cx-agent-studio/deploy/five9): Connect to your Five9 voice calls.
    * [Google Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS)](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/cx-agent-studio/deploy/ccaas): Connect to a Google Cloud Contact Center as a Service instance in voice mode.
    * [Google Telephony Platform](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/cx-agent-studio/deploy/google-telephony-platform): Connect to a new Google Telephony Platform phone number.
    * [SecureCo](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/cx-agent-studio/deploy/secureco): Connect to your SecureCo voice calls.
    * [Twilio](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/cx-agent-studio/deploy/twilio): Connect to your Twilio voice calls.
    * [WhatsApp and Instagram](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/cx-agent-studio/deploy/whatsapp): Connect to your WhatsApp or Instagram conversations.
  * [API access](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/cx-agent-studio/deploy/api-access): Connect using the API.



## Configure a deployment channel

To configure a deployment channel:

  1. Click the **Deploy** button at the top of the agent builder.
  2. Select **Web widget** , **Platform** , or **API access**.
  3. Provide configuration details specific to the channel you've chosen.



Some deployment options also let you choose the modality. Choose **Text** or **Voice** for text or voice conversations. You can reference channel and modality types in instructions, callbacks, and Python tools.
