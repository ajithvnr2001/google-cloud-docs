The advanced preview functionality is housed within the Merchandising console. Its primary purpose is to allow users to preview the serving experience and confirm that their specific serving configurations are working as expected before those changes are pushed live to the website code.

## Access the preview in Merchandising console

To access the preview:

  1. Click **Home** home  in the Merchandising console navigational panel.

  2. Enter your test query, such as **shoes**.




### Preview query controls

Click a product in the preview to view preview each component of the product query.

  * **Product details**
  * **Filters**
  * **Controls applied**



![Click on a product to view product details.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/gemini-enterprise-cx/ai-commerce-search/images/merchandising-click-product-view-details.png)

### Preview controls applied

On the **Preview** page, click **Query controls** to open the pane for controls over the query. Or to find a product's specific control, click the up-arrow on any product tile.

![Preview behavior when the control is applied](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/gemini-enterprise-cx/ai-commerce-search/images/merchandising-preview-control-applied-pinning.png)

The **Query controls** pane includes several capabilities to help you visualize or **Preview** your search behavior by query and by product:

  * **Highlight _control name:_** This option selects the products to which it's applied.



![Highlight boost or bury on products](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/gemini-enterprise-cx/ai-commerce-search/images/merchandising-highlight-boost-bury.png)

  * **_add_box_ Create control**: This is a shortcut that lets you create a control directly from the **Query controls** pane.
