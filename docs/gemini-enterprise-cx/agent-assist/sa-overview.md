**Caution:** This feature has limited access. Contact your Google Cloud representative to access private GA features.

When an end user connects through the [bidirectional API](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/agent-assist/bidi-stream-api) to either a [Dialogflow CX playbook live agent](https://docs.cloud.google.com/dialogflow/cx/docs/quick/build-agent-playbook) or a [generative agent](https://docs.cloud.google.com/customer-engagement-ai/conversational-agents/ps), supervisor assist monitors the interaction and escalates the conversation to human supervisors for review and intervention. This automated conversation monitoring provides the following benefits for your contact center.

## Enhance customer experience

To ensure a consistently high-quality customer experience, supervisors have real-time visibility into conversational agents' performance and ongoing conversations. With this visibility, supervisors can proactively identify and address issues.

## Improve containment rates

Supervisors can monitor and intervene in conversations. They can guide conversations toward a successful resolution, which significantly improves containment rates.

## Prevent churn

An advanced trigger mechanism that automatically flags high-risk conversations also allows supervisors to intervene before a customer becomes frustrated or a situation escalates. Such interventions prevent churn and protect your brand.

## Create revenue opportunities

Supervisors have the ability to identify conversations with upsell opportunities and transfer them directly to a sales agent. This ability turns routine support calls into a valuable revenue stream.

## Accelerate AI deployment

You can launch conversational agent capabilities faster by mitigating risk with human oversight. This hybrid approach ensures a smooth transition for your customers and support teams.

## Human-agent collaboration

Collaboration between supervisors and conversational agents enables the next generation of customer support experiences.

## AI coach integration

To improve automated conversation monitoring, you can also enable [AI coach](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/agent-assist/ai-coach-overview) for [supervisor assist](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-cx/agent-assist/sa-human-agent#choose-alerts).
