## Contact Google Cloud support

Google Cloud offers different support packages to meet different needs, such as 24/7 coverage, phone support, and access to a technical support manager. For more information, see [Google Cloud Support Plans](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support).

If you already have a support package, you can contact [Google Cloud Support](https://cloud.google.com/support-hub).

## Send feedback to Managed Microsoft AD

Send your feedback to [google-cloud-managed-microsoft-ad-discuss@googlegroups.com](mailto:google-cloud-managed-microsoft-ad-discuss@googlegroups.com) or by selecting **Help > Send feedback** in the Google Cloud console to open a feedback form. The Google Cloud team reviews and takes appropriate action on your feedback.

Join the [Google Cloud Managed AD Updates](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!forum/google-cloud-managed-ad-updates) Google group to receive Managed Microsoft AD announcements and updates.
