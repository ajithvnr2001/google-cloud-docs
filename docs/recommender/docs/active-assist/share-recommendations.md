# Share recommendations

This page shows you how to share recommendations with others who have the permissions to view them.

## Before you begin

Ensure that you have the [necessary roles and permissions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/recommender/docs/active-assist/find-active-assist#before_you_begin) to view recommendations.

## Share a single recommendation

Perform the following steps to share a single recommendation:

  1. Navigate to Active Assist.

[Go to Active Assist](https://console.cloud.google.com/active-assist)

     * To view all of your recommendations, click **All recommendations** from the left-hand navigation.

     * To view recommendations for a specific value pillar, such as **Cost** or **Security** , click the name of the respective pillar. The list of pillars is displayed in the left-hand navigation, as well as in the **Category** column of the **All recommendations** card.

  2. Identify the recommendation you want to share, then click the adjacent overflow menu (the three dots).

  3. Click **Share** to copy a link to the recommendation.




You can also share the recommendation from its detail page. To do so, click the link icon found within the detail page in order to copy a link to the detail page.

## Share a set of recommendations

Perform the following steps to share a set of recommendations:

  1. Navigate to Active Assist.

[Go to Active Assist](https://console.cloud.google.com/active-assist)

     * To view all of your recommendations, click **All recommendations** from the left-hand navigation.

     * To view recommendations for a specific value pillar, such as **Cost** or **Security** , click the name of the respective pillar. The list of pillars is displayed in the left-hand navigation, as well as in the **Category** column of the **All recommendations** card.

  2. Using the recommendation table, filter for a set of recommendations. You can apply multiple filters at a time.

  3. Click **Copy URL** in the recommendation table. This copies a link to the Active Assist's category page with your filters applied.
