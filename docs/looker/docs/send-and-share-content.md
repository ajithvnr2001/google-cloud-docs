#  Send and share content Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

One of Looker's advantages is the many ways you can send and share content to colleagues or those outside your organization. This page outlines the documentation on sending and sharing content. 

The following pages will help you start sending and sharing content:

  * [Sharing data](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/sharing-data) — Get an overview of ways to share Looker data with others.
  * [Sharing data from URLs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/sharing-urls) — Share data using browser, short, or expanded URLs.
  * [Downloading content](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/downloading) — Download data or visualizations from Looks and Explores, and format those downloads.
  * [Public sharing, importing, and embedding of Looks](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/publishing-looks-with-public-urls) — Give public access to data using URLs, embedding, or placement in spreadsheets.
  * [Using the Looker Scheduler to deliver content](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/scheduling) — Make instant or recurring deliveries of Explores, Looks, legacy user-defined dashboards, and LookML dashboards to email, webhook, Amazon S3, SFTP, and some integrated services.
  * [Scheduling deliveries to the Slack integration](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/scheduling-slack) — Schedule deliveries of Looker data directly to Slack using Looker's Slack integration.
  * [Sharing data through an action hub](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/action-hub) — Configure an action hub server to deliver content to third-party services integrated with Looker.
  * [Alerts](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/alerts-overview) — Create alerts that are triggered when query results reach or exceed a specified threshold.
