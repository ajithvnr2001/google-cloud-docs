#  Viewing alerts Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

View alerts that you or other users have created from a dashboard tile or the [**Manage Alerts** user page](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/user-alerts).

## Viewing alerts from a dashboard tile

If your Looker admin has granted you [permissions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/alerts-for-admins) to [create](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/creating-alerts) or [follow](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/following-alerts) alerts, the add_alert **Alerts** icon appears when you hold your pointer over the dashboard tile. A numeric indicator shows how many alerts you're permitted to view for that tile, even alerts that don't include you as a recipient and that you aren't following.

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/modal-alert-icon-2216.png)

Click the **Alerts** button on the top right of the tile to display a list of alerts that you are permitted to see based on your permissions. You may need to scroll to see the entire list.

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/modal-alert-view-2216.png)

A description below the alert title shows the details of that alert's notification scheme. For example, when the conditions that are specified in the first alert (listed in the previous image) are true, a notification will be sent every day at 5:00 AM. This alert is subject to one [dashboard filter](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/creating-alerts#editing_a_dashboard_filter). The description does not include the alert notification method.

## Viewing alerts from the Manage Alerts user page

You can view additional details and history information about alerts that you are following or that you have created on [dashboard tiles](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/viewing-dashboards#alerts) from the [**Alert Details** page](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/user-alerts#viewing_the_alert_details_page), available on the [**Manage Alerts** user page](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/user-alerts).
