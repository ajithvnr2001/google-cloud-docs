#  Installing the mobile application on your mobile device Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

If **Mobile Application Access** is [enabled for your instance](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/mobile-app-enablement), you can sign in to your Looker account on an Android or iOS device.

Once you have installed the Looker app, you can [sign in to your instance](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/mobile-app-sign-in) to view your [favorite and most recently viewed Looks and dashboards](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/mobile-app-navigating-to-content#viewing_your_most_recently_viewed_or_favorite_content), view [boards](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/mobile-app-navigating-to-content#navigating_to_content_from_boards), browse [content in folders](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/mobile-app-navigating-to-content#browsing_content_in_folders), and [share content](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/mobile-app-sharing-content) from the app.

## Device requirements

To use the Looker app, your mobile device must meet the following requirements:

  * iOS device on iOS 13 or higher
  * Android device on Android 8 or higher



## Installing the Looker app

To download the Looker mobile app, search for **Looker** in the Apple App Store or the Google Play Store or use the following links:

  * [Download the Looker app on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/looker-studio/id1644381985) (for iOS)

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/ios-qr-code.png)

[![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/app-store.png)](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/looker-studio/id1644381985)

  * [Get the Looker app on the Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.cloud.cloudbi) (for Android)

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/play-qr-code.png)

[![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/play-store.png)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.cloud.cloudbi)




## Checking the app version

To find the version number of the Looker app on your mobile device, select **More** in the mobile app's bottom navigation menu. The version number appears after the option to [sign out of the app](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/mobile-app-sign-in#signing_out_of_the_app).

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/mobile-check-version-26-6.png)

For information on updating an app on your device to the latest version, see the [Google Play Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/113412) documentation for Android or the [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT202180) documentation for iOS.
