#  Admin settings - Platform pages Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

These pages appear under the **Platform** section of the Looker **Admin** panel:

  * [Actions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-actions): Configure the ability to deliver data to third-party services that are integrated with Looker.
  * [API](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-api): Change API settings in the Looker **Admin** panel.
  * [Continuous Integration](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-ci): Enable, disable, or configure the [Continuous Integration](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/continuous-integration) feature.
  * [Embed](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-embed): Change settings that are related to embedding in the Looker **Admin** panel.
  * [Extension framework](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-extension-framework): Enable or disable the Looker [extension framework](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/extension-framework) in the Looker **Admin** panel.
  * [Gemini in Looker](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-gil): Enable or disable Gemini in Looker for Looker (original) instances. Verify Gemini in Looker enablement settings for Looker (Google Cloud core) instances.
  * [Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-marketplace): Change settings that are related to the Looker [Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/marketplace) in the Looker **Admin** panel.
  * [Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-mcp): Enable and manage the Looker-managed MCP server, which allows AI agents to securely connect to your Looker instance.
  * [Themes](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/themes-for-embedded-dashboards-and-explores): Customize the appearance of your embedded content from Looker.
  * [SMTP](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-smtp): Configure Looker email settings, including custom email settings.
  * [Visualizations](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-visualizations): View, add, edit, and delete custom visualization types.
  * [Looker Studio Pro](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/admin-panel-platform-lsp): Accept complimentary licenses to use Data Studio Pro.
  * [Studio in Looker](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/enabling-studio-in-looker): Enable or disable Studio in Looker for Looker (original) instances and for Looker (Google Cloud core) instances.
