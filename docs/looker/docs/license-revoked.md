#  Your license has been revoked Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

If, for some reason, your Looker license has been revoked, Looker displays a banner visible to the instance admins that reads as follows:

**Your license has been revoked, and your Looker instance will be shut down soon. If you feel this is in error, kindly contact support.**

This banner is displayed for 14 days after the license has been revoked. In the event the license was revoked in error, this provides your Looker admin time to contact [Looker support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/best-practices/looker-support-details) and [open a support request](http://console.cloud.google.com/support/cases) to have the license reinstated.

If your Looker license is renewed or reactivated after having been revoked, the Looker UI might continue to display the **License has been revoked** banner. To manually refresh the license status and clear the banner, in the **Admin > General > Settings** panel, click the **Update** button next to the **License Key** field.

If no action is taken, after 14 days the Looker instance will be shut down.
