#  Viewing information about your session Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

To view information about your current session, first select the **Profile** menu, and then select **Sessions**.

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/user-account-sessions-2300.png)

The **Sessions** page displays the following information:

  * Your IP address
  * Your browser type
  * Your location
  * A timestamp indicating when you logged in
  * A timestamp indicating when your session will expire
