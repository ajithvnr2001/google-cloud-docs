#  Viewing Looks in the Looker Mobile (Legacy) app Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

**Warning:** The Looker Mobile (Legacy) application will be deprecated on August 31, 2026. Use the [Looker application](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/looker-core-mobile-app) instead.

If access to the Looker Mobile (Legacy) app is enabled for your instance, you can view [Looks](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/saving-and-editing-looks) and [dashboards](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/mobile-app-legacy-viewing-dashboards) on your mobile device in the Looker Mobile (Legacy) app.

While viewing a Look in the app, you can switch between portrait mode and landscape mode, and select the options to add or remove the Look from your favorites, share a link to the Look, copy the link to the Look to your clipboard, and view information about the Look from the Look's three-dot menu.

## Opening a Look

You can navigate to Looks from the [**Recently Viewed** and **Favorites** screens](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/mobile-app-legacy-navigating-to-content#viewing_your_most_recently_viewed_or_favorite_content), while [viewing a board](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/mobile-app-legacy-navigating-to-content#navigating_to_content_from_boards) that you follow, or while [browsing content in folders](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/mobile-app-legacy-navigating-to-content#browsing_content_in_folders) in the Looker Mobile (Legacy) app.

To open a Look in the app, select the [content card](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/mobile-app-legacy-navigating-to-content#content_cards) for the Look that you want to view:

![Content card for the Weekly Returns Look shows a visual preview of the Look, the Look title, and the name of the Look creator.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/mobile-select-look-716.png)

You can also [search for a specific Look](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/mobile-app-legacy-navigating-to-content#searching_your_most_recently_viewed_or_favorite_content) on the **Recently Viewed** and **Favorites** screens.

## Viewing a Look

Looks in the Looker Mobile (Legacy) app include the following elements:

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/mobile-look-overview-716-v2.png)

  1. **Back button** : Returns you to the previous screen
  2. **Look title** : Displays the title of the Look
  3. **Three-dot menu**: Provides the options to add or remove the Look from your favorites, share the Look, copy the link to the Look, or view information about the Look
  4. **Filters** : If an admin has enabled the [Show Filters on Embedded Looks](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/admin-panel-platform-embed#show_filters_on_embedded_looks) option in the **Admin** panel, this section displays any [filters](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/filtering-and-limiting) that have been applied to a Look



You can also tap a data point on the visualization to show a tooltip with more information about that data point.

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/mobile-look-data-point-info-716.png)

## Switching between portrait mode and landscape mode

When you view a Look or [a dashboard](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/mobile-app-legacy-viewing-dashboards) in the Looker Mobile (Legacy) app, you can rotate the screen of your mobile device to switch between portrait mode and landscape mode.

The app displays content in portrait mode by default.

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/mobile-look-portrait-716.png)

In landscape mode, the Look's visualization expands horizontally to fill the width of the screen.

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/looker/docs/images/mobile-look-landscape-716.png)

## Using the three-dot menu for a Look

The Looker Mobile (Legacy) app displays a three-dot menu at the top of a Look.

Select the three-dot menu for a Look to use the following options:

  * **Add to Favorites** or **Remove from Favorites**: Add a Look to your favorites, or remove a Look from your favorites.
  * **Share**: Share a link to a Look from your mobile device.
  * **Copy link**: Copy the link to a Look.
  * **Get info**: View information about a Look, including its title, information about views and favorites, the date the Look was created, and the name of the user who created the Look.



You can also access the three-dot menu for a Look from the [**Recently Viewed** and **Favorites** screens](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/mobile-app-legacy-navigating-to-content#viewing_your_most_recently_viewed_or_favorite_content), a [board](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/mobile-app-legacy-navigating-to-content#navigating_to_content_from_boards), or a [folder](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/mobile-app-legacy-navigating-to-content#browsing_content_in_folders) that contains the Look.

### Adding or removing a Look from your favorites

The [**Favorites** screen](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/mobile-app-legacy-navigating-to-content#viewing_your_most_recently_viewed_or_favorite_content) in the Looker Mobile (Legacy) app lists the Looks that you have added to your favorites.

To add a Look to your favorites while viewing the Look, or from the **Recently Viewed** screen, a board, or a folder:

  1. Select the three-dot menu for the Look.
  2. Choose **Add to Favorites**.



To remove a Look from your favorites while viewing the Look, or from the **Recently Viewed** screen, the **Favorites** screen, a board, or a folder:

  1. Select the three-dot menu for a Look that you have added to your favorites.
  2. Choose **Remove from Favorites**.



### Sharing a Look

You can share Looks with other users using the sharing options available on your mobile device, or with a third-party service that is [integrated with Looker](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/admin-panel-platform-actions#list_of_integrated_services), such as [Slack](https://slack.com/).

### Copying the link to a Look

You can copy the link to a Look to your mobile device's clipboard:

  1. Select the three-dot menu for the Look.
  2. Select **Copy link**.



After you copy a link, the app displays the message **Link copied to clipboard**.

### Viewing information about a Look

Select **Get info** from the three-dot menu for a Look to view the following information:

  * **Title** : Shows the title of the Look, the number of views, and the number of favorites
  * **Description** : Displays the description of the Look, if a [description was added](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/2614/saving-and-editing-looks#editing_a_looks_description)
  * **Created** : Displays the date the Look was created and the name of the user who created it
