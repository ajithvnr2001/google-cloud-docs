The [Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service (section "Discontinuation of Services")](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms) defines the deprecation policy that applies to IAP OAuth Admin API. The [deprecation policy](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms/deprecation) only applies to the services, features, or products listed therein. 

After a service, feature, or product is officially deprecated, it continues to be available for at least the period of time defined in the Terms of Service. After this period of time, the service is scheduled for shutdown. 

The following table lists deprecated features and their related shutdown dates:

Feature | Deprecated date | Turndown date (New project) | Shutdown date | Details  
---|---|---|---|---  
IAP OAuth Admin API | January 22, 2025 | January 19, 2026 | March 19, 2026 | For more information and migration procedures, see [Migrate from the IAP OAuth Admin API](https://docs.cloud.google.com/iap/docs/deprecations/migrate-oauth-client).
