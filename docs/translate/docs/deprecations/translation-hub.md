# Translation Hub deprecation

The [Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service (section "Discontinuation of Services")](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms) defines the deprecation policy that applies to Translation Hub. The [deprecation policy](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms/deprecation) only applies to the services, features, or products listed therein. 

After a service, feature, or product is officially deprecated, it continues to be available for at least the period of time defined in the Terms of Service. After this period of time, the service is scheduled for shutdown. 

Feature | Deprecated date | Shutdown date | Details  
---|---|---|---  
Translation Hub  | June 30, 2025  | September 20, 2026  | Starting on June 30, 2025, Translation Hub is officially deprecated and will no longer be supported. You can continue using Translation Hub until September 20, 2026, when it will be shut down. For continued service, you should migrate to [Cloud Translation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/translate).
