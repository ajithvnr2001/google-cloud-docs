# AutoML Translation deprecation  |  AutoML Translation Documentation  |  Google Cloud Documentation

> Source: https://docs.cloud.google.com/translate/automl/docs/deprecations/

AutoML Translation API is [deprecated](https://cloud.google.com/translate/automl/docs/deprecations) and will no longer be available on Google Cloud after September 30, 2025. The functionality and management of custom models is available through [Cloud Translate API - Advanced (v3)](https://cloud.google.com/translate/docs).

The [Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service (section "Discontinuation of Services")](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms) defines the deprecation policy that applies to AutoML Translation. The [deprecation policy](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms/deprecation) only applies to the services, features, or products listed therein. 

After a service, feature, or product is officially deprecated, it continues to be available for at least the period of time defined in the Terms of Service. After this period of time, the service is scheduled for shutdown. 

Feature | Deprecated date | Shutdown date | Details  
---|---|---|---  
AutoML Translation | September 16, 2024 | September 30, 2025 | Migrate your applications to [Cloud Translation - Advanced](https://docs.cloud.google.com/translate/docs/advanced/custom-translation-quickstart) to replicate the functionality of AutoML Translation.
