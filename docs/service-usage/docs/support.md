# Get support

Service Usage community channels let you connect with other developers and Google engineers to resolve technical issues. You can participate in the Google Developer Program forums or ask questions on Stack Overflow. You can also provide feedback to the Service Usage team.

## Google Developer Program forums

For community-based support, discussions, and to connect with other users and Googlers, your primary destination is [discuss.google.dev](https://discuss.google.dev/c/google-cloud/14). This is the official forum for:

  * **Asking questions:** Get peer-to-peer support for your technical challenges.
  * **Sharing knowledge:** Contribute your expertise and help other members of the community.
  * **Engaging with Googlers:** Discuss with Google Cloud team members and find best practices and tutorials from Googlers.



## Ask questions on Stack Overflow

Google engineers monitor and answer questions on Stack Overflow with the [google-cloud-platform](http://stackoverflow.com/questions/ask?tags=google-cloud-platform) tag. Use this tag when asking questions.

  * [Ask a question](http://stackoverflow.com/questions/ask?tags=google-cloud-platform).
  * [See a list of all questions](http://stackoverflow.com/questions/tagged/google-cloud-platform).



## Send feedback

If you encounter any bugs, confusing commands, or unclear documentation, or if you'd like to propose a feature idea, you can click the **Send feedback** link within the Service Usage documentation, and choose to provide either documentation or product feedback through a form. Your comments will be reviewed by the Service Usage team.
