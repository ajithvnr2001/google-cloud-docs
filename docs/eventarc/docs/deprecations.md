The [Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service (section "Discontinuation of Services")](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms) defines the deprecation policy that applies to Eventarc support for Cloud IoT events. The [deprecation policy](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms/deprecation) only applies to the services, features, or products listed therein. 

After a service, feature, or product is officially deprecated, it continues to be available for at least the period of time defined in the Terms of Service. After this period of time, the service is scheduled for shutdown. 

The following table lists features and functionality that are deprecated, along with their related shutdown schedules:

Feature (status) | Deprecated date | Shutdown date | Details  
---|---|---|---  
Eventarc support for Cloud IoT events | August 16, 2022 | August 16, 2023 | Service shutdown complete
