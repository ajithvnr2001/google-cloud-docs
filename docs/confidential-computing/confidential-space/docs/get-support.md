The following support channels are available for Confidential Space.

## Get a Google support package

Google Cloud offers different support packages to meet different needs, such as 24/7 coverage, phone support, and access to a technical support manager. For more information, see [Cloud Customer Care](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support).

## Join the Learning Group

To connect directly with the development team, you can join the Confidential Computing Learning Group, where you can learn about our products and help us understand how to make them work better for you. The group's activities include:

  * Virtual workshops to learn more about the products

  * Feedback surveys to share your needs and priorities

  * 1:1 sessions with Google Cloud employees as we explore new features




Activities are offered about once a quarter. You can take part in as many or as few as you'd like, or you can opt out entirely at any time.

To join the group, complete the [Confidential Computing Learning Group signup form](https://forms.gle/c4iJrjpKRocFBY8z9).

## Send feedback

To file a bug or a feature request, [create a new Confidential Space issue](https://issuetracker.google.com/issues/new?component=1134314&template=0).

If you have questions or feedback about Confidential Space, contact [confidential-compute@google.com](mailto:confidential-compute@google.com).
