    public enum ProductView.ClickPotential extends Enum<ProductView.ClickPotential> implements ProtocolMessageEnum

A product's [click potential](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/188488) estimates its performance potential compared to highest performing products of the merchant. Click potential of a product helps merchants to prioritize which products to fix and helps them understand how products are performing against their potential.
