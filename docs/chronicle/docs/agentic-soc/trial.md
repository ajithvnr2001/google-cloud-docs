# Participate in the Agentic SOC trial

The Triage and Investigation Agent (TIN) is an agentic AI feature for Google Security Operations that helps automate security investigations. This document describes the terms, usage limits, and duration for the no-cost trial of TIN.

For more information on TIN, see [Use Triage and Investigation Agent to investigate alerts](https://docs.cloud.google.com/chronicle/docs/secops/triage-investigation-agent).

## Eligibility

The Google Agentic SOC trial is available for customers with an active subscription to one of the following Google SecOps packages:

  * Enterprise
  * Enterprise Plus
  * Google Unified Security (GUS)



Tokens are granted on a per-subscription basis and may not be pooled, banked, or transferred between your different eligible subscriptions.

## Trial period

This trial starts **April 1, 2026** and automatically ends for all customers on **August 31, 2026** , unless Google provides written notice of an extension.

## Trial usage limits

Your use of TIN during the trial is measured in _trial runs_. A trial run is equivalent to a single investigation performed by TIN, either automatically or manually.

Your organization's usage is subject to the following limits:

  
**Customer Tier** |   
**Total Hourly Limit** |   
**Limit Breakdown**  
---|---|---  
  
Enterprise |   
10 trial runs |   
Up to 5 automatic and 5 manual runs  
  
Enterprise Plus & GUS |   
10 trial runs |   
Up to 5 automatic and 5 manual runs  
  
If your organization's use reaches or exceeds these limits, you will have to wait for one hour for the limits to refresh again.

## After the trial ends

When the trial period ends on August 31, 2026, the following occurs:

**For Enterprise Plus and Google Unified Security Customers** : Your trial access ends and automatically transitions to the complimentary Security Token allotment included with your base subscription. To purchase capacity beyond your complimentary allotment, you must purchase a paid subscription to the Google SecOps Security Tokens SKU.

**For Enterprise Customers** : Your access to TIN ends. You retain access to historical detections from investigations run during the trial, but you can't run new investigations. To continue using TIN after the trial, you must purchase a paid subscription to the Google SecOps Security Tokens SKU.

**Note:** The trial for TIN might not be available for all customers due to regulatory or compliance restrictions.
