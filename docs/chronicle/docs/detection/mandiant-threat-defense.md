Mandiant Threat Defense is a comprehensive, 24/7/365 managed threat detection, investigation, and response (TDIR) subscription service natively integrated into the Google Security Operations platform. By combining Google's artificial intelligence with Mandiant's frontline threat intelligence and global security operations center (SOC) expertise, the service active-monitors a customer's entire security telemetry stack to proactively hunt for undetected threats and prioritize critical security incidents.

## Key capabilities

Within the Google SecOps ecosystem, Mandiant Threat Defense provides:

  * **Unified security operations:** Mandiant's threat hunters use Google SecOps to proactively search for hidden adversaries within your data. These activities use real-time Mandiant threat intelligence and frontline knowledge of attacker behaviors to uncover sophisticated threats.

  * **Intelligence-driven detection and analysis:** Security events and alerts within Google SecOps are enriched with Mandiant's deep threat intelligence. This context helps you prioritize high-risk threats and lets Mandiant experts rapidly triage and investigate the most critical issues.

  * **Streamlined incident response:** When threats are confirmed, Mandiant Threat Defense provides rapid guidance and support for containment and eradication. Use the integrations between Mandiant workflows and Google SecOps to efficiently orchestrate response actions and neutralize threats.




## View Mandiant Threat Defense reports

**Note:** This option is only available if you have Mandiant Threat Defense enabled as part of your Google SecOps service package.

Mandiant Threat Defense analyzes curated detections raised in the system and produces investigation reports based on these detections. You can view the investigation reports to understand what happened and review any recommendations from the Mandiant Threat Defense team. This lets you take any action based on the reports.

To view an investigation report, do the following:

  1. From the navigation menu, click **Mandiant Threat Defense** to view a list of the investigation reports published on the Mandiant Threat Defense activity timeline.
  2. Select the report you want to review.
  3. Click **Findings** to view a timeline of what happened during the detected activity.
  4. Click **Comments** to leave a comment on the report.
  5. To communicate with the Mandiant Threat Defense team, enter your question, select the **Request response** checkbox and click **Send**. The Mandiant team will review and respond to your inquiry.
