# Rebrand your platform 

Supported in:

Google SecOps  [ SOAR ](https://docs.cloud.google.com/chronicle/docs/secops/google-secops-soar-toc)

You can design the platform to reflect your company's branding by replacing the logo.

## Replace a logo

To replace a logo, follow these steps:

  1. Go to **SOAR Settings > Organization > Rebranding.**
  2. Choose where to replace the logo with yours:
     * **Header** : Your logo replaces the Google SecOps logo. The Google SecOps logo remains on the other side at the top of your page.
     * **SOAR Reports** : Your logo replaces the Google SecOps logo in the report header. The report footer displays **Powered by Google SecOps**.
  3. Browse and attach your logo.
  4. Click **Apply**.
