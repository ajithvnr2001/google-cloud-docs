# Monitor cases from your workdesk

Supported in:

Google SecOps  [ SOAR ](https://docs.cloud.google.com/chronicle/docs/secops/google-secops-soar-toc)

In **Your Workdesk** , the **My Cases** tab contains cases assigned to you, your role, or cases where you're mentioned.

## View case details

To view more details about a specific case, click **View Details** next to the case. This opens the **View Details** side drawer, which displays additional case details, including the highlighted information. If you need further details or want to configure a service-level agreement (SLA), click **Go to case**.

## Access SLAs for alerts

To view SLAs for alerts for a specific case, hold the pointer over **SLA for Alerts**.
