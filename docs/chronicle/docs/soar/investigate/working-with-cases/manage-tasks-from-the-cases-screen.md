# Track case tasks

Supported in:

Google SecOps  [ SOAR ](https://docs.cloud.google.com/chronicle/docs/secops/google-secops-soar-toc)

This document explains how to create and manage case-specific or general tasks, and assign them to a SOC role or team member. You can perform these actions directly within a case (such as from the top toolbar or **Case Wall**) or from **Your Workdesk** > **My Tasks**.

## Add a task

To add a task within a case, follow these steps:

  1. Open the relevant case.
  2. Access the task creation menu: 
     * In the enhanced experience, click **Case Actions** in the top menu (available across the **Overview** , **Case Wall** , **Detections** , or **Playbooks** tabs) and select **Add Task**.
     * In the legacy experience, click ![casetaskicon](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/chronicle/images/soar/casetaskicon.png)**Case Task** on the **Overview** tab.
  3. In the **Add Task** dialog, enter the title, SOC role or team member, task content, and due date.
  4. Click **Save**.



## Assign the SOC role or team member

To change the SOC role or team member assigned to a task, follow these steps: 

  1. Open the tasks list within the case or on the **Case Wall** tab.
  2. Select the task you want to assign.
  3. Click the list next to **Assigned to:**.
  4. Select the required SOC role or team member. The assignment is updated.



## Close a task

To close a task, follow these steps: 

  1. Open the tasks list within the case or on the **Case Wall** tab.
  2. Select the task you want to close.
  3. Click **Mark as done**.
  4. Enter a comment in the **Comment** field and then click **Send**.
