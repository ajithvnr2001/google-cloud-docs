# Configure user preferences

Supported in:

Google SecOps  [ SOAR ](https://docs.cloud.google.com/chronicle/docs/secops/google-secops-soar-toc)

The **User Preferences** dialog is where you manage your user-related configurations, such as localization, notifications, and accessibility. The dialog lets you configure your time zone, date/time formats, notification settings, and accessibility options.

To configure user preferences, do the following:

  1. Click your user avatar.
  2. Select **User preferences** from the menu.
  3. In the **User preferences** dialog, access the following options:
     * **Localization** :
       * **Time Zone** : configure your time zone.
       * **Date/Time Format** : set your preferred date and time format.
     * **Notifications** :
       * Choose how many days of past notifications to show in the **Notifications** panel.
       * Click the **User Notifications** toggle to the ON or OFF position.
       * Select the types of notifications you want to receive.
       * Select how you want to receive notifications (on the platform, by email, or both).
**Note:** To receive notifications by email, ensure you configure the email settings in **Settings > Advanced > Email Settings**. 
     * **Accessibility** :
       * Set the duration for how long feedback messages (such as confirmations or errors) remain visible on the platform before they disappear.
