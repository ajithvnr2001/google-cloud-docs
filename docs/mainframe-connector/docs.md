# Mainframe Connector documentation

[Read product documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/mainframe-connector/docs/overview)

Mainframe Connector lets you move data located on your mainframe in and out of [Cloud Storage](https://docs.cloud.google.com/storage/docs/introduction) and submit [BigQuery](https://docs.cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs/introduction) jobs from mainframe-based batch jobs, defined by job control language (JCL).
