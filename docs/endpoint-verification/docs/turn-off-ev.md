#  Turn off Endpoint Verification Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

This document shows how administrators can turn off Endpoint Verification for their organizational units.

After you turn off Endpoint Verification, if you add any devices to your organization unit, you won't see those devices in the Google Admin console. You will continue to see the devices that were monitored before, however, the device information is no longer updated.

To turn off Endpoint Verification for your organizational unit, do the following:

  1. In the Google Workspace Admin console, go to the **Devices** page.

[Go to Devices](https://admin.google.com/ac/devices/list)

  2. In the navigation menu, click **Mobile & endpoints > Settings > Universal**.
  3. In the **Universal settings** pane, click **Data access**.
  4. In the **Data access** pane, click **Device signals**.
  5. Clear the **Monitor which devices access organization data** checkbox.
  6. Click **Save**.
