#  Getting started for agents Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

As an agent, you access the Contact Center AI Platform (CCAI Platform) portal to use the agent adapter, which is your tool for interacting with end-users.

## Before you begin

Before accessing the CCAI Platform portal, do the following:

  * Update your browser. Google recommends using Chrome.

  * Be sure that your Chrome browser accepts notifications. That includes ensuring that it's not in full-screen mode, which disables browser notifications.

  * Disable third-party ad blockers. These can prevent the agent adapter from functioning properly.




## Access the CCAI Platform portal

If your organization uses single sign-on (SSO) to access the CCAI Platform portal, you don't need to create a password when you activate your account. If your organization does not use SSO, you need to create your own password when you activate your account. Your CCAI Platform administrator can tell you whether your organization uses SSO.

Depending on your situation, do one of the following:

  * Sign in with SSO

  * Create a password and sign in




### Sign in with SSO

If your administrator sent you a CCAI Platform invitation email, and your organization uses SSO, you can sign in without a password.

To sign in using SSO, follow these steps:

  1. Open the invitation email from your administrator and click the link to activate your account. A **Login with company SSO** dialog appears.

  2. Click **Login with company SSO**. The CCAI Platform portal appears, displaying a dialog asking to use your microphone.

  3. Click **Allow**. A dialog appears asking to show notifications.

  4. Click **Allow**. You are now signed into the CCAI Platform portal.

**Note:** At subsequent sign-ins, the system won't ask to use your microphone or show notifications.
  5. Optional: Create a bookmark of the page and use it the next time you want to sign into the CCAI Platform portal.




### Create a password and sign in

If your administrator sent you a CCAI Platform invitation email, and your organization doesn't use SSO, you can create your own CCAI Platform portal password.

To create a CCAI Platform portal password and sign in, follow these steps:

  1. Open the invitation email from your administrator and click the link to activate your account. A welcome page appears.

  2. Set your initial password. A sign-in page appears.

  3. Enter your email name and password. The CCAI Platform portal appears, displaying a dialog asking to use your microphone.

  4. Click **Allow**. A dialog appears asking to show notifications.

  5. Click **Allow**. You are now signed into the CCAI Platform portal.

**Note:** At subsequent sign-ins, the system won't ask to use your microphone or show notifications.
  6. Optional: Create a bookmark of the page and use it the next time you want to sign into the CCAI Platform portal.




## Update your profile information

When you are in an active chat with an end-user, your profile image and first name are displayed to the end-user. If your administrator has enabled it, you can upload a profile photo.

To update your profile information, follow these steps:

  1. In the CCAI Platform portal, click **Agent > My Profile**. If you don't see the **Agent** menu, click menu **Menu** , and then click **My Profile**.

  2. To upload a new profile photo, do the following:

     1. Go to the **Profile Photo** pane.

     2. Click **Choose File** , and then go to the profile photo that you want.

     3. Click **Upload photo**.

  3. To edit your basic profile information, do the following:

     1. Go to the **Basic Information** pane.

     2. In the **Email Address** field, enter your email address.

     3. In the **First Name** field, enter your first name.

     4. In the **Last Name** field, enter your last name.

     5. In the **Alias** field, enter the alias that you want. Your alias is the name that the end-user sees when you communicate with them. Your administrator must give you permission to change this.

     6. In the **Verify Current Password** field, enter your password. This is required to be able to update your profile information.

     7. Click **Update**.




## Change your password

To change your password, follow these steps:

  1. In the CCAI Platform portal, click **Agent > My Profile**. If you don't see the **Agent** menu, click menu **Menu** , and then click **My Profile**.

  2. Go to the **Change Password** pane.

  3. In the **Verify Current Password** field, enter your current password.

  4. In the **New Password** field, enter your new password.

**Note:** Passwords must contain 10 or more characters, an upper and lowercase character, a number, and a special character. Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
  5. In the **Confirm New Password** field, re-enter your new password.

  6. Click **Update**.
