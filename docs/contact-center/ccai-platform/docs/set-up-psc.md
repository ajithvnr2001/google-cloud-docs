**Preview**

This feature is subject to the "Pre-GA Offerings Terms" in the General Service Terms section of the [Service Specific Terms](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms/service-terms#1). Pre-GA features are available "as is" and might have limited support. For more information, see the [launch stage descriptions](https://cloud.google.com/products/#product-launch-stages). 

With Private Service Connect, you can set up private ingress into your Contact Center AI Platform (CCAI Platform) instance, private egress out of your instance, or both. You can do this by setting up a Private Service Connect connection using your own internal IP address, keeping your traffic private and within Google Cloud. For more information, see [Private Service Connect](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vpc/docs/private-service-connect).

To use Private Service Connect to set up private ingress or private egress for your instance, see the following:

  * [Set up private ingress](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/private-ingress)

  * [Set up private egress](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/private-egress)
