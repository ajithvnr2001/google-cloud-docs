#  Advanced capabilities Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

**Note:** Merge queries are not supported by advanced reporting.

The advanced capabilities of the advanced reporting dashboards include the following:

  * Create new custom dashboards based on tiles from other dashboards.

  * Create Looks and link them to dashboards.

  * Use powerful editing capabilities to customize dashboards to suit your business needs.




Access advanced capabilities by starting from the **Advanced Reporting Landing Page**. To get there, click **Dashboard > Advanced Reporting** in the CCAI Platform portal. If you don't see the **Dashboard** menu, expand the window horizontally until the **Dashboard** menu appears.

The **Advanced Reporting Landing Page** appears.

To get started, try [creating a dashboard from an existing metrics tile](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/dashboards-create-dashboard-from-tile).
