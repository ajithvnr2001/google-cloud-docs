#  Block phone numbers Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

You can add phone numbers to a blocklist to prevent inbound calls and SMS chats from those numbers from being handled.

## Add a phone number to the blocklist

To add a phone number to the blocklist, follow these steps:

  1. In the CCAI Platform portal, click **Settings > Consumer Management**. If you don't see the **Settings** menu, click menu **Menu**.

  2. In the **Phone Number Blocklist** pane, click **Add Phone Number**. The **Add Phone Number** dialog appears.

  3. In the **Phone number** field, enter the phone number to be blocked, including the country code.

  4. Click **Save**.




## Remove a phone number from the blocklist

To remove a phone number from the blocklist, follow these steps:

  1. In the CCAI Platform portal, click **Settings > Consumer Management**. If you don't see the **Settings** menu, click menu **Menu**.

  2. In the **Phone Number Blocklist** pane, for the phone number you want to remove, click close **Close**.

  3. In the confirmation dialog, click **OK**.
