#  Link a Look to a dashboard Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

You can [create a Look](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/dashboards-create-look#create-a-look) and then link the Look to one or more existing dashboards. After you link a Look to one or more dashboards, any edits that you make to the Look are reflected in the dashboards. You might need to refresh your browser to see the update.

**Important:** To link a Look to a dashboard, the Look and the dashboard must be in the same folder.

To save a Look to a dashboard without linking it, see [Save a Look to a dashboard](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/dashboards-save-look).

To link a Look to a dashboard, follow these steps:

  1. In the CCAI Platform portal, click **Dashboard > Advanced Reporting**. If you don't see the **Dashboard** menu, expand the window horizontally until the **Dashboard** menu appears.

The **Advanced Reporting Landing Page** appears.

  2. Click folder **Open Folders**. The **Folders** pane appears. For more information, see [Manage folders](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/dashboards-manage-folders).

  3. Navigate to the Look that you want to link to a dashboard, and then click it. The Look appears.

  4. Hold the pointer over the Look. The ![The Explore actions icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/images/dashboards-explore-actions.png) **Explore actions** icon appears.

  5. Click ![The Explore actions icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/images/dashboards-explore-actions.png) **Explore actions** , and then click **Save** > **To an existing dashboard**. The **Add to a Dashboard in this folder** dialog appears.

  6. Click the folder that contains the dashboard that you want to link the Look to.

  7. Click the dashboard that you want to link the Look to.

  8. Click **Add Look to Dashboard**.

  9. Optional: Click your dashboard to view it.
