#  Save a Look to a dashboard Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

You can [create a Look](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/dashboards-create-look#create-a-look) and then save the Look to an existing dashboard. After you save a Look to a dashboard, edits to the Look aren't reflected in the dashboard.

To save a Look to a dashboard, follow these steps:

  1. In the CCAI Platform portal, click **Dashboard > Advanced Reporting**. If you don't see the **Dashboard** menu, expand the window horizontally until the **Dashboard** menu appears. The **Advanced Reporting Landing Page** appears.

  2. Click folder **Open Folders**. The **Folders** pane appears. For more information, see [Manage folders](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/dashboards-manage-folders).

  3. Navigate to the Look that you want to save to a dashboard, and then click it. The Look appears.

  4. Hold the pointer over the Look. The ![The Explore actions icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/images/dashboards-explore-actions.png) **Explore actions** icon appears.

  5. Click ![The Explore actions icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/images/dashboards-explore-actions.png) **Explore actions** , and then click **Save** > **To an existing dashboard**. The **Add to a Dashboard in this folder** dialog appears.

  6. Click the folder that contains the dashboard that you want to save the Look to.

  7. Click the dashboard that you want to save the Look to.

  8. Click **Save to Dashboard**.

  9. Optional: Click your dashboard to view it.
