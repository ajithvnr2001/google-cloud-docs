#  View agent chat statistics Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

This page explains how to view your chat statistics for the current day. You can also view your team-average chat statistics to compare your performance with your team's performance. Your team, for the purposes of the team average, is comprised of the members of your queue.

This page assumes that you are signed into the Contact Center AI Platform (CCAI Platform) portal. For more information, see [Access the CCAI Platform portal](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/getting-started-for-agents#access-the-ccaip-portal).

To view your chat statistics, follow these steps:

  1. In the CCAI Platform portal, click the click chat_bubble **Chat icon** to open the agent chat adapter.

  2. In the chat adapter, click double_arrow **Show chat overview**.

  3. Click more_vert **Options** , and then click **Stats**. The **Stats** page appears, displaying your individual chat statistics and the average statistics for your team.
