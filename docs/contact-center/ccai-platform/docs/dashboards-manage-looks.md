#  Manage Looks Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

A Look is a saved query that can be displayed in a metrics tile in a dashboard. You can organize Looks in folders for quick access by yourself and other team members. After creating a Look, you can [Create a dashboard from a Look](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/dashboards-create-dashboard-from-look).

## Create a Look

The easiest way to create a Look is to copy it from a metrics tile in a dashboard.

To create a Look, follow these steps:

  1. In the CCAI Platform portal, click **Dashboard > Advanced Reporting**. If you don't see the **Dashboard** menu, expand the window horizontally until the **Dashboard** menu appears. The **Advanced Reporting Landing Page** appears.

  2. Click a dashboard that contains a tile that you want to create a Look from. The dashboard appears.

  3. Hold the pointer over the tile that you want to create a Look from. The ![The Explore from here icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/images/dashboards-explore.png)**Explore from here** icon appears.

  4. Click ![The Explore from here icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/images/dashboards-explore.png) **Explore from here**. The **Explore** window appears.

  5. Click ![The Explore actions icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/images/dashboards-explore-actions.png) **Explore actions** , and then click **Save** > **As a Look**. The **Save as a Look** dialog appears.

  6. In the **Title** field, enter a name for your Look.

  7. Optional: In the **Description** field, enter a description for your Look.

  8. For **Folder** , select the folder where you want to save your Look.

  9. Click **Save**.




## Edit a Look

To edit a Look, follow these steps:

  1. In the CCAI Platform portal, click **Dashboard > Advanced Reporting**. If you don't see the **Dashboard** menu, expand the window horizontally until the **Dashboard** menu appears. The **Advanced Reporting Landing Page** appears.

  2. Click folder **Open Folders**. The **Folders** pane appears. For more information, see [Manage folders](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/dashboards-manage-folders).

  3. Click the Look that you want to edit. The Look appears.

  4. Hold the pointer over the Look. The ![The Explore actions icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/images/dashboards-explore-actions.png) **Explore actions** icon appears.

  5. Click ![The Explore actions icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/images/dashboards-explore-actions.png) **Explore actions** , and then click **Edit**. The **Edit Look** page appears.

  6. Edit your Look and then click **Save**. Your edited Look appears.




## Delete a Look

To delete a Look, follow these steps:

  1. In the CCAI Platform portal, click **Dashboard > Advanced Reporting**. If you don't see the **Dashboard** menu, expand the window horizontally until the **Dashboard** menu appears. The **Advanced Reporting Landing Page** appears.

  2. Click folder **Open Folders**. The **Folders** pane appears. For more information, see [Manage folders](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/dashboards-manage-folders).

  3. Click the folder that contains the Look that you want to delete.

  4. Next to the Look that you want to delete, click more_vert **More Actions** , and then click **Move to trash**. The **Move to trash** pane appears.

  5. Click **Move to trash**.




## Mark a Look as a favorite

To mark a Look as a favorite, follow these steps:

  1. In the CCAI Platform portal, click **Dashboard > Advanced Reporting**. If you don't see the **Dashboard** menu, expand the window horizontally until the **Dashboard** menu appears. The **Advanced Reporting Landing Page** appears.

  2. Click folder **Open Folders**. The **Folders** pane appears.

  3. Do one of the following:

     * To mark a Look in a personal folder, click **My Folder**.

     * To mark a Look in a shared folder, click **Group**.

  4. Click the name of the Look that you want to mark as a favorite. The Look appears.

  5. Next to the title of the Look, select the favorite favorite icon. The icon turns blue. The next time you look in the Favorites folder, the Look appears. For more information, see [Folder types](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/dashboards-manage-folders#folder-types).

  6. Optional: To unmark the Look as a favorite, click the icon again to clear it.
