#  Create a dashboard from a Look Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

You can [create a Look](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/dashboards-manage-looks#create-a-look), and then create a dashboard from it.

To create a dashboard from a Look, follow these steps:

  1. In the CCAI Platform portal, click **Dashboard > Advanced Reporting**. If you don't see the **Dashboard** menu, expand the window horizontally until the **Dashboard** menu appears. The **Advanced Reporting Landing Page** appears.

  2. Click folder **Open Folders**. The **Folders** pane appears. For more information, see [Manage folders](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/dashboards-manage-folders).

  3. Navigate to the Look that you want to create a dashboard from, and then click it. The Look appears.

  4. Hold the pointer over the Look. The ![The Explore actions icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/images/dashboards-explore-actions.png) **Explore actions** icon appears.

  5. Click ![The Explore actions icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/contact-center/ccai-platform/docs/images/dashboards-explore-actions.png) **Explore actions** , and then click **Save** > **As a new dashboard**. The **Save as a new dashboard** page appears.

  6. In the **Title** field, enter a name for your dashboard. The system also uses this as the name for the metrics tile in your dashboard.

  7. For **Folder** , select the folder where you want to save your dashboard.

  8. Optional: Click the **Filters** tab, and then select the filters to include in your dashboard.

  9. Click **Save**.

  10. Optional: Click **View dashboard**.
