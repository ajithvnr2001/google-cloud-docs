#  Dialogflow CX documentation Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

[Go to the Dialogflow CX console](https://dialogflow.cloud.google.com/cx/projects)

Dialogflow CX is a new natural language understanding platform built on [generative models](https://docs.cloud.google.com/dialogflow/cx/docs/concept/generative) that can control conversations and on [flows](https://docs.cloud.google.com/dialogflow/cx/docs/basics) that can be used for more explicit conversation control.

Dialogflow CX lets you design and integrate a conversational user interface into your mobile app, web application, device, bot, or interactive voice response system. Dialogflow CX provides new and engaging ways for users to interact with your product.

Dialogflow CX can analyze multiple types of input from your customers, including text or audio inputs, such as phone or voice recordings. It can also respond to your customers in multiple ways, either through text or with synthetic speech.

You receive a [$600 credit](https://cloud.google.com/products/conversational-agents/pricing#cx-trial) for a $0 trial of Dialogflow CX.
