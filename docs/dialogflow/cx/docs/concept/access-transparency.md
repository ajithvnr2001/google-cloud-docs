#  Access Transparency Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

[Access Transparency](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/access-transparency/docs/overview) provides you with logs that capture the actions Google personnel take when accessing your content.

You can [enable Access Transparency](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/access-transparency/docs/enable) for your Google Cloud organization if you have the required permissions for your organization. Once enabled, you can [read the logs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/access-transparency/docs/reading-logs).
