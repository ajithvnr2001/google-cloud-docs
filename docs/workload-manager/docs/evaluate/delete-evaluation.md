### Delete a workload evaluation

If you delete a workload evaluation, it does not have any impact on your workloads. However, to evaluate your workloads again, you need to create another evaluation.

To delete a workload evaluation, follow these steps:

  1. In the Google Cloud console, go to the **Workload Manager** page.

[ Go to Workload Manager](https://console.cloud.google.com/workloads?walkthrough_id=workload-manager--delete-evaluation)   
school The remaining steps appear in the Google Cloud console. 

  2. Click the name of the evaluation that you want to delete.
  3. On the **Evaluation information** page, click **Delete**.
  4. To confirm deletion, in the **Delete evaluation** dialog, enter `delete`, then and click **Confirm**.
