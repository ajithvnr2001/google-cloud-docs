#  Chrome Enterprise Premium deprecations Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

The [Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service (section "Discontinuation of Services")](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms) defines the deprecation policy that applies to Chrome Enterprise Premium. The [deprecation policy](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms/deprecation) only applies to the services, features, or products listed therein. 

After a service, feature, or product is officially deprecated, it continues to be available for at least the period of time defined in the Terms of Service. After this period of time, the service is scheduled for shutdown. 

The following table lists deprecated features and their related shutdown dates:

Feature | Deprecated date | Shutdown date | Details  
---|---|---|---  
Chrome Enterprise Premium App Connector | May 2026 | July 31, 2026 | The Chrome Enterprise Premium App Connector is deprecated. Contact your account team with any questions.  
Chrome Enterprise Premium client connector | March 15, 2023 | December 31, 2023 | The Chrome Enterprise Premium client connector is deprecated. Contact your account team with any questions.
