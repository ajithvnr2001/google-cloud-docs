Features

### Build, scale, govern, and optimize enterprise grade AI agents

[Gemini platform](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/agents/overview) is our open and comprehensive platform that empowers businesses to rapidly build, scale, govern and optimize enterprise-grade agents grounded in your enterprise data. It provides the full-stack foundation and extensive developer choice you need to transform your applications and workflows into powerful agentic systems at global scale.

[Scale agents from Gemini platform to your organizationUse Gemini to securely register, manage, and govern your custom-built agents.Learn about Gemini](https://cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise/agents)

### Agent-powered development and workflows

Now available through Gemini platform, Google Antigravity provides a centralized app to steer, customize, and orchestrate agents. You can deploy multiple agents to simultaneously execute entire workflows like product launches—automating the code generation for your website, on-brand asset creation, and customer email production. [Download](https://antigravity.google/docs/enterprise) Antigravity and log in to the [desktop application](https://antigravity.google/docs/overview) or [Antigravity CLI](https://antigravity.google/docs/cli-overview) using your standard Google Cloud credentials.

### 200+ Google and third-party AI models and tools

Choose from Google’s latest multimodal models like Gemini 4 Argon, third-party models like Anthropic's Claude Model Family, and open models like [Gemma](https://cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/open-models/use-gemma) in [Model Garden](https://console.cloud.google.com/agent-platform/model-garden). You can also customize models to your use case with a variety of [tuning options](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models#model_customization_tuning).

Our [Model Evaluation service](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/evaluation-overview) provides enterprise-grade tools for objective, data-driven assessment of generative AI models.

### Open and integrated AI platform

Data scientists can move faster with Gemini platform tools for training, tuning, and deploying ML models.

[Notebooks](https://cloud.google.com/products/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/notebooks) in Gemini platform, including your choice of Colab Enterprise or Workbench, are natively integrated with [BigQuery](https://cloud.google.com/bigquery) providing a single surface across all data and AI workloads.

[Training](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/training/overview) and [Prediction](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/predictions/overview) in Gemini platform help you reduce training time and deploy models to production easily with your choice of open source frameworks and optimized [AI infrastructure](https://cloud.google.com/ai-infrastructure).

### MLOps for predictive and generative AI

Gemini platform provides purpose-built [MLOps tools](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/start/introduction-mlops) for data scientists and ML engineers to automate, standardize, and manage ML projects.

Modular tools help you collaborate across teams and improve models throughout the entire development lifecycle—identify the best model for a use case with [Model Evaluation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/evaluation/introduction), orchestrate workflows with [Pipelines](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/pipelines/introduction), manage any model with [Model Registry](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/model-registry/introduction) serve, share, and reuse ML features with [Feature Store](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/featurestore/latest/overview), and [monitor](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/featurestore/latest/monitor-features) models for input skew and drift.

How It Works

### 

Gemini platform provides several options for agent building, model training and deployment:

  * [Gemini platform](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform) enables you to build, scale, govern and optimize enterprise ready agents in one unified platform
  * [Agent Studio](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/agent-studio) gives you access to large generative AI models, including Gemini 4 Argon, so you can evaluate, tune, and deploy them for use in your AI-powered applications
  * [Model Garden](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models) lets you discover, test, customize, and deploy in Gemini platform and select open-source (OSS) models and assets
  * [Custom training](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning) gives you complete control over the training process, including using your preferred ML framework, writing your own training code, and choosing hyperparameter tuning options

#### How much does Gemini platform cost?

The cost of the Gemini platform is usage-based and flexible, derived from the specific AI models deployed and infrastructure usage. For models, users pay based on token consumption through several payment tiers—including standard Pay-as-you-go (PayGo), discounted Flex PayGo for latency-tolerant jobs, and premium Priority PayGo for higher reliability—or via Provisioned Throughput subscriptions for predictable, guaranteed throughput. 

Additional infrastructure charges, with the exception of those not available through Gemini platform such as Model Armor or Observability, are metered via three unified dimensions: Agent Compute (vCPU), Agent Memory (RAM), and Agent Storage (GiB) (plus token pass-through). Because expenses vary heavily by model and scale, users should consult [contact sales](https://cloud.google.com/contact) to get granular estimates or explore volume-based enterprise discounts.

#### How much does it cost to use Gemini platform for document retrieval?

Document retrieval on the Gemini platform is handled by [Agent Search](https://cloud.google.com/products/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/agent-search). The billing is primarily usage-based, calculated per 1,000 successful search queries alongside standard storage charges for indexed data (billed per GiB/month). Standard semantic or keyword-based document retrieval is billed at approximately $4.00 per 1,000 queries, covering tasks like scanning the index, returning document snippets, and querying enterprise connectors.

#### Does Google Cloud offer a free trial for the Gemini platform?

Yes, Google Cloud offers a [free trial](https://console.cloud.google.com/freetrial/?redirectPath=%2Fagent-platform%2Foverview&facet_utm_source=%28direct%29&facet_utm_campaign=%28direct%29&facet_utm_medium=%28none%29&facet_url=https%3A%2F%2Fcloud.google.com%2Fproducts%2Fgemini-enterprise-agent-platform) that applies directly to the Gemini platform, allowing new users to explore and build AI agents without financial commitment. Eligible new users receive a standard $300 Google Cloud free trial credit valid for 90 days, which can be applied directly toward Gemini platform usage, including agent runtime compute processing, active memory, and underlying generative model queries. In addition to this promotional welcome credit, the platform provides continuous monthly Free Tier allowances (per billing account, per month) for core infrastructure, covering the first 50 hours (180,000 vCPU-seconds) of compute, the first 100 hours (360,000 GiB-seconds) of RAM, and the first 1 gibibyte of storage for agent runtimes. This setup makes it easy for users to prototype agentic workflows, integrate private data stores, and launch pilot programs at no initial cost.
