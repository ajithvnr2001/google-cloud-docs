#  Configure widget feedback Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

The search widget includes feedback buttons that help assess search quality issues. This page explains how to turn this feature on or off using the Google Cloud console.

When the feedback feature is turned on, the widget rendered on your web page displays **Thumbs up** and **Thumbs down** buttons. Your end users can click the buttons to indicate whether the quality of the results appeared as expected. If a user clicks the **Thumbs down** button, they are prompted to select from a list of reasons why the quality is not as expected and to provide additional comments.

If your app is located in the global region, then the widget feedback is visible to your Google Cloud administrator and Google. If your app is located in a multi-region, then only your Google Cloud administrator sees the feedback. Regardless, your users shouldn't include Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or sensitive data with feedback.

## Turn feedback buttons on or off

To turn feedback buttons, thumb_up and thumb_down, in your widget on or off, follow these steps:

  1. In the Google Cloud console, go to the **AI Applications** page.

[AI Applications](https://console.cloud.google.com/gen-app-builder/start)

  2. Click the name of the search app that you want to edit.

  3. Click **Configurations**.

  4. Click the **UI** tab.

  5. Toggle **Enable feedback** to turn feedback buttons on or off.

  6. Click **Save and publish**.

Your users' feedback can be viewed on the app's Analytics page. See [View analytics](https://docs.cloud.google.com/generative-ai-app-builder/docs/view-analytics).
