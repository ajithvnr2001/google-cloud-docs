The [Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service (section "Discontinuation of Services")](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms) defines the deprecation policy that applies to certificate bundles. The [deprecation policy](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms/deprecation) only applies to the services, features, or products listed therein. 

After a service, feature, or product is officially deprecated, it continues to be available for at least the period of time defined in the Terms of Service. After this period of time, the service is scheduled for shutdown. 

Feature | Deprecated date | Shutdown date | Details  
---|---|---|---  
Certificate bundles | February 21, 2023 | March 20, 2024 | Certificate bundles have been replaced by certificate chains. To learn how to use certificate chains to verify attestations for Cloud HSM keys, see [Verifying the attestation manually](https://docs.cloud.google.com/kms/docs/attest-key#verify_chains).
