#  Community support Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Google Cloud community resources let you get peer-to-peer technical assistance, discuss best practices, and collaborate with other developers. You can engage in official developer forums, ask code-level questions on Stack Overflow, and submit product feedback or bug reports through issue trackers.

## Google Developer Program forums

For community-based support, discussions, and to connect with other users and Googlers, your primary destination is [discuss.google.dev](https://discuss.google.dev/c/google-cloud/14). This is the official forum for:

  * **Asking questions:** Get peer-to-peer support for your technical challenges.
  * **Sharing knowledge:** Contribute your expertise and help other members of the community.
  * **Engaging with Googlers:** Discuss with Google Cloud team members and find best practices and tutorials from Googlers.



## Technical questions on Stack Overflow

For code-level or technical configuration questions, the [Google Cloud Collective on Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com/collectives/google-cloud?tab=questions) is a good resource.

## Report bugs and request features

To send feedback or report issues directly from the [Google Cloud console](https://console.cloud.google.com/), click help > **Send feedback**.

For information about reporting bugs, making feature requests, and about how Google prioritizes requests, see [Report bugs and request features](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/issue-trackers).
