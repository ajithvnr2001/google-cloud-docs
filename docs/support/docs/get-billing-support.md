#  Get Cloud Billing support Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

All Google Cloud accounts get free billing and payments support. This page tells you how to contact Cloud Billing Support if you need help with your Cloud Billing account, and shows you where to get more information about managing your billing account.

Cloud Billing Support is available in several regions and languages. For information about support availability, see [Google Cloud Support plans](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support).

You can get interactive help in the Google Cloud console:

  1. In the Google Cloud console, go to the **Billing support** page.

[Go to Billing support](https://console.cloud.google.com/support/chat)

  2. Click **Get billing support**.

The **Billing Assistant** pane opens and you can get interactive help.




## Contact Cloud Billing Support

To contact support for a Cloud Billing issue, you must be a _Billing Account Administrator_ on the Cloud Billing account. If you are not a billing administrator, we provide an alternative contact path for some Cloud Billing issues.

Before you contact Cloud Billing support, consider these other sources of information that might help you resolve Cloud Billing issues.

If you are a billing administrator | If you aren't a billing administrator  
---|---  
If you are a billing administrator on at least one Cloud Billing account, you can contact Cloud Billing Support by using the following method: 

  1. In the Google Cloud console, go to the **Help and support** page. [Go to Help and support](https://console.cloud.google.com/support/createcase)
  2. In the _Select a product_ field, select **Billing** and click **Select**. 
  3. At the prompt, click **Get billing support** to access the interactive **Billing Assistant**. 

When using the _Billing Assistant_ , if you get an error message when you attempt to contact Cloud Billing Support, consider the following options: 

  * Sign in to a billing administrator account or ask a billing administrator on the Cloud Billing account to contact support on your behalf.
  * If you use Google Cloud through a reseller or other Google Partner, you must contact them for billing support.

|  If you are _not_ a billing administrator on at least one Cloud Billing account, and you don't know how to contact your billing administrator, you can open the **Support Contact Troubleshooter** to find an alternative contact option, depending on your issue:  [Open the Support Contact Troubleshooter](https://support.google.com/cloud/troubleshooter/9664343) The troubleshooter can help _non-billing-administrators_ contact support for issues like the following: 

  * Your Cloud Billing account is suspended.
  * Issues with your form of payment, such as an error message when attempting to add or update a payment method.
  * You are unable to edit, update, or delete your Google Cloud project or resources.
  * You have lost access to your Google Cloud project or Cloud Billing account and you need to regain access.
  * You see unknown charges from Google Cloud on your form of payment and you are unable to stop these charges.
  * You need billing support for a product, such as Gemini Enterprise - Business edition, after the associated account or team has been deleted.

### Google Cloud console

In the Google Cloud console, you can monitor your costs, access billing reports, make changes to your Cloud Billing account settings, set up budgets and alerts, view your payment history, view and download documents such as statements, invoices, and receipts; and export billing data for custom analysis.

  1. In the Google Cloud console, go to your Cloud Billing account.

[Go to your Cloud Billing account](https://console.cloud.google.com/billing/overview)

  2. In the **Billing account** list, select a billing account to go to its overview page.

The **Billing account overview** page opens.

  3. You can use the navigation pane to open a specific page—for example, **Reports** , **Billing export** , **Payment settings** , or **Account management**. The available Google Cloud console pages vary depending on the [type of billing account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/concepts#billing_account_types) you are viewing and [your level of permissions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/billing-access) on the Cloud Billing account.

To view a different Cloud Billing account, at the top of the navigation pane, in the **Billing account** list, select the account you want to view. If you don't see the account listed, click **Manage billing accounts** to view a list of Cloud Billing accounts that you can view.




## Get billing support for other Google products

If you are having trouble with the billing account associated with your Google Maps Platform projects or with your Google Workspace account, other troubleshooting tools and contact options are available.

### Google Maps Platform

Google Maps Platform customers need a valid Cloud Billing account to use the Google Maps Platform APIs.

**Note:** For Google Maps Platform customers, support is provided in English only.

To get billing support for your Google Maps Platform account:

  1. In the Google Cloud console, go to the **Google Maps Platform** > **Support** page.

[Go to Google Maps Platform Support](https://console.cloud.google.com/google/maps-apis/support)

  2. At the top of the page, use the resource selector ( My Project arrow_drop_down) to select the **project** related to your Google Maps Platform billing question.

     * To view the support form, the project you select must contain at least one enabled Google Maps Platform API.
     * To file a Google Maps Platform billing support case, you need to be a _Project Owner_ or _Project Editor_ on the selected project.
  3. Click add_box **Create case**.

  4. In the **Create a Case** form, in the **Issue type** list, select **Billing issue**.

**Note:** If you don't see the support form, it might be because the project that you have selected does not have any Google Maps Platform APIs enabled.
  5. Fill out the rest of the form fields in detail, and then click **Submit**.

After you submit the form, a new support case is created. It might take a few minutes to create your case.




### Google Workspace

  * To troubleshoot issues with a **suspended Google Workspace account** or service, including making sure you have a valid form of payment set up, see [Billing & payment issues](https://support.google.com/a/topic/2413217) in the Google Workspace Admin Help.

  * If you have **lost access to the billing administrator account** associated with a Google Workspace account, you need to contact your [administrator](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6208960) for assistance or use the [Google Workspace Admin Help](https://support.google.com/a/contact/recovery_form).

  * If you are a **reseller for Google Workspace services** , see [Support for Google Workspace resellers](https://support.google.com/channelservices/answer/6182474) for information about the Google Cloud Support Portal.
