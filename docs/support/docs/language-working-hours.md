#  Language support and working hours Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Cloud Customer Care provides technical support in multiple languages and regional hours when you [submit a support case](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/customer-care-procedures#create_a_support_case) in the Google Cloud console. Your support tier and case priority determine whether support is available 24/7 or during local business hours.

[Community support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/community-support), including Google issue trackers, is available only in English. For language support in Google Workspace, see [Google Workspace & Cloud Identity language support and hours of operation](https://support.google.com/a/table/3247295).

## Language support

Customer Care is available in the following languages:

  * English
  * Japanese
  * Mandarin Chinese
  * Korean
  * French



The language in which you write your case determines the language in which you receive support.

### Customer Care availability

For incoming support cases, Customer Care is available based on the following factors:

  * The language in which you submit the case.
  * The support service of your Google Cloud organization, such as [Enhanced Support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/enhanced) or [Premium Support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/premium). Your service determines whether case support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week (24/7). Otherwise, Customer Care is available during Hours of Operation or Business Days.
  * The [priority](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/customer-care-procedures#support_case_priority) of the case (P1-P4).



The following table outlines Customer Care availability based on language and service. For more details on response times, see the [technical support guidelines](https://cloud.google.com/terms/tssg/#supportlevels).

## Working hours

Customer Care's working hours, defined as Hours of Operation or Business Days, depend on the support language of your case.

**Note:** Customer Care is unavailable during regional holidays, unless your case qualifies for 24/7 support.

### Hours of Operation

English and French support is available during Hours of Operation, which run from 5:00 PM on Sunday to 5:00 PM on Friday, Pacific Time Zone.

For more information, go to [Google Cloud Platform Services: Technical Support Services Guidelines](https://cloud.google.com/terms/tssg), expand the **General Support Services Terms** section, and then see "Additional Definitions: Hours of Operation."

### Business Days

A Business Day means any day during the Hours of Operation. The following support languages operate under regional Business Days:

Support Language  
| Business Days  
  
---|---  
Japanese | 9:00am-5:00 PM, Monday-Friday, Japan Standard Time (GMT+9)  
Mandarin Chinese | 9:00am-5:00 PM, Monday-Friday, China Standard Time (GMT+8)  
Korean | 9:00am-5:00 PM, Monday-Friday, Korea Standard Time (GMT+9)
