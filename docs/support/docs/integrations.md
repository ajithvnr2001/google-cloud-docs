Cloud Customer Care provides technical support for acquired products that are integrated into Google Cloud. You can manage support cases and choose a support offering for the integrated products described in this document. To select an offering for your organization, see [Set up support for integrations](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/integrations#set_up_support_for_integrations).

## Looker

This section describes how support is provided to Looker customers.

Google Cloud acquired Looker in 2020. As a result of this integration, Looker is now part of the wider range of Google Cloud products and solutions. Until now, Looker customers have been receiving support through legacy systems for technical issues related to Looker.

**Attention:** Starting October 2, 2024, Looker's legacy support will no longer be available. The technical support cases for your Looker product will be submitted through the Google Cloud console. This means that you will need to onboard to a Cloud Customer Care support package before October 2, 2024 to ensure that you have continued support. 

Now that Looker is integrated with Google Cloud, technical support is provided through the [Google Cloud console](http://console.cloud.google.com/support/cases) and Google Cloud in-app chat using [Cloud Customer Care offers](https://cloud.google.com/support#support-plans).

For more information on how these changes affect you, see FAQs and Additional Looker-Specific FAQs.

## Google SecOps SIEM

This section describes how support is provided to Google SecOps SIEM customers.

Google Cloud acquired Google SecOps SIEM (formerly Chronicle) in 2019. As a result of this integration, Google SecOps SIEM is now part of the wider range of Google Cloud products and solutions. Until now, Google SecOps SIEM customers have been receiving support through legacy systems for technical issues related to Google SecOps SIEM.

As of February 14, 2023, upon the renewal of your support service, technical support is provided through the Google Cloud console, and your access to the Google SecOps SIEM legacy portal is set to view only.

As of August 1, 2024, all technical support will be provided through the Google Cloud console.

  * If your contract renews before August 1, 2024: You will migrate to Cloud Customer Care and the Google Cloud console at the time of your renewal. Once your renewal closes, you will receive an email from our Onboarding team (`saas-support-onboarding@google.com`) to schedule an appointment to get you set up on the Google Cloud console.
  * If your contract renews after August 1, 2024: Starting April 2024, look for an email from our Onboarding team (`saas-support-onboarding@google.com`) to schedule an appointment to get you set up on the Google Cloud console before August 1, 2024. Look out for communications from our team about access to the Google Cloud console.
  * After you have onboarded to Cloud Customer Care and the Google Cloud console, access to the Google SecOps SIEM legacy portal will be set to view only. Any open and active cases in the legacy support portal will continue to be supported until resolved and all new cases will be submitted through the Google Cloud console.



If you have an existing Customer Care service, you can file your Google SecOps SIEM support tickets through the Google Cloud console. You need to add a Google SecOps SIEM project to file support cases against and grant the required permissions to the users. See [manage support cases](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/customer-care-procedures#manage_a_support_case).

Now that Google SecOps SIEM is integrated with Google Cloud, support is provided through the [Google Cloud console](http://console.cloud.google.com/support/cases) using [Cloud Customer Care offers](https://cloud.google.com/support#support-plans).

For more information on how these changes affect you, see FAQs and Additional Google SecOps SIEM-Specific FAQs.

The following sections describe support processes and show you how to onboard to Cloud Customer Care.

### Choose a Customer Care service

Each service in the [Customer Care portfolio](https://cloud.google.com/support#support-plans) is designed to meet you where you are in your cloud journey.

In addition to our three core support services (that is, Standard Support, Enhanced Support, and Premium Support), we offer Comprehensive support to Google Cloud SaaS products including Looker (Google Cloud core), Looker (Original), Google SecOps SIEM, Google SecOps SOAR, Apigee Hybrid, Apigee Edge Cloud, Apigee X, Mandiant, Application Integration, and Integration Connectors.

Comprehensive Support offers unlimited 1:1 technical support for outages and defects, unexpected product behavior, product usage questions, billing issues, and feature requests.

**Important:** While Comprehensive Support comes with no additional costs, it is not automatically provisioned. You must onboard to the Google Cloud console and have an active Google Cloud organization ID to be eligible. Once this is set up, work with your Google representative to have Comprehensive Support provisioned to your account. If you purchase support for other Google Cloud products under the same organization or have an existing paid support entitlement, the purchased entitlement supersedes the Comprehensive Support entitlement, and applies to your SaaS products as well.

### Onboarding to Customer Care

Our dedicated support onboarding team is happy to assist you in getting Comprehensive Support, Standard Support, or Enhanced Support. For Premium Support, contact [sales](https://cloud.google.com/contact). If you're new to Google Cloud, and need assistance setting up support, our team is available to walk you through our [onboarding guide](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/external_playbook_v5_onboarding_to_customer_care.pdf). To reach out to our onboarding team, you can email us at `saas-support-onboarding@google.com`.

#### Get started with Google Cloud Customer Care services and onboarding

Once you've chosen a support service that fits your needs, you're ready to proceed with your onboarding.

If you already have an active Customer Care service (Basic, Standard, Enhanced, or Premium), you are able to use the same support package for your new products.

To purchase Premium support service, contact [Google Cloud Sales](https://cloud.google.com/contact).

#### Information required for onboarding to Customer Care

You need to have the following information ready for your onboarding meeting:

  * Name of your business domain.
  * Access to your Business Domain DNS settings.
  * Identify your Organization Admin user and their email address. The Organization Admin sets up the projects within the organization, purchases support service and files support tickets.



You're now ready for your onboarding meeting. A dedicated member of our onboarding team will walk you through the process of setting up your Google Cloud Support.

At the end of the process you will generate an Organization ID which would allow the Onboarding team to entitle your organization for Customer Care. The entitlement process can take up to 1 hour to complete. Once completed, you're ready to submit support cases through the Google Cloud console.

## Manage support cases through the Google Cloud console

### Grant access to support service

Before you can view and manage support cases, you need the required permissions. Your administrator can give you the required permissions through Identity and Access Management (IAM).

See [How to grant IAM roles](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/access-control#granting_roles).

### Create a project in the Google Cloud console

Before you are able to submit support tickets, you need to create a project through the Google Cloud console for your Google Cloud product.

See [Creating and managing projects](https://docs.cloud.google.com/resource-manager/docs/creating-managing-projects).

### Submit a support case

If you have a technical issue with a Google Cloud product, raise a case with Customer Care.

See [How to create a support case](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/customer-care-procedures#create_a_support_case).

### Submit an Assured and FedRAMP support case

Assured support can be purchased by Premium or Enhanced Support customers. For more information, see [Google Cloud Customer Care portfolio](https://cloud.google.com/support#support-plans).

To file an Assured and FedRAMP support case, see [Getting support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/docs/getting-support).

**Important:** You must have an [Assured Workloads folder and project set up](https://docs.cloud.google.com/resource-manager/docs/creating-managing-projects) to file these cases.

### Manage support cases

You can manage cases from the **Cases** page. Any user in your organization with permission to edit support cases can comment on the case, upload file attachments, or modify case attributes, even if they did not create the case.

See [How to manage existing support cases](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/customer-care-procedures#manage_existing_cases).

### Escalate a case

**This does not apply to Comprehensive Support and Standard Support.**

If an issue is causing a pressing negative impact to your business and you require help beyond the regular case route, use the **Escalate** option. Escalating your case pages a case Escalation Manager who provides additional resources to fast-track the resolution process and minimize any impact on your business.

See [How to escalate a case](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/customer-care-procedures#escalate_case).

## FAQs

### Why is my support changing?

Looker, Google SecOps SIEM, Google SecOps SOAR, Mandiant, Google Threat Intelligence, and Apigee are now part of the wider range of Google Cloud products and solutions. Until now, customers have been receiving support through legacy systems for their technical issues.

Now that Looker, Google SecOps SIEM, Google SecOps SOAR, Mandiant, Google Threat Intelligence, and Apigee have been integrated with Google Cloud, support is provided through the [Google Cloud console](http://console.cloud.google.com/support/cases) using [Customer Care services](https://cloud.google.com/support#support-plans), which means all of your queries are answered by Google Cloud technical experts.

Accessing support for all Google Cloud products under a single platform delivers powerful benefits. You'll enjoy the support you know and love, enhanced by all the capabilities of the Google Cloud support portfolio.

### What does this mean for me?

Customer Care will now serve as your one-stop shop for all queries pertaining to Looker, Google SecOps SIEM, Google SecOps SOAR, Mandiant, Google Threat Intelligence, Apigee, and any other Google Cloud products as you scale to incorporate new solutions. With an accessible Google Cloud console, you can raise and manage technical issues for these products consistently in a single, centralized platform without the inconvenience of switching between systems.

### How will I know which Customer Care service is right for my business?

The Google Cloud Customer Care portfolio offers various levels of support, including Comprehensive, Standard, Enhanced, and Premium Support. To determine which Customer Care service best suits your organization size and workload, you can review the capabilities and response times offered in each core service.

[Compare Customer Care services](https://cloud.google.com/support#support-plans)

### What additional features are included in Google Cloud Customer Care services?

The Customer Care portfolio offers various levels of support, including Comprehensive, Standard, Enhanced, and Premium support. [Compare Customer Care services](https://cloud.google.com/support#support-plans) to find the one that best suits your business needs.

The portfolio also offers an array of custom Value-Add Services to supercharge support for your business. These services include the Technical Account Advisor Service, Planned Event Support, Assured Support, and Mission Critical Services.

### How do I sign up for a Customer Care service?

For customers moving from a legacy support system to the Google Cloud console, a dedicated customer care Onboarding Agent will help guide you through the process. If you have not heard from your Onboarding Agent, connect with your Account Representative or review the [Onboarding to Customer Care guide](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/external_playbook_v5_onboarding_to_customer_care.pdf) for more information.

### How do I submit support cases in the new system?

First, you'll need to be granted Identity and Access Management (IAM) permissions by your Organizational Administrator. Once you have IAM permissions, you can reach out to support for help. If you want to raise a case, see [How to create a support case](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/customer-care-procedures#create_a_support_case).

### Where can I find directions for specific support procedures?

See [Customer Care procedures & support activities](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/customer-care-procedures) for instructions on Customer Care support procedures.

### Can I escalate cases?

**Applicable to Enhanced Support and Premium Support services only.**

Yes - If your case requires more attention or the impact to your business has increased, you can choose to escalate. From the Google Cloud console, you can [escalate a case](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/customer-care-procedures#escalate_case).

### How do I get in touch with Google Cloud support?

Google Customer Care offers multi-channel access. This means you have multiple ways to access the support and technical guidance you need through your preferred channel. For most customers, the Google Cloud console offers the best option. Seeking technical assistance through the Google Cloud console is quick and simple. See [Contact customer care](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/customer-care-procedures#contact_customer_care).

## Additional Looker-specific FAQs

### What is changing

As part of Looker's integration with Google Cloud, starting October 2, 2023, upon your scheduled renewal, technical support for Looker will transition to be a part of the [Google Cloud Customer Care portfolio](https://cloud.google.com/support), along with other Google Cloud services.

After the renewal, you can use the [Google Cloud console](http://console.cloud.google.com) to submit your new technical support cases. Any pre-existing open cases in the [Looker legacy customer support portal](https://help.looker.com/hc/) will continue to remain active until resolved.

On October 2, 2024, we will replace the Looker legacy support portal with the Google Cloud console. After this time, the Looker legacy support portal will be unavailable and all technical support will be provided through the Google Cloud console.

If your renewal is impacted by this change, our Google Cloud Support Onboarding team will reach out to you with instructions on how to get you set up on Cloud Customer Care before the October 2024.

### Why is it changing?

As part of Looker's integration with Google Cloud, moving to the [Google Cloud Customer Care portfolio](https://cloud.google.com/support) helps ensure that we deliver a world class support experience across all our Google Cloud services. Customers with multiple Google Cloud services, including Looker, will appreciate the consistency and ease of access to support across their portfolio.

### What is the process to switch to Google Cloud Customer Care?

No action is required on your part until you begin planning for your renewal. At the time of renewal, your Google representative will share the details of the process to onboard to Google Cloud Customer Care.

At that time, you may choose one of the listed [Google Cloud Customer Care](https://cloud.google.com/support) support services, and our team will assist you with getting your support set up. Once onboarded to Customer Care, you can create technical support cases for Looker through the [Google Cloud console](http://Console.cloud.google.com).

### What changes will I see in my support experience?

The [Google Cloud console](http://Console.cloud.google.com) offers a different experience compared to the [Looker legacy customer support portal](https://help.looker.com/hc/). In addition, features of each Customer Care service may vary from our Looker legacy support offers. For more information about the offers, see [Looker Technical Support Services Guidelines](https://cloud.google.com/terms/apigee-support) and [Google Cloud Platform Services: Technical Support Services Guidelines](https://cloud.google.com/terms/tssg).

### How do I upgrade to a higher tier of Support?

During the renewal process, you can select a support service from the [Google Cloud Customer Care portfolio](https://cloud.google.com/support), which includes various tiers of support designed to meet your business needs.

### Can I still use the Looker chat function to file a case?

Yes. However, customers with Comprehensive support don't have access to the chat feature. Refer to the [Customer Care portfolio](https://cloud.google.com/support#support-plans) to view the options.

### Why is the price changing?

The Customer Care portfolio offers you the ability to choose a support service based on your organization's needs and budget. This portfolio includes different levels of support with various features that deliver added value to help your business succeed. Whether you're looking for system optimization, self-management capabilities, or Value-Add Services, you can find a service that suits your needs. This not only means that you gain a richer support experience, but also gives you more control over choosing what's right for your business. All this additional value is reflected in the price.

As Looker Support integrates into Google Cloud, you will transition to one of the Customer Care support services. Each service has its own SLO which means that your SLO might be different from what you're used to.

You can further upgrade your support service anytime to get an SLO that suits your organization's needs. For information on SLOs, see [Looker SLOs](https://cloud.google.com/terms/looker/support) and [Google Cloud SLOs](https://cloud.google.com/terms/tssg).

### What languages are supported?

For information on languages available for your service and products, see [Language support and working hours](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/language-working-hours).

### Where can I find my Technical Support Service Guidelines?

For information about your Looker Technical Support Services, see [Google Technical Support Services Guidelines](https://cloud.google.com/terms/tssg/looker).

### Can I just stay on legacy Looker Support?

At your upcoming renewal, it is required that you switch to a [Google Cloud Customer Care](https://cloud.google.com/support) service. This will help ensure we can deliver a world class support experience across all our Google Cloud services.

### Who can I contact if I have any questions?
