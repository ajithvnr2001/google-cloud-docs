#  Event readiness services Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

**Tip:** For detailed guidance on preparing for peak capacity events and high traffic, see the [Prepare for a peak capacity event](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/checklists/plan-peak-capacity-event) checklist.

Depending on the Cloud Customer Care support package that you have purchased, specific event readiness services are available to you:

  * **Enhanced Support** : you can optimize readiness for your events, launches, and migrations by using [Planned Event Support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/enhanced#pes) which is a Value-Add Service that provides coverage for critical phases of high-traffic short-term digital events like grand openings, new product launches, or data migrations.

**Note:** Requests for Planned Event Support service requires a lead time of 60 days.
  * **Premium Support** : you can use the [Event Management Service](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/premium#event_management) for planned peak events, such as a product launch or major sales event. With this service, Customer Care and your account team will guide you through the following:

    * Preparing your systems for key moments and heavy workloads
    * Running load tests and disaster tests to proactively resolve potential issues
    * Engaging with Customer Care in the event of any issues that might occur
    * Reviewing the outcomes of your event and making recommendations for future events.
**Note:** Requests for [Event Management Service](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/peak-events/create-manage-event-requests) require a lead time of 30 days.
