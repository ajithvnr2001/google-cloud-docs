You can downgrade your Cloud Customer Care offering from Premium, Enhanced, or Standard Support when your workload requirements or budget change. Depending on your current tier, you can switch to a lower paid tier or to Basic Support. To cancel or close your support service, see [Close your Customer Care account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/close-account).

## Before you begin

  * You must have the [Organization Administrator](https://docs.cloud.google.com/resource-manager/docs/access-control-org#using_predefined_roles) (`roles/resourcemanager.organizationAdmin`) and [Support Account Administrator](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/access-control#cloudsupport.admin) (`roles/cloudsupport.admin`) roles.

If you don't have these roles, contact an Organization Administrator for access.

  * If you are downgrading to a paid offering, you must also have a Cloud Billing account for the new offering.




## Verify your current offering

  1. In the Google Cloud console, go to the **Support** page.   
[Go to the Support page](https://console.cloud.google.com/support)

  2. Click **Overview** to view your dashboard.

  3. View the **Support Information** card to see your current offering.




## Downgrade your offering

If you're a current Standard or Enhanced Support customer, downgrade your offering in the Google Cloud console.

To downgrade your offering, complete the following steps based on the offering you want to use:

### Basic

To downgrade to Basic support:

  1. Select **View Customer Care services**.
  2. Under **Basic Support** , click **Select Basic**.
  3. View the selected Google Cloud organization resource, and verify that you have the required permissions to proceed.
  4. Read and agree to the terms of service and transitioning terms. You can continue to use your existing support offering until the end of the billing cycle.
  5. Click **Save Changes**.



Users with Basic support cannot create technical support cases but can continue to use free Cloud Billing support and self-help resources. To learn more about the features included in Basic Support, visit [Getting support with Cloud Customer Care](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs#basic_support).

### Standard

To downgrade to Standard Support:

  1. Select **View Customer Care services**.
  2. Under **Standard Support** , click **Buy Now**.
  3. View the selected Google Cloud organization resource, and verify that you have the required permissions to proceed.
  4. Select the billing account from the drop down menu. Read and agree to the terms of service.
  5. Click **Save Changes**.



The offering and billing details is updated, and you can start using Standard Support immediately.

To learn about the features included, see the [Standard Support overview](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/standard).

### Enhanced

To downgrade to Enhanced Support from Premium Support, contact your Technical Account Manager (TAM).
