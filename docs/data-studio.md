# Data Studio documentation

[Read product documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/welcome)

Data Studio is a no-cost tool that turns your data into informative, easy to read, easy to share, and fully customizable dashboards and reports. Use the drag and drop report editor to tell your data story with charts, make your reports interactive with viewer filters, and share your insights with your team or with the world.
