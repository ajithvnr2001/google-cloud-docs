#  App Design Center locations Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

In App Design Center, you can store the templates and applications that you create in the following locations.

For steps to select and manage a region in App Design Center, see [Select a region](https://docs.cloud.google.com/application-design-center/docs/manage-spaces#select-region).

For more information about regions and zones, see [Geography and regions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/geography-and-regions).

## Global

You can store your templates and applications in the global region. For example, you might want to make some templates available to all development teams, regardless of their location. You might also use the global region for applications that span multiple Google Cloud regions.
