Food Ordering AI Agent (PAYG Enterprise Edition SKU) provides a next-generation platform powered by Gemini to rapidly build, evaluate, and deploy highly personalized conversational food ordering experiences across digital channels. Food Ordering AI Agent is part of Gemini Enterprise for CX, and provides:

  * **Structured menu integration:** A comprehensive platform that ingests, transforms, and manages restaurant menu data (items, modifiers, and modifier groups) to align with conversational ordering flows.
  * **Multimodal ordering engine:** Seamless, bidirectional processing of real-time text and audio inputs for fluid ordering experiences.
  * **Enterprise-grade scaling:** Flexible configurations mapping distinct restaurant identity parameters across multiple brands, stores, operating hours, and dayparts.
  * **Frictionless resolution:** Intelligent automated order validation, cart management, and payment handoffs with zero wait times.



## Pricing details

Food Ordering AI Agent charges for sessions. A session is a single food ordering conversation managed by Food Ordering AI Agent, and can be a chat, voice, or mixed session.

Agent type | Pricing  
---|---  
Chat | $0.25 / session  
Voice | $0.25 / session  
  
## Additional details

  * Food Ordering AI Agent capabilities for automated evaluations, menu ingestions, cart management, and monitoring are included in the per session prices at no additional charge.
  * If a session starts with chat and then switches to voice, the whole duration will be billed as one voice session.
