Overview

### Leading AI capabilities

**Gemini is now available on GDC.** Gemini’s advanced reasoning and state-of-the-art generation capabilities on GDC unlock key generative AI capabilities including automation, content generation, discovery, and summarization on-premises.

[![What’s new with Google Distributed Cloud video thumbnail](https://www.gstatic.com/bricks/image/658f6d49-adb2-4ab9-87ba-586a336adb73.png)Unlock the best of Google AI on your premises34:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wlvllf4RidE)

### Enable inference at the edge

On-premises computing is not new, but extending AI-optimized cloud infrastructure from the cloud to on-premises deployments is. With Google Distributed Cloud, you can extend the latest in AI models and AI-optimized infrastructure without compromising on data residency, latency, or connectivity.

[![Modern Edge AI experiences](https://www.gstatic.com/bricks/image/232c69a9-8697-4690-8f66-9789539440fe.jpeg)12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LoA_nMEtX3M&list=PLBgogxgQVM9uZ7nh2WNikI5vd9UdeZKsp)

### Run modern apps on-premises

Build modern apps everywhere in a uniform developer environment from cloud to edge. Google Distributed Cloud enables your team to build, deploy, and scale with a Kubernetes-based developer workflow and leverage an active ecosystem of partners.

[![Building a cloud-native inventory detection app with AI and Kubernetes on Google Distributed Cloud](https://www.gstatic.com/bricks/image/af63c98f-3701-4543-968d-9207da15cd79.jpeg)12:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QkR0unObE3Q)

### Address digital sovereignty needs

[Google Distributed Cloud air-gapped](https://cloud.google.com/distributed-cloud-air-gapped) is a fully managed experience for organizations requiring complete isolation to [meet stringent sovereignty](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/identity-security/google-advances-sovereignty-choice-and-security-in-the-cloud) and regulatory requirements. It does not require connectivity to an external network. Our air-gapped option is one of our [Sovereign Cloud solutions](https://cloud.google.com/sovereign-cloud). 

[![Google Distributed Cloud air-gapped video thumbnail](https://www.gstatic.com/bricks/image/22239b55-4461-4c00-a661-31e133273a52.png)1:25](https://youtu.be/8ppQ6O3NVw8)

### Scale anywhere with cloud-native agility

Scale from one to thousands of locations with flexible hardware and software options for your business. Google Distributed Cloud equips your team with fully managed software to quickly adapt and respond to the latest customer requirement and market changes.

#### What is a Google Cloud Ready partner and how does my company become one?

The Google Cloud Ready - Distributed Cloud validation recognizes partner solutions that have met a core set of functional and interoperability requirements. Through the process, partners closely collaborate with Google to provide new integrations to best support customer use cases. Learn more in the [Google Cloud Ready - Distributed Cloud Partner Validation Guide](https://cloud.google.com/distributed-cloud/docs/cloudready).

#### What is a Managed Google Distributed Cloud provider and how does my company become one?

The Managed GDC Providers (MGP) is a strategic partnership initiative by [Google Cloud Partner Advantage program](https://cloud.google.com/partners) designed to accelerate Google Distributed Cloud adoption by collaborating with specialized partners who are skilled in deploying, operating, and managing services. These MGPs form a comprehensive ecosystem, providing end-to-end Google Distributed Cloud solutions, including top-tier support, robust data security, and more. By offering Google Distributed Cloud as a managed service, MGPs empower businesses to scale efficiently while maintaining high service quality.
