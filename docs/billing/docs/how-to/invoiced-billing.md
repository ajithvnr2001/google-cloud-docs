This document describes how to get started with monthly invoicing for Google Cloud. If your Cloud Billing account is already set up as an invoicing account and you'd like to learn how to pay for your Google Cloud costs, visit [ Make a payment to an invoiced Cloud Billing account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/pay-invoice). 

Cloud Billing accounts pay for Google Cloud projects and Google Maps Platform projects. A project and its service-level resources are always paid for by a Cloud Billing account.

When you first set up your Cloud Billing account, you create a [self-serve, automatically paid, account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/concepts#billing_account_types). You might be eligible to switch your account type to [monthly invoiced billing](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/concepts#billing_account_types) if your business meets certain requirements. These requirements include, but aren't limited to the following:

  * You must be registered as a business for a minimum of one year.
  * You expect to spend a minimum of $40,000 a year on Google Cloud.
  * Invoiced billing must be available in your country.



With monthly invoicing:

  * You accrue your Google Cloud costs for a month.
  * You receive your monthly invoice by email at the beginning of the following month, usually by the 5th business day of the month.
  * You can [pay your invoice](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/pay-invoice) using check or wire transfer.
  * You can increase your project and quota limits.
  * You're required to make payments within the timeframe that you've agreed to with Google, according to the [Payment Terms](https://cloud.google.com/terms/).



You might receive multiple invoices that are split across a set of transactions, when they involve resellers or multiple countries. For more information, see [Split invoicing for agency model transactions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/split-invoice).

## Check eligibility and request invoiced billing

Use this form to apply for an invoiced Cloud Billing account and find out if your organization is eligible to switch to monthly invoicing.

To apply, you must be a [Billing Account Administrator](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/billing-access#overview_of_billing_roles_in) of your organization's current billing account.

[Apply for an invoiced Cloud Billing account](https://support.google.com/cloud/contact/apply_for_invoiced_billing)
