This guide describes how to enable or disable Infrastructure Management Essentials for your Google Cloud project.

For information about Infrastructure Management Essentials, see [About Infrastructure Management Essentials](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/infra-mgmt-essentials).

## Limitations

  * The availability of Infrastructure Management Essentials is subject to certain factors. For more information, see [Availability](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/infra-mgmt-essentials#availability).
  * You can enable and disable Infrastructure Management Essentials only by using the Google Cloud console.
  * Infrastructure Management Essentials has a minimum subscription term of 30 days, after which you can cancel your subscription to it at any time. For more information, see [Billing cycle](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/infra-mgmt-essentials#billing_cycle).



## Required IAM roles

To get the permissions that you need to enable or disable Infrastructure Management Essentials in a Google Cloud project, ask your administrator to grant you the [Billing Account Administrator ](https://docs.cloud.google.com/iam/docs/roles-permissions/billing#billing.admin) (`roles/billing.admin`) IAM role on the corresponding Cloud Billing Account. For more information about granting roles, see [Manage access to projects, folders, and organizations](https://docs.cloud.google.com/iam/docs/granting-changing-revoking-access). 

You might also be able to get the required permissions through [custom roles](https://docs.cloud.google.com/iam/docs/creating-custom-roles) or other [predefined roles](https://docs.cloud.google.com/iam/docs/roles-overview#predefined). 

## Enable the Infrastructure Management Essentials

To enable Infrastructure Management Essentials for your Google Cloud project, complete the following steps:

  1. In the Google Cloud console, go to the Compute Engine **Overview** page.

[Go to Overview](https://console.cloud.google.com/compute/overview)

  2. Under **Cost and optimization** , in the card titled **Boost efficiency and savings with Infrastructure Management Essentials** , click **Learn more**.

  3. Review the information, and then click **Enable**.




Upon enablement, you see the following:

  * Confirmation text: **Enabled Infrastructure Management Essentials**.
  * Status information **Status: Active since` DATE_TIME_OF_ENABLING`**.



### Verify billing for Infrastructure Management Essentials

When Infrastructure Management Essentials is active for your Google Cloud project, you won't see individual usage or billing data for the [services included in it](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/infra-mgmt-essentials#included_services_and_use_cases). Instead, you see the **Infrastructure Management Essentials** SKU on your bill, which covers your usage of the included services.

For information about this SKU, see [Pricing](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/infra-mgmt-essentials#pricing).

## Disable the Infrastructure Management Essentials

To disable Infrastructure Management Essentials for your Google Cloud project, complete the following steps:

  1. In the Google Cloud console, go to the Compute Engine **Overview** page.

[Go to Overview](https://console.cloud.google.com/compute/overview)

  2. Under **Cost and optimization** , in the card titled **Boost efficiency and savings with Infrastructure Management Essentials** , click **Learn more**.

  3. Click **Cancel subscription > Confirm cancellation**.
