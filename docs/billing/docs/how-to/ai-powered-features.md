#  AI-powered cost monitoring and cost optimization features Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Cloud Billing uses AI-powered features to let you monitor and optimize your costs and gain better insights into your spending. These features use Google's machine learning capabilities to do the following:

  * Analyze your usage and spend patterns
  * Detect cost anomalies
  * Forecast future spend
  * Create and analyze cost reports
  * Provide intelligent cost optimization recommendations



## Anomaly detection

Cloud Billing uses an AI-powered algorithm to detect cost anomalies in your projects. Anomalies are spikes or deviations in usage costs that differ from your expected spend, based on historical spending patterns.

View and manage cost anomalies from the **Anomalies** dashboard in the Google Cloud console. The AI algorithm improves its accuracy based on your usage and your feedback.

For more information, see [View and manage cost anomalies](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/manage-anomalies).

## AI Cost Summary Agent

The AI Cost Summary Agent uses Gemini to analyze your spending on Google Cloud AI services, such as Vertex AI and Gemini API. In the **Your AI Spend** widget on the **Billing Overview** page, you can ask natural language questions to do the following:

  * Gain insights into your AI costs
  * Identify cost drivers
  * Understand spending anomalies



For more information, see [Analyze your AI spend with the AI Cost Summary Agent](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/gemini/ai-cost-summary).

## AI-driven cost forecasts

In [Cloud Billing Reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/reports), the cost forecasting feature uses advanced machine learning models to analyze historical spending trends, detect seasonality and recurring cycles, regularize data anomalies, and predict your future spend up to 12 months in advance.

For more information, see [View your forecasted costs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/reports/forecasted-costs#ai-driven-forecasts).

## Gemini Cloud Assist in Billing Reports

In [Cloud Billing Reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/reports), you can use Gemini to analyze your costs and gain insights into your cloud spend. Gemini in Cloud Billing Reports lets you do the following:

  * **Create custom reports using natural language** : Describe the report that you need, and Gemini configures the report filters for you.
  * **Summarize your spending** : Gemini provides summaries of your reports, highlighting key cost trends and drivers.
  * **Access preconfigured reports** : Use Google-configured reports for common cost management tasks.



For more information, see [Gemini Cloud Assist in Cloud Billing](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/gemini/overview#ai-assistance).

## Gemini Cloud Assist insights in FinOps hub

In the [FinOps hub](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/finops-hub), Gemini Cloud Assist provides AI-assisted insights to help you understand costs, identify waste, and prioritize cost optimization opportunities:

  * **Optimization and utilization insights** : View key optimization recommendations and high-impact utilization insights directly in the FinOps hub dashboard.
  * **Draft remediation emails** : Generate structured email drafts detailing top wasted usage and recommended optimizations to share with engineering teams.
  * **Focus on top projects** : Filter utilization insights to highlight projects with the greatest potential cost savings.



For more information, see [Gemini Cloud Assist in Cloud Billing](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/gemini/overview#ai-assistance-finops-hub).
