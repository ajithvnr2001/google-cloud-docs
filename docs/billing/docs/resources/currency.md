## Overview

This document lists countries and regions where Cloud Billing accounts are charged in local currency, as well as the payment methods accepted in each country or region to pay for Google Cloud costs.

**Note** : This document is _not_ a price list for Google Cloud products. 

  * For pricing information, see the [Google Cloud price lists](https://cloud.google.com/pricing/list).
  * If you're an existing Google Cloud customer with a Cloud Billing account, you can also view _prices by SKU_ in the [Pricing Table report](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/pricing-table).



## About payment methods for Cloud Billing accounts

  * The payment methods described in this document refer to the types that are accepted for automatically paid accounts, applicable only to [self-serve, online Cloud Billing accounts](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/concepts#billing_account_types) with [_Postpay_ and _Prepay_ charging cycles](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/billing-cycle#view_your_charging_cycle), and not to accounts paid by invoice. 
    * If your Cloud Billing account is set up as an invoicing account and you want to learn how to pay your Google Cloud invoice, see [Make a payment to an invoiced Cloud Billing account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/pay-invoice).
  * The payment methods accepted for your self-serve, online Cloud Billing account vary by country and region.
  * Your [Google payments profile](https://support.google.com/paymentscenter/topic/9017382?ref_topic=9037778) might allow [other payment methods](https://support.google.com/paymentscenter/answer/9001356#allowed-methods) that are _not accepted_ as a valid form of payment for your Cloud Billing account. These forms of payment might be valid to be used to pay for non-Google Cloud products such as Google Play and Google Ads.
  * Cloud Billing payment methods are described in more detail in [Add, remove, or update a payment method](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/payment-methods).
  * For more payment options than those on this page, consider working with a local reseller, who might be able to accept more forms of payment, or help you switch to [invoiced billing](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/concepts#billing_account_types). Find a list of resellers on the [Google partners page](https://cloud.google.com/find-a-partner/).



## About currencies for Cloud Billing accounts

Each Cloud Billing account operates in a _single currency_ , which you _can't change_ after you create your Cloud Billing account. If you need a Cloud Billing account to operate in a currency different from the currency of your existing Cloud Billing account, you need to [set up a new Cloud Billing account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/create-billing-account).

  * When Google charges in a local, non-USD currency, we convert [Google Cloud prices](https://cloud.google.com/pricing/list) from USD into the applicable local currency pursuant to the conversion rates published by leading financial institutions.
  * The exchange rates are set at the beginning of every month.

    * For [select currencies](https://cloud.google.com/skus/exchange-rates), we provide protection from large month-to-month rate changes.
    * See the [Exchange rates](https://cloud.google.com/skus/exchange-rates) documentation for more details.
  * If you [export your Cloud Billing data to BigQuery](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/export-data-bigquery), you can access the specified exchange rate information that was applied to your billing account. Look for the `currency_conversion_rate` field available in your [standard](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/export-data-bigquery-tables#standard-usage-cost-data-schema), and [detailed](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/export-data-bigquery-tables#detailed-usage-cost-data-schema), usage cost data exports and [pricing data](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/export-data-bigquery-tables#pricing-data-schema) export.

  * Not all currencies are supported for your Cloud Billing account.

**Some debit cards might not be an acceptable form of payment for Cloud Billing Accounts**

Using a debit card to complete online transactions might require 2-step verification (for example, entry of a one-time password sent by your bank directly to you using SMS). 2-step verification requires you to be in-session at the time of the transaction. Cards that require you to be in-session can't be used for subscriptions or similar recurring automatic transactions.

When you [ set up your Cloud Billing account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/create-billing-account), you must associate your billing account with a [ Google payments profile](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/concepts#billing_account) and a [ Google payments account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/concepts#payments-accounts). Your Google payments account includes the [ forms of payment](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/payment-methods) you use to pay for the costs that are accrued in your Cloud Billing account for your usage of Google Cloud and other eligible services.

When you add your debit card as a form of payment on your linked Google payments account, Google checks whether your issuing bank approves subscriptions or recurring payments using that card. If your bank doesn't approve, your debit card won't work for automatic recurring payments.

If you receive an error such as _Your card does not support automatic recurring payments_ , select a different form of payment.
