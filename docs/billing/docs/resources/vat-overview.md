#  Taxes in your country Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

For Google Cloud customers, your Cloud Billing account might be subject to taxes, such as Goods and Services Tax (GST), Value Added Tax (VAT), or other levies, depending on your location. Use this document to see. what applies in your country.

**Note:** Prices shown on [Google Cloud SKUs](https://cloud.google.com/skus) don't include taxes.

If your individual or business location isn't listed in this document, you might still be responsible for paying taxes for your Google Cloud services.

**Caution** : We cannot advise you on tax matters.. Contact your tax advisor for questions about taxes.

## Update your Google payments profile with your tax ID

If your country requires these taxes, complete the following steps to add tax information to the Google payments profile associated with your Cloud Billing account. To update your payments profile, you must have [edit permissions on the Google payments profile](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/modify-contacts#permissions).

  1. Sign in to your [Google payments account](https://payments.google.com/).
  2. Click the **Settings** tab at the top of the page.
  3. Click the edit icon (edit) next to your country's tax info and enter all required information.
  4. Click **Save**.



If you are unable to edit the tax information for your Google payments profile, this might be because you [pay by invoice](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/concepts#billing_account_types). For invoiced accounts, our support specialists can help update the tax information on your payments profile. To contact support for assistance, do the following:

  1. **Find your country listed in this document**. If you pay by invoice, in your country's section, there might be a link to a form you can fill out to provide us with. your tax information. A support specialist. will then update your payments profile.
  2. If you still need help updating the tax information on your payments profile, contact [Cloud Billing Support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/support) for further assistance.

## Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast)

To comply with local tax requirements, we charge an 18% value added tax (VAT) is charged. Value added tax (VAT) on all digital services provided to customers in Côte d'Ivoire.

Non-resident ecommerce operators providing electronic services to individuals and business customers located in Côte d'Ivoire (Ivory Coast) are obligated by the Côte d'Ivoire tax authorities to register for, charge, and remit a VAT of 18% on these supplies.

If you are registered for VAT in Côte d'Ivoire, provide us with your tax identification number (TIN). After your payments profile is updated, your TIN will appear on your invoices.

To provide us with your TIN, follow the instructions for your type of account to update your payments settings.

### Invoiced account

If you're billed by invoice, [fill out this form](https://support.google.com/paymentscenter/contact/ivoryCoast_tax_info) to contact our support team and provide your Côte d'Ivoire Tax Identification Number (TIN) so our specialists can update your Google payments profile accordingly.

### Self-serve account

If you have a [self-serve account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/concepts#billing_account_types), go to the **Google payments profile** of your billing account and add your Côte d'Ivoire Tax Identification Number (TIN).

To add your TIN to your Google Cloud account, complete the following steps:

  1. Navigate to the [**Payment settings**](https://console.cloud.google.com/billing/profile) page in your Google Cloud Cloud Billing console.
  2. At the prompt, choose the Cloud Billing account for which you want to update your tax information.
  3. In the **Tax Info** section, click the pencil icon to edit, and click **Manage tax info**.
  4. Navigate to the "Côte d'Ivoire" country card, click **Add tax info** , and enter your TIN.
  5. Click **Save**.

## European Union (EU) countries (excluding France and Ireland)

The [account type](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/create-billing-account#account_type) on your Cloud Billing account sets your tax status. You set the account type when you first create your Cloud Billing account. During [billing account setup](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/create-billing-account) in the EU region, the account type defaults to **Business**. If applicable to your tax status, you can set the account type to **Individual Entrepreneur**.

The account type _cannot be edited_ after you create your Cloud Billing account.

For customers in France or Ireland, see France or Ireland.

**Business Accounts**

VAT charges are _not_ added to your account. You're responsible for determining taxes due at your country's local rate.

**Individual Entrepreneur Accounts**

VAT is charged at your country's local applicable rate.

## Non-European Union (EU), EMEA countries (excluding South Africa and Russia)

**Business Accounts**

We won't add VAT charges to your account. You're responsible for determining taxes due at your country's local applicable rate.

**Individual Accounts**

If your country has rules which require foreign entities to register and charge VAT, then your account is charged VAT at your country's local applicable rate.

## Italy

Google Cloud services in Italy, including Google Cloud Platform, are provided by Google Cloud Italy S.r.l., from 2022. A 22% VAT (Partita IVA) typically applies to Italian customers, with the exception of the following types of customers that receive a 0% VAT rate:

  * Habitual Exporter (HE)
  * Split Payment (SP) program
  * Tax-exempt locations like San Marino or Vatican City



If you're registered for VAT in Italy, Google needs your VAT number so we can update our records and print your tax information on invoices. You'll receive a local e-invoice in addition to Google's commercial invoice. To generate an e-invoice, you must add a valid VAT ID to your Cloud Billing account. Otherwise, no e-invoice is generated.

### Google Cloud Marketplace transactions

For Google Cloud Marketplace transactions between vendors located in Italy and customers located in Italy, a 22% VAT (Partita IVA) applies to charges for purchases or usage of Cloud Marketplace products, unless a specific exemption applies to the customer.

For Google Cloud Marketplace transactions between customers located in Italy and vendors located outside of Italy, VAT charges follow the rules for European Union (EU) countries (excluding Ireland).

### Invoiced account

The Italian Tax Authority provides the approved DOI to Google through our Cassetto Fiscale (tax drawer).

### Split Payment (SP)

Google automatically determines your SP eligibility on a monthly basis by using the published SP government lists from the [Department of Finance (SDI)](https://www1.finanze.gov.it/finanze/split_payment/public/#/#testata) and [AgID](https://indicepa.gov.it/ipa-dati/dataset/unita-organizzative-che-ricevono-fatture-elettroniche).

SP eligibility is determined using your Fiscal Cloud Code (Codice Fiscale) and company name. To make sure that your SP eligibility is correctly determined, add your Fiscal Cloud Code to the **Google payments profile** in your Cloud Billing account and make sure that your company name that is on your Cloud Billing account aligns with the company name in the SP government lists.

Google automatically determines your SP eligibility on a monthly basis by using the published SP government lists from the Department of Finance and AgID. Providing the Fiscal Cloud Code is _optional_ for invoiced customers.

### Self-serve account

If you have a [self-serve account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/concepts#billing_account_types), you are required to add your Fiscal Cloud Code in the **Google payments profile** when you create your Cloud Billing account. If you're registered for VAT in Zimbabwe, you can provide Google with your Value Added Tax on Local Sales Number (VAT ID), which we will print on your invoice for tax compliance. To update our records, follow the steps in Update your Google payments profile with your tax ID.
