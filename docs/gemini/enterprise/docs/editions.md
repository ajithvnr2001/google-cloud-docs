[Gemini Enterprise](https://cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise) offers a range of AI-powered solutions designed to meet diverse organizational needs. These solutions are organized into distinct editions, each providing a specific set of features and capabilities tailored to different use cases and stages of AI adoption. This page describes these editions to help you choose the right solution for driving productivity and innovation in your organization.

## Compare features

To compare features and capabilities of different Gemini Enterprise editions, see the following table.

**Note:** To subscribe to the Gemini Enterprise Pay-as-you-go edition, your project must be linked to an [invoiced Cloud Billing account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/concepts#billing_account_types) that receives an active monthly invoice.
