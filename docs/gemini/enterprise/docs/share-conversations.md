#  Share your conversations Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

You can share your previous conversations with the assistant. When you share a conversation with someone, they can see all of the existing content of the conversation, including your prompts, the assistant's responses, and any generated content such as images or videos. They won't see any new prompts, responses, or generated content.

**Important:** If a conversation contains sensitive or confidential information (including generated content), that information is visible to anyone with whom you share the conversation. If you have a Google Workspace account, your Google Workspace administrator can restrict who you can share conversations with. If someone who isn't allowed to access a conversation tries to open the link, they see an error message.**Note:** To share conversations, your Gemini Enterprise administrator must turn on the **Enable session sharing** toggle in the web app feature configurations. For more information, see [Manage web app features](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/manage-web-app-features).

## Share a conversation

To share a conversation with the assistant, do the following:

  1. To share your current conversation, click the share icon.

  2. To share a previous conversation:

     1. In the sidebar of your Gemini Enterprise workspace, under **Chats,** hover over the chat you want to share.

     2. Click the more_vert icon.

![Share one of your conversations with the assistant](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/gemini/enterprise/docs/images/assistant/share-conversation.png)Share a conversation 
     1. Click **Share**.
  3. Click **Create link**. A link to the conversation is automatically copied to your system clipboard.


**Note:** When you share a conversation, recipients can't open or edit documents or slide presentations created in [Canvas](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/assistant-canvas). To share Canvas content with others, [export](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/assistant-canvas#export_documents_and_slides_from_canvas) the document or slide presentation to Google Docs, Google Slides, or another supported format, and then share the exported file.

## View and manage your shared conversations

To view a list of conversations you've shared, do the following:

  1. Navigate to your Gemini Enterprise app.

  2. Click the settings icon.

  3. Click **Shared chats**.

![View previously shared conversations](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/gemini/enterprise/docs/images/assistant/shared-conversations.png)View previously shared conversations 



For each shared conversation, you can see the title, when the conversation was shared, and the link. You can also delete the shared conversation.

**Note:** If you delete a shared conversation, others are no longer able to view the conversation, but it's still available in your personal chat history.
