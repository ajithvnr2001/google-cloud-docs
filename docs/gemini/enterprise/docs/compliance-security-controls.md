This page provides a high-level view of the compliance certifications and security controls that are supported by Gemini Enterprise Standard edition, Gemini Enterprise Plus edition, and Gemini Notebook Enterprise.

## Certifications

The Agentic AI Service formerly known as Agentspace, which is part of Gemini Enterprise Standard edition and Gemini Enterprise Plus edition, and Gemini Notebook Enterprise have many of the following certifications.

To find out if a product has a compliance certification, search for the product name "Gemini Enterprise" or "Gemini Notebook" in these security pages:

  * [HIPAA](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/hipaa)
  * [FedRAMP](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/fedramp)
  * [ISO 27001](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/iso-27001)
  * [ISO 27017](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/iso-27017)
  * [ISO 27018](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/iso-27018)
  * [ISO 27701](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/iso-27701)
  * [SOC 1](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/soc-1)
  * [SOC 2](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/soc-2)
  * [SOC 3](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/soc-3)
  * [PCI DSS](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/pci-dss)
  * [BSI C5:2020](https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/bsi-c5)

## AlphaEvolve limitations

AlphaEvolve doesn't support the following compliance programs:

  * International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR)
  * FedRAMP (Moderate and High)
  * Impact Level 5 (IL5)
  * Impact Level 4 (IL4)



## Antigravity limitations

Antigravity in Gemini Enterprise doesn't support the following compliance certifications and security controls:

  * Access Transparency (AXT)
  * FedRAMP (Moderate and High)
  * Impact Level 4 (IL4) and Impact Level 5 (IL5)
  * International Organization for Standardization (ISO) certifications, including ISO 27001 and ISO 42001
  * International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR)
  * Service Organization Control (SOC) 1, SOC 2, and SOC 3
