#  Get started with using the Gemini Enterprise app Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

The guides in this section help **app users, knowledge workers, and team members** use Gemini Enterprise. They describe how to navigate the app experience, search grounded company data, interact with assistants, run custom skills, and build or execute agents.

For an in-depth walkthrough of building AI agents and automating business workflows with Gemini Enterprise, you can watch this video:

## Choose your app experience

Gemini Enterprise is available as both a web app and a mobile app. Select an experience to get started:

###  Use the web app 

Access the assistant on your web browser to query company data, create documents in Canvas, and build skills or agents.

Explore web app journey

###  Use the mobile app 

Access company data, chat with agents, use voice and camera multimodal inputs, and manage tasks on the go from your mobile device.

[Use the mobile app](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/use-the-mobile-app)

## Your product journey in the Gemini Enterprise web app

To get started with Gemini Enterprise, choose a path based on your goals:

### Discover

Explore the capabilities of Gemini Enterprise and customize your experience:

Assistant 

###  Chat with the assistant 

Chat with the conversational AI assistant to answer questions using connected company data, verify sources, and draft high-quality content.

[Chat with assistant](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/assistant-chat) [Organize conversations](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/assistant-organize)

Personalization 

###  Tailor the app to your needs 

Adjust how the app looks and feels, and configure what data it can index to provide more relevant, personalized answers.

[Configure appearance](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/configure-appearance) [Configure personalization](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/configure-personalization)

Assistant 

###  Access your data with assistant actions 

Connect to Google Workspace and approved third-party apps like Jira or Microsoft to authorize the assistant to perform tasks on your behalf.

[Extend with actions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/assistant-use-actions) [Connector files](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/assistant-chat#chat-with-files-in-connectors)

Agents 

###  Simplify complex work with agents 

Browse the Agent Gallery to find Google-made agents, tools shared by your organization, or custom multi-step agents created in Workflow Builder.

[Agent Gallery](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/agent-gallery) [Deep Research](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/research-assistant) [Co-Scientist](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/co-scientist-and-alphaevolve)

Skills 

###  Complete activities using custom skills 

Browse, install, and invoke domain-specific skills to standardize recurring workflows and get consistent, repeatable results in conversations.

[Browse skills](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/skills#browse-skills)

### Create

Build your own customized experiences without needing to code:

Assistant 

###  Create documents and presentations in Canvas 

Use Canvas as a dedicated side-by-side workspace to draft, edit, and format documents and presentations directly alongside your conversation.

[Canvas guide](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/assistant-canvas)

Assistant 

###  Generate custom images and visual media 

Bring your ideas to life by asking the assistant to generate custom images and visual media for your presentations, documents, and content.

[Generate visuals](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/assistant-analyze)

Notebooks 

###  Use Gemini Notebook Enterprise 

Create private notebooks to ground AI responses in your uploaded documents, Google Drive files, and web links, and generate audio overviews.

[Overview](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/notebooklm-enterprise/docs/overview) [Use Gemini Notebook Enterprise](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/access-notebooklm)

Agents 

###  Build custom agents without writing code 

Create, update, and edit single or multi-step agents in Workflow Builder using natural language prompts, data connectors, and scheduled tasks.

[Workflow Builder overview](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/workflow-builder) [Edit an agent](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/workflow-builder/edit-chat-agent)

Skills 

###  Build custom skills for reusable workflows 

Teach the assistant domain-specific workflows and tasks by defining reusable instructions in a SKILL.md file with optional context files.

[Create & manage skills](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/skills) [Use skills in chat](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/skills#invoke-a-skill-in-a-conversation)

### Share and collaborate

Share your agents, skills, and conversations securely with your team or organization:

Assistant 

###  Share conversations 

Export chat transcripts and share conversations, research responses, and assistant outputs securely with team members.

[Share conversations](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/share-conversations)

Agents 

###  Share custom agents 

Share your custom agents securely with team members, specific Google groups, or publish them to the organization-wide gallery.

[Share an agent](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/workflow-builder/share-chat-agent)

Skills 

###  Share custom skills 

Import and export custom skill definitions to share reusable instructions, rules, and domain-specific knowledge across your team.

[Share a skill](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/skills#share-a-skill)
