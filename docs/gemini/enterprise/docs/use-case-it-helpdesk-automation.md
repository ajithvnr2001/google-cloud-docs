#  Resolve IT issues Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Resolve common IT issues without needing to contact a human technician. This frees IT staff to focus on more complex problems.

## Example prompt

In the chat box, enter a prompt such as the following:
    
    
    I'm having trouble connecting to the company VPN. My password seems to be
    correct, but it's not working. Can you help me troubleshoot?
    

Or the following:
    
    
    I need to request a new software license. What's the process and what
    information do I need to provide?
    

## Example output

In response to such a prompt, Gemini Enterprise produces output similar to the following:

> ### 1\. Basic Checks
> 
> First, let's rule out the most common and simplest culprits.
> 
> Step | Action  
> ---|---  
> Check for Typos | Carefully re-enter your password. It's easy to make a small mistake. You can try typing it into a text editor like Notepad to see the characters clearly, then copy and paste it into the password field.  
> Verify Username | Double-check that your username or email address is spelled correctly. An error there will cause the login to fail even with the right password.  
> Caps Lock | Ensure that the Caps Lock key is turned off. Passwords are almost always case-sensitive.  
> Virtual Keyboard | Try using the on-screen or virtual keyboard to enter your password. This can help rule out any issues with your physical keyboard.  
>   
> ### 2\. Browser and Device Troubleshooting
> 
> Sometimes, the problem isn't the password itself, but the environment you're using to log in.
> 
> Step | Action  
> ---|---  
> Clear Cache and Cookies | Your browser might be holding on to old, incorrect login information. Clearing the cache and cookies can resolve this.  
> Try a Different Browser | Attempt to log in using a different web browser (like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari) or a private/incognito window.  
> Disable Extensions & VPN | Browser extensions or a VPN can sometimes interfere with login processes. Try disabling them temporarily to see if that resolves the issue.  
> Use a Different Device | If possible, try logging in from another device, like your phone or a different computer. This helps determine if the issue is specific to one device.  
>   
> ### 3\. Account-Specific Issues
> 
> If the basic checks don't work, the issue might be with your account's status.
> 
> Issue | Description  
> ---|---  
> Account Lockout | If you've entered the wrong password too many times, your account may be temporarily locked for security reasons.
