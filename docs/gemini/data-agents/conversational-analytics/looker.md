#  Conversational analytics in Looker Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Conversational Analytics in Looker is a chat-with-your-data feature that is powered by Gemini for Google Cloud and grounded in the Looker semantic modeling layer. It empowers users in your organization to ask data-related questions in regular, natural language for governed, trusted self-service BI. This approach accelerates adoption of analytics across your organization by providing enterprise-grade governance and security. 

Conversational Analytics is available in both Looker (Google Cloud core) instances and in Looker (original) instances.

For more information, see [Conversational Analytics in Looker overview](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/conversational-analytics-overview) in the Looker documentation.
