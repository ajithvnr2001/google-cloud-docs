To view your logs in Splunk or on another third-party platform, route your logs to a Pub/Sub topic. For information about how to create a Pub/Sub topic and how to authorize a third-party to subscribe to the topic, see [Third-party integration with Pub/Sub](https://docs.cloud.google.com/logging/docs/export/pubsub#integrate-thru-pubsub).
