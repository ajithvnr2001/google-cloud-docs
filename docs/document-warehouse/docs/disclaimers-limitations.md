#  Document AI Warehouse disclaimers and known limitations Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

**Caution** : Document AI Warehouse is deprecated and will no longer be available on Google Cloud after January 16, 2025. To safeguard your data, migrate any documents currently saved in Document AI Warehouse to an alternative like Cloud Storage. Verify that your data migration is completed before the discontinuation date to prevent any data loss. See [Deprecations](https://docs.cloud.google.com/document-warehouse/docs/deprecations) for details.

**UI** —Use of the UI is governed by the standard [Google Cloud Terms of Service](https://cloud.google.com/terms) and the Pre-GA Offerings Terms of the Google Cloud Service Specific Terms. Its features may be unstable, may change in backward-incompatible ways, and are not guaranteed to be released. There are no SLAs provided and no technical support obligations.

**Document AI** —OCR and other document extractors are available in Document AI products but are not included in Document AI Warehouse.

These features are not part of the Document AI Warehouse product; rather, they are workflow templates made freely available for customers and account teams to configure and customize to the customer's requirements.

**API Quota** —The available API throughput (queries per second, or qps) is a shared Google Cloud resource, and customers may experience longer latency and response times during peak traffic.

**Records Retention** —Any use of Document AI Warehouse for records retention compliance is at Customer's own risk.
