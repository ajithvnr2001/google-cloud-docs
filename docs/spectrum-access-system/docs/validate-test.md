#  Validate test results with Google Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

As you go through the [test plan](https://docs.cloud.google.com/spectrum-access-system/docs/sas-iottest), we recommend that you take note of the results of the different test cases ([CBSD interoperability with SAS](https://docs.cloud.google.com/spectrum-access-system/docs/sas-iottest) and [PAL interoperability with SAS](https://docs.cloud.google.com/spectrum-access-system/docs/pal-iottest)) in the _Test Result_ spreadsheet provided by Google.

When you complete the testing, email the test results to [SAS Support](https://docs.cloud.google.com/spectrum-access-system/docs/getting-support) for validation. SAS Support adds your name to the spreadsheet as proof that you have successfully completed interoperability testing with Google SAS.

The test plan and the _Test Result_ spreadsheet are considered confidential and proprietary. If you want to share your results with a third party, then you must first get confirmation from Google.
