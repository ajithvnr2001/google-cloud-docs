# Managed devices

This page describes the concepts behind device management and how it works in the Cloud Identity Groups API.

## Devices

Device management lets you administer mobile devices, such as smartphones, tablet computers, laptops and desktop computers that are associated with your organization.

Helps to ensure that:

  * Mobile devices' access to corporate data is controlled.
  * Mobile devices conform to approved configurations and policies
  * Mobile devices can be tracked and diagnosed



Cloud Identity device management lets you manage company-owned and employee-owned (BYOD) devices.

## Access to account data

Each mobile device is associated with an account in the organization.
