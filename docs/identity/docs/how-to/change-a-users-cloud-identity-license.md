# Change a user's Cloud Identity license

If you have both the free and Premium editions of Cloud Identity, you can change a user's license from one edition to the other.

## Change a free license to Premium

You can't remove a user's free Cloud Identity license. If a user or group of users needs access to the Premium features, assign them a Premium license by following the steps in [Assign, remove, and reassign licenses](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1727173). This replaces the free license with a Premium one.

## Change a Premium license to free

You don't need to assign users a license to the free edition of Cloud Identity. If a user or group of users who have Premium licenses only need access to the core free features, remove their Premium license by following the steps in [Assign, remove, and reassign licenses](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1727173#auto). This removes their Premium license, and they get a free license automatically.

You can't remove a user's Premium license if you have automatic licensing for Cloud Identity Premium turned on for that user's organizational unit or for your organization as a whole:

  * To turn off Cloud Identity Premium automatic licensing for your entire organization, follow the steps to [automatically assign a license to everyone from the Billing page](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1727173#auto). Just turn automatic licensing off instead of on.
  * To turn off Cloud Identity Premium automatic licensing for an organizational unit, see [Set automatic licensing for organizational units](https://support.google.com/a/answer/7610656).
