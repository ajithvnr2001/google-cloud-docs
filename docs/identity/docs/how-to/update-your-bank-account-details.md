# Update your bank account details

_Applies to Cloud Identity Premium and other paid subscriptions in your Google Admin console._

_Are you fixing a payment failure?[Follow these steps instead](https://support.google.com/a/answer/2523116)._

To protect your financial information, we don't show the bank account information you add to your billing account. Therefore, you cannot update account information in the billing settings in the Google Admin console.

To update any bank account information or make sure that you entered the information correctly, you must:

  1. Add the bank account as a new payment method. For details, see [Add a bank account as primary payment method](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1218834).
  2. Remove the old bank account from your billing account. For details, see [Remove a payment method](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1229446).
