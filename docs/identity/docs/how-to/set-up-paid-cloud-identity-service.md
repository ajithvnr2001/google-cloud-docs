# Set up paid Cloud Identity service

_Applies to Cloud Identity Premium only_

To use Cloud Identity Premium beyond your [free trial period](https://docs.cloud.google.com/identity/docs/how-to/about-your-free-cloud-identity-premium-trial), set up billing for your subscription and start making payments. If you added your billing information during the sign-up process, you don't need to do anything. Your paid subscription automatically begins when your trial ends.

**Set up billing early during your free trial** —We won't charge you until your trial is over, and your service will continue uninterrupted after your trial ends. If you decide not to use Cloud Identity Premium after all, you can cancel your subscription during your trial without making any payments.

## Step 1: Start billing setup

  1. In the Google Admin console, go to **Billing > Subscriptions**.

[Go to Subscriptions](https://admin.google.com/ac/billing/subscriptions)

Requires having the [Billing management privilege](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1219251#api). If you aren't using a super administrator account, you can't complete these steps.

  2. At the top, click **Start Paid Service** or **Set Up Billing**.

  3. Follow the onscreen instructions to set up Cloud Identity Premium billing.




## Step 2: Choose your billing options

### Country and currency (can't change later)

For available currencies and payment methods, see [Payment options in my country](https://support.google.com/a/answer/2380700).

The country and currency you choose determine what payment methods you can use to pay for your subscription.

**Important:** You can't change the billing country or currency after you complete and exit billing setup.

### Payment plan

**Flexible Plan** —You're billed monthly for each user account. You can add and remove users at any time and pay only for accounts used that month. You can cancel service at any time to stop all payments. Learn more about the [Flexible Plan](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1247362).

**Annual Plan** —You commit to paying for the number of licenses you need. You can purchase more licenses at any time. However, you can't reduce licenses and lower your monthly payments until it's time to renew your annual contract. Learn more about the [Annual Plan](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1247364).

[Compare billing plans](https://docs.cloud.google.com/identity/docs/how-to/compare-cloud-identity-billing-plans)

### Account type (can't change later)

Specify whether your Cloud Identity Premium service is for individual or business use.

**Important:** Choose carefully: You _can't change your Business or Individual setting_ after you complete and exit billing setup.

#### EU: Tax status

Select the **Business** account type. If your business is in Ireland, you can enter a valid value-added tax (VAT) ID. This will prevent you from incurring Irish VAT charges of 23%.

Learn more about [Taxes in the EU](https://docs.cloud.google.com/a/topic/3722998).

#### India: Tax status

If you select the **Business** account option, select your tax status. All business accounts can enter a Goods & Services Tax identification number (GSTIN) or other tax information.

If you select the **Individual** account option, select your tax status: registered or unregistered. A registered tax status means that you have a GSTIN that you want to enter. If you don't have a GSTIN, or you don't want to enter it, select the unregistered tax status.

For both the **Business** and **Registered individual** options, entering your tax information ensures that Google charges you the correct tax for your services. If you don't enter your tax information, your account charges might increase.

Learn more about [Taxes in India](https://docs.cloud.google.com/a/topic/7437855).

#### Taiwan: Tax status

If you select the **Business** account option, you have the business tax status by default.

If you select the **Individual** account option, select your tax status: registered or unregistered. A registered tax status means that you have a value-added tax (VAT) ID number that you want to enter. If you don't have a VAT ID, or you don't want to enter it, select the unregistered tax status.

For both the **Business** and **Registered individual** options, entering your VAT ID ensures that Google charges you the correct tax for your services. If you don't enter a VAT ID, your account charges might increase.

Learn more about [Taxes in Taiwan](https://support.google.com/a/answer/7371471).

### Payment method

Enter a payment method we can charge for your monthly payment. Google accepts major credit cards in over 230 countries, including Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.

Depending on your country and currency, we might be able to withdraw payments directly from your bank account (direct debit).

For available currencies and payment methods, see [Payment options in my country](https://support.google.com/a/answer/2380700).

## Frequently asked questions

#### Why do I get an error message when I try to enter my payment information?

See the troubleshooting steps for [Billing error messages](https://support.google.com/a/answer/3530878).

#### Can I switch payment plans later?

  * **Annual/Fixed-Term to Flexible Plan** —You can switch from the Annual/Fixed-Term to the Flexible Plan during your free trial or when you renew your subscription.
  * **Flexible to Annual/Fixed-Term Plan** —You can switch from the Annual/Fixed-Term to the Flexible Plan during your free trial or when you renew your subscription. You can switch from the Flexible Plan to the Annual/Fixed-Term Plan anytime in the Admin console. When you switch, you must make an annual upfront payment.



For details, go to [Manage your payment plan & renewal](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1668003).

#### When do automatic payments occur?

  * **Google Workspace, Essentials, and Cloud Identity Premium** —After your free trial ends and your paid subscription starts, we charge your payment method at the beginning of the following month. If your paid service starts in May, for example, we charge you at the beginning of June. After that, charges continue automatically at the beginning of each month.
  * **Domain registration** —We start charging your payment method at the time of purchase. If you set up your domain for automatic renewal, we renew your domain 7 days before the annual renewal date and charge your payment method at the beginning of the following month.



Learn more about how to [understand your monthly costs](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1230658).

#### How long until my bank account can be debited?

_Applies to Italy, United States, Great Britain, Spain, Germany, the Netherlands, Austria, and France._

Before we can debit your new bank account, we need to verify that it's yours. How long this takes depends on your location. If you're in:

  * **The U.K.** —It takes about 2 days for us to verify your account. No action is required on your part.
  * **The U.S. and Europe, outside the U.K.** —You must verify the account before we can begin payments. You can do this by making a test deposit. This process can take up to 10 days, but it often happens much sooner. Go to [Verify bank account with test deposit](https://support.google.com/a/answer/1229546).



You can tell whether Google is still verifying your bank account if a **Verification in progress** or **Mandate pending** message appears on your Payments page. If you're setting up billing for the first time, your paid service won't begin until we verify your account.

#### Can I pay in advance?

Yes. You can pay ahead for your subscription instead of making monthly payments by adding credit to your account. For details, see [Make a manual payment or pay early](https://support.google.com/a/answer/6140582).
