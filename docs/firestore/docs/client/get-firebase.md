##  Learn Firebase fundamentals 

Get started with a Firebase project and adding Firebase to your app.

Explore key concepts and best practices for managing your project, protecting user privacy, and launching your app. 

![](https://firebase.google.com/static/docs/images/hero-solo-dev.png)

###  Add Firebase to your apps 

Learn how to create a Firebase project, register apps to it, and integrate the Firebase SDKs for your products and platform: [iOS, ](https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/ios/setup)[Android](https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/android/setup) and [Web](https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/web/setup)

![](https://firebase.google.com/static/docs/images/hero_team.png)

###  Manage Firebase projects 

Learn about Firebase projects, including concept overviews, deep-dives into topics like project permissions and project management tools, or preparing to launch your app. Start by [understanding Firebase projects](https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/projects/learn-more) and [reviewing the launch checklist](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/guides/launch-checklist). 

![](https://firebase.google.com/static/docs/images/hero-user-mobile.png)

###  Protect user privacy 

Protect user privacy. Learn about privacy best practices. Understand how Firebase can help protect you and your users. Read [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/privacy).
