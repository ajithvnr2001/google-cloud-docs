#  Overview of Threat Horizons previews Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Threat Horizons previews notify you about forthcoming Google Cloud Threat Horizons reports. The Google Cloud Threat Horizons report brings you strategic intelligence on threats to cloud enterprise users and the best original cloud-relevant research and security recommendations from throughout Google's intelligence and security teams.

## Who receives Threat Horizons previews

Advisory Notifications integrates with Essential Contacts to identify which users should receive notifications. Essential Contacts lets you control who receives notifications by providing a list of contacts. Make sure that your Essential Contacts are up to date. For more information, see [Managing contacts for notifications](https://docs.cloud.google.com/resource-manager/docs/managing-notification-contacts).

Threat Horizons previews are sent to Essential Contacts in the Security and All categories.

### Opting out

If you don't want to receive Threat Horizons previews, you can opt out of these notifications. For more information, see [Configure notifications](https://docs.cloud.google.com/advisory-notifications/docs/opt-in-out-notifications).
