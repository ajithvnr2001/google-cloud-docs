# Model Armor documentation

[Read product documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/model-armor/overview)

Model Armor is a Google Cloud service designed to enhance the security and safety of your AI applications. It works by proactively screening LLM prompts and responses, protecting against various risks and ensuring responsible AI practices. [Learn more](https://docs.cloud.google.com/model-armor/overview).
