  * **Latency-sensitive experiences** : Anthropic's Claude Haiku 4.5 on Google Cloud's speed is ideal for real-time applications like customer service agents and chatbots where response time is critical.
  * **Coding sub-agents** : Use Anthropic's Claude Haiku 4.5 on Google Cloud to power sub-agents, enabling multi-agent systems that tackle complex refactors, migrations, and large feature builds with quality and speed.
  * **Financial analysis** : Use Anthropic's Claude Haiku 4.5 on Google Cloud to monitor thousands of data streams—tracking regulatory changes, market signals, and portfolio risks to preemptively adapt compliance and trading systems at previously impossible scales.
  * **Research sub-agents** : Perform parallel analyses across multiple data sources while maintaining fast response times. Ideal for rapid business intelligence, competitive analysis, and real-time decision support.
