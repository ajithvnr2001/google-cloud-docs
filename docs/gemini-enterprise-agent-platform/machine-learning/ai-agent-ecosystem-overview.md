#  Overview of the Google Cloud AI Agent Ecosystem Program Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

The Google Cloud AI Agent Ecosystem Program is designed to accelerate the development, deployment, and adoption of AI agents by providing partners with the resources and support they need. Through this program, we aim to foster a rich ecosystem of innovative AI agent solutions built on top of Google Cloud's global infrastructure. 

## What are AI Agents

AI agents are AI-powered applications that can understand and respond to user requests by using natural language. They can automate tasks, personalize interactions, and improve operational efficiency across various industries.

![AI agents are applications that use generative AI models to think and act towards goals.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning/images/aiagent.png)

## AI agents in Google Cloud Marketplace

AI agents is a category within Google Cloud Marketplace that's specifically dedicated to partner-built AI agents. This category simplifies the discovery and deployment process for customers, with many AI agents listed and ready to use.

## Join the ecosystem

By participating in the Google Cloud AI Agent Ecosystem Program, you gain access to a wealth of resources and support to help you build and deploy successful AI agents. We're excited to see the innovative solutions that you create and the positive impact they have on businesses around the world.

### Benefits

  * **Accelerated Development** : Gain access to Google Cloud product and engineering teams for guidance and optimization of your AI agents. Receive early access to cutting-edge AI technologies, technical enablement resources, and dedicated support to bring your solutions to market faster through Google Cloud Marketplace.
  * **Go-to-Market Success** : Use go-to-market programs and co-selling opportunities that are designed specifically for AI agent solutions. Effectively promote your offerings and reach a wider range of customers.
  * **Increased Visibility** : Get featured through targeted marketing resources, blogs, and dedicated events. This promotion enhances the visibility of your AI agents and help them stand out in the growing AI ecosystem.



### Eligibility

The program focuses on select independent software vendor (ISV) and systems integrator (SI) partners, as well as larger developer and partner communities. To qualify, your AI agent must meet the following technical requirements:

  * Address a specific goal, potentially using tools, reasoning, or planning capabilities.
  * Uses a Gemini model or a third-party model from the Model Garden.
  * Is deployed on Google Cloud.
  * Uses or plans to migrate to Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform services beyond its Large Language Model (LLM) offerings.



## Apply to the program

Partners can apply by visiting [Google Cloud Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace/browse?filter=category:ai-agent) or by contacting their partner representatives to explore collaboration opportunities. You can also apply by filling out the [application form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeDnpJ3ba09_gpCVX0KfpaXGhfSbCeMWhC7hfdZq-ux7yPAWQ/viewform?resourcekey=0-25js3rOW_VGc4NmmjwN5Jw).
