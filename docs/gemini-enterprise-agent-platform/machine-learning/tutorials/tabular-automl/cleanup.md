#  Hello tabular data: Clean up your project Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Clean up the Google Cloud resources that you created during this tutorial. Follow these steps to avoid incurring unexpected charges from some of the resources.

## Delete Agent Platform resources

This section describes how delete the following project resources: endpoint, model, and dataset.

### Delete your endpoint

  1. In the Google Cloud console, in the Agent Platform section, go to the **Endpoints** page.

[Go to the Endpoints page](https://console.cloud.google.com/agent-platform/endpoints)

  2. Click your endpoint to open its details page.

  3. On the row for your model, click **Undeploy model delete**.

  4. In the **Undeploy model from endpoint** dialog, click **Undeploy**.

  5. Return to the **Endpoints** tab, and find your endpoint. Click **View more more_vert**.

  6. Click **Remove endpoint** and confirm the operation.




### Delete your model

  1. In the Google Cloud console, in the Agent Platform section, go to the **Models** page.

[Go to the Models page](https://console.cloud.google.com/agent-platform/models)

  2. Find your model. On that row, click **View more more_vert**.

  3. Click **Delete model** and confirm the operation.




### Delete your dataset

  1. In the Google Cloud console, in the Agent Platform section, go to the **Datasets** page.

[Go to the Datasets page](https://console.cloud.google.com/agent-platform/datasets)

  2. Find your dataset. On that row, click **View more more_vert**.

  3. Click **Delete dataset**.
