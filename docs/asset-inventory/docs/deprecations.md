#  Deprecations and shut down features Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

The [Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service (section "Discontinuation of Services")](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms) defines the deprecation policy that applies to Cloud Asset Inventory. The [deprecation policy](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms/deprecation) only applies to the services, features, or products listed therein. 

After a service, feature, or product is officially deprecated, it continues to be available for at least the period of time defined in the Terms of Service. After this period of time, the service is scheduled for shutdown. 

Feature | Deprecated date | Shutdown date | Details  
---|---|---|---  
Cloud Asset Inventory API v1p4beta1 | May 17, 2021 | May 17, 2022 | On May 17, 2022, the Cloud Asset Inventory API version 1.4 Beta1 will be removed. We recommend upgrading to Cloud Asset Inventory API version 1.
