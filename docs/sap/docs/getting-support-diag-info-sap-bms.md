#  SAP on Bare Metal Solution diagnostic information Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

For your SAP workload on Bare Metal Solution, if you experience problems with the Bare Metal Solution server, storage, or network, capture and collect the following information and contact Cloud Customer Care:

  * The exact time that the issue occurred.
  * What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
  * All relevant logs, traces, and other diagnostic information that might be relevant, such as an extract from `/var/log/messages`.



If you are having issues with a RHEL HA cluster, gather the required information from both cluster nodes and contact Cloud Customer Care. For a list of the required diagnostic information, see [High-availability clusters on RHEL diagnostic information](https://docs.cloud.google.com/sap/docs/getting-support-diag-info-ha-rhel).

If you are having issues with a SLES HA cluster, gather the required information from both cluster nodes and contact Customer Care. For a list of the required diagnostic information, see [High-availability clusters on SLES diagnostic information](https://docs.cloud.google.com/sap/docs/getting-support-diag-info-ha-sles).
