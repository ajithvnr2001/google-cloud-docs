This topic provides a conceptual overview of Cyber Insurance Hub. Cyber Insurance Hub is a component of the [Risk Protection Program (RPP)](https://docs.cloud.google.com/risk-protection-program).

Cyber Insurance Hub is a Google Cloud security tool that offers insight into your organization's technical risk posture. It lets you generate reports to profile risk across your organization. You can generate a view of your organization's risk posture and understand where to focus your investments to reduce risk. Cyber Insurance Hub integrates with other Google Cloud tools like Security Command Center to provide fast remediation of risk.

Cyber Insurance Hub simplifies the process of obtaining cyber insurance by letting you send reports directly to select cyber insurance partners. By sharing Cyber Insurance Hub reports with our insurance partners, you can unlock cyber insurance policies with fast quoting, broad coverage, and competitive pricing. Learn more about the [Risk Protection Program](https://docs.cloud.google.com/security/products/risk-protection-program) and our cyber insurance partners' offerings: [Full Spectrum Cyber for Google Cloud](https://www.beazley.com/fullspectrumcyber/google-cloud/) by Beazley, [Google Cloud Risk Protection Endorsement](https://www.chubb.com/risk-protection-program.html) by Chubb, and [Cloud Protection+](https://www.cloud-protection-plus.com/en.html) by Munich Re.

## Cyber Insurance Hub reports

A report is an organization-level object that aggregates information from various sources like [Cloud Asset Inventory](https://docs.cloud.google.com/asset-inventory) and [Security Command Center](https://docs.cloud.google.com/security-command-center) to build an aggregate view of risk across your organization. These reports are aligned to the [CIS Google Cloud Platform Foundation Benchmark v1.2.0](https://www.cisecurity.org/insights/blog/cis-benchmarks-may-2021-update). For more information on this framework, see [Vulnerability findings](https://docs.cloud.google.com/security-command-center/docs/concepts-vulnerabilities-findings).

Cyber Insurance Hub lets you [create manual reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/risk-manager/docs/managing-reports#create) on an ad hoc basis or [schedule reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/risk-manager/docs/automated-reports) to be automatically created on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis.

## Report sharing and approval

Before sharing your data with our insurance carrier partners, you can invite other members of your organization to review and [approve](https://docs.cloud.google.com/risk-manager/docs/managing-reports#approve) reports directly within Cyber Insurance Hub.

You control your data and when to send a report to our insurance partners. Data is sent when you explicitly [do so through Cyber Insurance Hub](https://docs.cloud.google.com/risk-manager/docs/managing-reports#send).

Sending a report to our insurance partners will prompt them to generate a quote for cyber insurance coverage. Engage with your insurance broker to continue the insurance conversation.
