# Video Intelligence API Deprecations  |  Google Cloud Documentation

> Source: https://docs.cloud.google.com/video-intelligence/docs/deprecations/

Starting on September 14, 2026, Video Intelligence API is officially deprecated and will no longer be supported. You can continue using Video Intelligence API until September 14, 2027, when it will be shut down. We recommend migrating to [Gemini family of models](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/models/google-models).

The [Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service (section "Discontinuation of Services")](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms) defines the deprecation policy that applies to Video Intelligence API. The [deprecation policy](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms/deprecation) only applies to the services, features, or products listed therein. 

After a service, feature, or product is officially deprecated, it continues to be available for at least the period of time defined in the Terms of Service. After this period of time, the service is scheduled for shutdown. 

Feature | Deprecated date | Shutdown date | Details  
---|---|---|---  
Video Intelligence API Celebrity Recognition | September 16, 2024 | September 16, 2025 | This feature is deprecated and will no longer be available on Google Cloud after September 16, 2025.
