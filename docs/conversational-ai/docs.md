# Conversational AI documentation

Google Cloud Conversational AI is a collection of conversational AI tools, solutions and APIs, both designers and developers can use: 

  * **Conversational AI Platforms (CAIP)**
    * [Dialogflow ES](https://docs.cloud.google.com/dialogflow/es/docs) for building small to medium, and simple to moderately complex virtual agents. ([Console](https://dialogflow.cloud.google.com/))
    * [Dialogflow CX](https://docs.cloud.google.com/dialogflow/cx/docs) for building large and complex virtual agents. ([Console](https://dialogflow.cloud.google.com/cx))
  * **Contact Center Solutions**
    * [Contact Center AI (CCAI)](https://docs.cloud.google.com/solutions/contact-center) for automating conversations in contact centers. ([Console](https://ccai.cloud.google.com/projects))
    * [CCAI Platform](https://docs.cloud.google.com/solutions/contact-center-ai-platform) a Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS).
    * [CCAI Insights](https://docs.cloud.google.com/contact-center/insights/docs) for processing and identifying call drivers and sentiment. ([Console](https://ccai.cloud.google.com/insights/projects))
    * [Agent Assist](https://docs.cloud.google.com/agent-assist/docs) for supporting human agents during calls and chats by providing real-time assistance. ([Console](https://agentassist.cloud.google.com/projects))
    * [Dialogflow CX](https://docs.cloud.google.com/dialogflow/cx/docs) for building virtual agents in contact centers. ([Console](https://dialogflow.cloud.google.com/cx))

There are many different ways to get started with Contact Center AI. Different products and tools are better suited for different technical requirements and types of users. The flowchart below is designed to guide you to the right product to solve a given problem in CCAI, whether you're just getting started or trying to determine where to go next in your journey to improve your customer experience. 

![Diagram for users to determine which tools they should use to solve a given problem in CCAI](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/conversational-ai/docs/img/decision-tree-ccai.png)
  * **Language and Voice processing APIs**
    * [Speech-to-Text (STT)](https://docs.cloud.google.com/speech-to-text/docs) for transcribing spoken voice to text. ([Console](https://console.cloud.google.com/speech))
    * [Text-to-Speech (TTS)](https://docs.cloud.google.com/text-to-speech/docs) for synthesizing written text to human voice.
    * [Text-to-Speech Custom Voice](https://docs.cloud.google.com/text-to-speech/custom-voice/docs) allows you to train a custom voice model using your own studio-quality audio recordings to create a unique voice.
    * [Natural Language](https://docs.cloud.google.com/natural-language/docs) for understanding text.
    * [Translation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/translation/docs) for translating text.
  * **Related Products**
    * [Business Messages](https://businessmessages.google/) Delight customers and drive results right from Google Search, Maps, and brand-owned channels. ([Console](https://business-communications.cloud.google.com/console/))
    * [Google Assistant](https://assistant.google.com/) Actions on Google is a development platform for the Google Assistant. It allows the third-party development. ([Console](https://console.actions.google.com/))
    * [Looker Dialogflow Analytics Parser Block](https://looker.com/platform/blocks/source/dialogflow-parser-by-google)
