Features

### Understand and monitor your security risk

Google’s security diagnostic tool, Cyber Insurance Hub, scans your workloads on Google Cloud and provides proactive security recommendations to minimize misconfigurations, drive down risk, and boost security readiness. Cyber Insurance Hub generates a report that helps you understand your security risk posture on an ongoing basis and serves as an indicator of your security baseline, so you can identify where to route your security investments.

### Share reports to qualify for cyber insurance

Cyber Insurance Hub gives you a report that maps your cloud configurations against industry standard CIS Benchmarks. The report can be shared with our partner insurers Beazley, Chubb, and Munich Re directly from the UI to determine underwriting eligibility for cyber insurance policies designed exclusively for Google Cloud customers.

### Affirmative AI insurance coverage

Through our expanded partnership, we offer Affirmative AI insurance coverage for your Google-related AI workloads, building upon our existing [AI indemnification](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/protecting-customers-with-generative-ai-indemnification) commitment.

### Quantum exploit coverage

Supporting Google’s commitment to [Post-Quantum Cryptography](https://cloud.google.com/security/resources/post-quantum-cryptography) (PQC), Chubb’s offerings include coverage specifically against quantum exploit risks, proactively helping to address risks and foster preparedness for quantum computing attacks.

### Exclusive Mandiant services

Munich Re’s policyholders receive access to exclusive Mandiant services. This offering helps support stronger resilience and business continuity for all insured.

### Accelerated onboarding for digital natives

For our Google Cloud digital native customers, Beazley is removing the traditional, often complex insurance application altogether and replacing it with a single-page attestation. This means an even faster, simpler path to securing tailored cyber insurance for organizations deeply integrated with Google Cloud.

Powered by Security Command Center, Cyber Insurance Hub allows customers to directly share their risk posture data with participating cyber insurance partners.

Cyber Insurance Hub represents a significant improvement over traditional, often lengthy and manual insurance applications, leading to a more streamlined underwriting process and competitive pricing for reducing risk.

#### Who can apply for the cyber insurance policy through the Risk Protection Program?

Any global entity using Google Cloud and falling into the appetite of our partner carriers: Beazley, Chubb, and Munich Re. More information about each carrier’s appetite and insurance offerings can be found on their product websites: [Beazley’s offering](https://www.beazley.com/fullspectrumcyber/google-cloud/); [Chubb’s offering](https://www.chubb.com/risk-protection-program.html); [Munich Re’s offering](https://www.cloud-protection-plus.com/en.html).

#### Does the Risk Protection Program replace the traditional cyber insurance process?

No. Google is not a licensed insurance broker and does not provide advice on nor issue insurance policies. The Risk Protection Program is a partnership with the insurance industry that leverages our technology to help customers lower their security risk and obtain specialized cyber insurance and competitive insurance premiums. To apply for insurance through our partner carriers, [submit](https://cloud.google.com/risk-manager/docs/managing-reports#send) your Cyber Insurance Hub report, then work with a licensed insurance broker to complete the submission process.

#### I don’t have a cyber insurance broker. Can Google recommend a broker to me?

Google cannot make recommendations related to your choice of broker. However, any broker should be able to access the program as the Risk Protection Program is not exclusive. Google does work closely with several major broker partners for the Risk Protection Program, which means they may be more familiar with the program. You may find their contact information on [our documentation pages](https://cloud.google.com/risk-manager/docs/managing-reports#reach_out_to_your_broker). You may also reach out to our insurance partners who may be able to offer a panel of broker options in your region: [Beazley’s product page](https://www.beazley.com/fullspectrumcyber/google-cloud/); [Chubb’s product page](https://www.chubb.com/risk-protection-program.html); [Munich Re’s product page](https://www.cloud-protection-plus.com/en.html).

#### Do I have to pay for Cyber Insurance Hub?

Cyber Insurance Hub is available at no additional charge for Google Cloud customers. Security Command Center Standard (also available for no additional charge) is required for the tool to run. To get started, the customer will need to contact their Google Cloud Organization Administrator or Security Administrator to enable Google Cloud’s Cyber Insurance Hub using our [public documentation](https://cloud.google.com/risk-manager/docs). This person is likely to be the one responsible for the day-to-day operation or security of cloud deployment.

#### I use multiple cloud providers and/or on-premises solutions—am I able to apply for a cyber insurance policy?

Yes. The insurance policies offered by our partners cover a customer’s entire IT footprint. The Risk Protection Program may be leveraged by any Google Cloud customer. You do not need to be a sole Google Cloud user—if you are a multicloud user or have a combination of cloud and on-premises, your organization can still benefit from this product.

Note that Cyber Insurance Hub only scans Google Cloud workloads. Those with stronger relative spend on Google Cloud may benefit more from the program due to enhanced Google Cloud coverage and competitive pricing.

#### I use Google Workspace, but not Google Cloud. Am I eligible for the Risk Protection Program?

No, this program is only available to Google Cloud users.

#### I am not an appointed broker with the insurers who are part of this program. How can I bring this program to my client?

You can access our program through our insurance partners’ preferred wholesale brokers. Please send your submission to our insurance partners and ask your client to submit their Cyber Insurance Hub report. Our partner(s) will reach out with the next steps to get you assigned with a wholesale broker. If you have any trouble with this process please report it at [rpp-brokers@google.com](mailto:rpp-brokers@google.com).

#### I am having trouble using Cyber Insurance Hub. What should I do?

We recommend you [contact Google Cloud Support](https://console.cloud.google.com/support/createcase/) and/or your assigned sales representative. You may also [contact the Risk Protection Program](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd-DUMctn7J2_jLM2YMGzmsjt9Y6ZCrNW0X6v6wModh4puL5A/viewform) team directly.
