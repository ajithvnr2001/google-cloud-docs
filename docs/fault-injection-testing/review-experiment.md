This page explains how to review the results of your experiments.

Before proceeding, you should have Viewer permissions for the experiment (`roles/faulttesting.viewer` or `roles/faulttesting.operator` roles).

You can review the results of experiments that have one of the following states:

  * They completed successfully and their status is **Completed**.
  * They were manually stopped.



To review concluded experiments:

  1. Select **Experiment** in the left-hand navigation.
  2. Select an experiment in the list. You can filter or sort the list by date range, fault type, impacted product, or status.



The details of the experiment appear, with its metadata and logs.
