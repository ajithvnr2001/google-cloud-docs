#  Step 3: Customize the logo Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

_This page applies to**Apigee** and **Apigee hybrid**._

_View[ Apigee Edge](https://docs.apigee.com/api-platform/get-started/what-apigee-edge) documentation._ ![](https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/i/googlematerialicons/help/v13/gm_grey-24dp/1x/gm_help_gm_grey_24dp.png)

Notice the company logo that appears in the top navigation bar in the portal. You're going to replace it in this step.

To replace the logo:

  1. Obtain a copy of your company logo that would be suitable for use in the top navigation bar for **desktop** (approximate size: 192 x 32 pixels) and **mobile** (approximate size: 156 x 32 pixels) displays.

**Note:** If your desktop and mobile logos are a different size than the defaults, they are resized automatically to 60% of the height of the navigation bar.
  2. Upload the primary logo.

     1. In the Theme editor, under **Basic Styles** , click **Browse**.
     2. Navigate to and select the logo file.
     3. Click **Open**.
  3. Upload the mobile logo.

     1. In the Theme editor, under **Basic Styles** , expand **Logo options**.
     2. Click **Browse**.
     3. Navigate to and select the mobile logo file.
     4. Click **Open**.
  4. Click **Save**.

  5. Click **Preview** to preview the portal changes in a browser. Depending on the width of your browser, you may see the desktop or mobile version of the logo.

  6. Click **Publish** to publish the changes to your portal. Confirm the operation when prompted.

  7. Click **View Portal** to open the live portal in a new browser tab (or refresh the tab in which it is already open).




Next, you'll **edit page content** in the portal.
