#  Steps to build your portal Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

_This page applies to**Apigee** and **Apigee hybrid**._

_View[ Apigee Edge](https://docs.apigee.com/api-platform/get-started/what-apigee-edge) documentation._ ![](https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/i/googlematerialicons/help/v13/gm_grey-24dp/1x/gm_help_gm_grey_24dp.png)

To build your portal, perform the steps defined in the following table.

Step | Description  
---|---  
[Manage your portals](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/manage-portals) | Create and manage your portals.  
[Manage the pages in your portal](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/portal-pages) | Create and manage your portal pages.  
[Develop portal content using the page editor](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/page-editor) | Customize the content on your pages using the page editor. See also [Markdown syntax quick reference](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/markdown-reference).  
[Customize your theme](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/api-portal-themes) | Customize, preview, and publish the theme for your portal.  
[Set up navigation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/portal-menus) | Add menus and container items to define how to navigate your portal.  
[Publish your APIs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/publish-apis) | Publish your APIs and auto-generate API reference documentation.  
[Manage access to your APIs using API keys](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/manage-api-keys) | Control who can access your APIs.  
[Manage the default visibility](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/default-audience) | Manage the default visibility for specific resources.  
[Manage your assets](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/portal-files) | Upload your own custom files and images for use in your portal.  
[Customize your domain](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/custom-domain) | Provide your own custom domain name to personalize your site.  
[Configure email](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/configure-email) | Configure your SMTP server and the content of email notifications.  
[Configure analytics tracking](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/api-portal-analytics) | Configure analytics tracking using Google Analytics or your own custom analytics tracking service.  
[Add custom scripts](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/custom-scripts) | Add custom JavaScript code to your portal.  
[Publish your portal content](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/portal-publish) | Publish your portal content to the live site.  
[View your live portal](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/view-live-portal) | View the currently published version of your portal.  
[Create a custom search page](https://docs.cloud.google.com/apigee/docs/api-platform/publish/portal/search-page) | Create a custom search page and embed a Google custom search engine.
