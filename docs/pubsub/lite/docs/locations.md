**Note:** Pub/Sub Lite is deprecated. Effective January 31, 2027, Pub/Sub Lite will be turned down.   


  * Current customers: Pub/Sub Lite remains functional until January 31, 2027.   
If you have not used Pub/Sub Lite within the 90-day period preceding July 15, 2025 (April 15, 2025 - July 15, 2025), you won't be able to access Pub/Sub Lite starting on July 15, 2025. 
  * New customers: Pub/Sub Lite is no longer available for new customers after September 24, 2024.



You can migrate your Pub/Sub Lite service to [Google Cloud Managed Service for Apache Kafka](https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/lite/docs/migrate-pubsub-lite-to-managed-service-for-apache-kafka) or [Pub/Sub](https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/lite/docs/migrate-pubsub-lite-to-pubsub).

This page lists the regions and zones where Pub/Sub Lite is available.

  * A regional Lite topic requires you to only specify a region. For example, `asia-east1(Taiwan)` for the **Region** field in the Cloud console. Regional Lite topics are only supported in Apache Beam version 2.34.0 and higher.

  * A zonal Lite topic requires you to specify a region and a zone within the region. For example, `asia-east1(Taiwan)` for **Region** and `asia-east1-a` for **Zone** fields in the Cloud console.




To reduce the end-to-end latency of a Pub/Sub Lite system, store messages in a zone close to where the [publisher](https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/lite/docs/publishing) and [subscriber](https://docs.cloud.google.com/pubsub/lite/docs/subscribing) applications run.

For more information, see [Geography and regions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/geography-and-regions).
