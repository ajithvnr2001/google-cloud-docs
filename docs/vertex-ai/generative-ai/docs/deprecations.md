The [Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service (section "Discontinuation of Services")](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms) defines the deprecation policy that applies to Generative AI on Vertex AI. The [deprecation policy](https://docs.cloud.google.com/terms/deprecation) only applies to the services, features, or products listed therein. 

After a service, feature, or product is officially deprecated, it continues to be available for at least the period of time defined in the Terms of Service. After this period of time, the service is scheduled for shutdown. 

**Feature** | **Deprecated date** | **Shutdown date** | **Details**  
---|---|---|---  
Generative AI module in the Vertex AI SDK | June 24, 2025 | June 24, 2026 |  Starting on June 24, 2025, the Generative AI module in the Vertex AI SDK is deprecated. The Generative AI module will be removed on June 24, 2026. For information about migrating to the Google Gen AI SDK, see [Vertex AI SDK migration guide](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/deprecations/genai-vertexai-sdk.md).
