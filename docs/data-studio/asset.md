#  Asset Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

A Data Studio report or data source.

Data Studio is based on 2 types of asset:

  * **Reports** \- A Data Studio asset that presents information and insights derived from your data.
  * **Data sources** \- A specific instance of a connection to your data. Data sources provide the fields (metrics and dimensions) available for you to visualize in your reports.
