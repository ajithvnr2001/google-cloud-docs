#  Step 3: Purchase Data Studio Pro licenses Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

After you've [selected a Google Cloud project](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/step-1-select-a-google-cloud-project) and have [added users and groups](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/step-2-add-people-to-the-subscription) to your Data Studio Pro subscription, you can manage the number of Data Studio Pro licenses that support your subscription.

In this step of the subscription process, Data Studio displays the number of licenses that your subscription requires. Your organization must purchase at least this number of licenses to support the number of users and groups that you added in the [previous step](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/step-2-add-people-to-the-subscription). To manage licenses for your Data Studio Pro subscription, you must meet [these requirements](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/start-a-new-pro-subscription#before_you_begin).

**Note:** Looker users who are setting up a Data Studio Pro subscription to make use of [complimentary licenses](https://cloud.google.com/terms/looker-studio/pro/complimentary-license-details) will also see the number of no-cost licenses reflected in this step. Your organization is effectively purchasing these licenses at no cost. If the number of users that you have added to your subscription exceeds the number of complimentary Data Studio Pro licenses that are allocated to your Looker instance or account, your organization must purchase paid licenses to cover the difference or remove users from the subscription. The **Total monthly cost** line item displays the cost of the additional licenses that you are purchasing.

You can purchase licenses and then assign them to users as needed — so the number of licenses may be greater than the number of users, but the number of users cannot exceed the number of licenses. You might want to do this if any of the following statements apply:

  * You didn't add users during the [previous step in setting up the subscription](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/step-2-add-people-to-the-subscription).
  * You're setting up the subscription but someone else in your organization will add users and groups later.
  * Your organization is growing and new users will be coming online soon.



Every user of Data Studio Pro, including those within a group, must have a Pro license. You'll be billed for the number of Pro licenses in the subscription, even if the license isn't currently in use.

### Automatically assign licenses to new users in Pro groups

When you select the **Auto assign available licenses to subscribed groups** option, Data Studio Pro automatically assigns available licenses to new users who join or who are added to Google Groups that are part of the subscription. Data Studio assigns available licenses in the order that users open or use the application, until all licenses are assigned.

This option simplifies the process of assigning new Pro users without having to [edit your subscription details](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/edit-a-pro-subscription).

## Review and confirm your subscription details

Before completing your subscription, you can review the following details:

  * Number of licenses in your Data Studio Pro subscription
  * Amount that you'll be billed each month
  * Duration of the [trial period](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/pro-subscription-overview), if applicable
  * Google Cloud project that you'll use for Pro content
  * Billing account that is associated with the Google Cloud project that you [selected](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/step-1-select-a-google-cloud-project) when you initiated the subscription
  * The number of no-cost, complimentary Data Studio Pro licenses that may be included in your subscription



Enabling Data Studio Pro features for your users should take only a few minutes. You'll see a notification when the upgrade is complete. You'll also see the Data Studio Pro logo in the upper left corner of the home page (you may need to refresh your browser to see this).
