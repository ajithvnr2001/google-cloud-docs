#  Tutorial: View and share your report Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/data-studio/images/bluestopwatchtimersessionicon-2015-09-11.png)

You can use Data Studio to transform your data into customizable reports, which you can then view and share. To help your teams make data-driven decisions, you can configure sharing settings and manage user access for your Data Studio reports. In this tutorial, you learn how to perform these configurations.

## Step 1: View the report

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/data-studio/images/icschoolgrey60024dp-2014-12-01-image.png) View mode displays your report exactly as others will see it when you share it with them.

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/data-studio/images/view--button-2020-03-12.png)

### To view the report

Click ![Visibility icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/data-studio/images/gmvisibilitygrey60048dp-2020-03-12.png)**View**.

To return to edit mode, click ![Edit icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/data-studio/images/gmcreategrey60048dp-2020-03-12.png)**Edit**.

## Step 2: Share the report

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/data-studio/images/icschoolgrey60024dp-2014-12-01-image.png) You determine who can view and who can edit your report.

![](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/data-studio/images/share-view-mode-2017-07-14.png)

### To share the report

  1. Click ![Share icon.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/data-studio/images/gmpersonaddgrey60048dp-2020-03-12.png)**Share**.
  2. You can specify individual email addresses, or use one or more Google Groups, or share with both.
  3. If you only want to grant _View_ access, change the drop-down menu to **Can view**.
  4. Click the **Advanced** link to set additional sharing options.



[Learn more about sharing reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/ways-to-share-your-reports).

## Next step

When you're ready to create reports with your own data, the [data sources tutorials](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/tutorial-data-source-intro) can help get you started.
