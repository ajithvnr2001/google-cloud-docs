#  Delete your Data Studio service Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Data Studio is a service that is linked to your Google Account, not a standalone account that can be deleted independently. To completely stop using Data Studio, delete or transfer ownership of your Data Studio assets and then disconnect Data Studio from your Google Account.

## Permanently delete Data Studio assets

Before you disconnect Data Studio from your Google Account, delete your reports, data sources, and other Data Studio assets by following the steps in this section.

### Delete reports

To move reports to the trash and permanently delete them, follow the steps in [Delete a report](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/delete-a-report).

Alternatively, you can [transfer ownership of your reports and data sources](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/transfer-ownership) to another user.

### Delete reusable data sources

To delete reusable data sources, move them to the trash and permanently delete them by following the steps in [Delete a data source](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/delete-a-data-source).

**Note:** Embedded data sources are deleted when the parent report is deleted.

### Delete data apps

If you created Data Apps in BigQuery, follow the steps in [Delete a data app](https://docs.cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs/colab-data-apps#delete-data-app) to delete the apps.

**Note:** Deleting Data Apps by using the Data Studio UI doesn't delete the source Colab notebook or the underlying BigQuery dataset. You must delete those assets separately.

## Disconnect Data Studio from your Google Account

After you delete your assets, you can [remove Data Studio's access to your Google Account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/remove-or-reconnect-access-to-your-google-account).

Removing account access revokes permissions for Data Studio in your Google Account security settings. However, removing access doesn't delete your reports or data sources. Always delete your assets before you remove account access.

## Cancel your Data Studio Pro subscription

If you use Data Studio Pro, deleting your assets doesn't automatically cancel your subscription or stop recurring charges. To stop charges, you must [cancel your Data Studio Pro subscription](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/cancel-a-pro-subscription).
