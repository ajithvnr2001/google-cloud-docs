#  LOG10 Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Returns the logarithm of a positive number with base 10.

## Sample usage

`LOG10(Users)`

## Syntax

`LOG10( X )`

### Parameters

  * `X` \- a field or expression containing at least one field. `X` must be positive.



## Examples

Example formula | Input | Output  
---|---|---  
` LOG10(Users) ` |  1  |  0   
` LOG10(Users) ` |  100  |  2
