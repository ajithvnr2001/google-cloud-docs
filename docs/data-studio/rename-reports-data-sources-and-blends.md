#  Rename reports, data sources, and blends Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

By default, when you create an asset -- reports, data sources, and blends - it's named "Untitled <asset>". You can rename your Data Studio assets to something more useful.

## Rename an asset from the home page

To rename an asset from the Data Studio home page, follow these steps:

  1. Select **Reports** or **Data Sources** , and then locate the asset.
  2. Hover over the asset to reveal the **More options** more_vert menu, and then click **Rename**.



## Rename an asset by editing the report or data source

An asset's name appears in the upper left corner when you view it. To rename the asset, follow these steps:

  1. Edit the **[report](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/edit-a-report)** or **[data source](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/edit-a-data-source)**.
  2. Click the current name and enter the new name.



Renaming an exploration works similarly to renaming a report.

**Tip:** To rename a blend, edit the report that contains the blend, and then use the **Resource > Manage blends** menu to edit the blend.
