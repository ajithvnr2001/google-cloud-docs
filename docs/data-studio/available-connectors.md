#  Available connectors Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Connect external data platforms to Data Studio by using Google connectors and third-party community connectors. Connecting your data helps you build interactive dashboards, visualize live metrics, and share actionable insights across your organization. You can use the following catalog to find supported connectors for your database, spreadsheet, or advertising platform.

Category  | Connector   
---|---

| [Looker](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/connect-to-looker)
