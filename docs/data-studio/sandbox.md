#  Sandbox Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

A location for personal content in Data Studio Pro.

Use your **Sandbox** to develop content that includes Data Studio Pro features. Unless you share the content directly, other Data Studio Pro users can't access that content. (Your organization's administrators can always access your content, however.)

**Note:** Sandboxes are a Data Studio Pro feature.
