#  Change user interface language Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

To use Data Studio in your preferred language, you can change the interface language for Data Studio specifically or for all Google services.

  * To change the language on all of your web-based Google services, follow the steps on the [Change your language on the web](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/32047) documentation page.
  * To change your language in Data Studio only, add the `?hl=` parameter to your URL, replacing `<language code>` with a [supported language code](https://developers.google.com/admin-sdk/directory/v1/languages). For example, to change your language to Spanish, enter the following URL in your browser: `https://datastudio.google.com/?hl=es`



## Limits

Note the following limits of changing the interface language:

  * Text in your data won't be translated.
  * Labels, field names, or other text elements that you have edited in Data Studio won't be translated.
  * You cannot change the language for other Data Studio users.
  * You cannot set a language at the report or chart level.
  * For Google Analytics and Google Ads data sources, if you change your language and then [refresh your data source fields](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/refresh-data-source-fields), the field names will revert to their default names in the new language.
  * When you schedule a report delivery, the content of the report, such as numbers and dates, will appear in English, even if your Google Account is set to another language.
