#  Owned by me Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

A location in Data Studio and Data Studio Pro for content that you own.

Content in **Owned by me** includes assets that you've created or that have been transferred to your ownership. Unless you share the content directly, other Data Studio users can't access that content. (Your organization's administrator can always access your content, however.)

Content in **Owned by me** can't use Data Studio Pro features. If you are a Data Studio Pro subscriber, you can move your content to your [Sandbox](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/sandbox) or to a team workspace in order to use Data Studio Pro features.

> **Note** : Your organization's administrator can turn off access to **Owned by me** for Data Studio Pro subscriptions. You can create content in your **Sandbox** or a team workspace instead.
