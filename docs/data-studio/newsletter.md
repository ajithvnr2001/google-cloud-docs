#  Data Studio newsletter Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

The monthly Data Studio newsletter delivers feature updates, tips, and best practices to your email inbox. You'll also learn how organizations around the world are using Data Studio.

## Sign up for the newsletter

  1. [Sign in to Data Studio.](https://datastudio.google.com)
  2. In the upper right, click **User Settings** settings.
  3. In the **Marketing preferences** tab, under **Tips and recommendations** , click **Yes**.
  4. At the bottom of the dialog, click **Save**.
