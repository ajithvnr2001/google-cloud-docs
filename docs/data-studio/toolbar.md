#  Toolbar Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

The section of the report editor that appears near the top of the report editor, under the menus.

The toolbar lets you quickly perform actions such as navigating between pages, adding new pages, and adding data and components to your report.

You can also find most of the available toolbar actions in the menus. For example, the **View** menu contains the zoom feature, the **Insert** menu contains the component addition options, and the **Page** menu contains the page management controls.
