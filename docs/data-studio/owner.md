#  Owner Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

A person who has complete control of a Data Studio asset.

You are the owner of all reports, data sources, and explorations you create. Owners have full [permissions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/ways-to-share-your-reports) to these assets. The owner's name is listed under the Owner column in the Data Studio _Home_ page. You can also see the owner listed in the _Advanced_ section of the _Share with others_ dialog.

Owners can transfer ownership of reports and data sources to another person by following [these directions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/transfer-ownership).
