#  About team workspaces Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

**Data Studio Pro feature**

The features and functionality described in this page are available to Data Studio Pro users.

[Get Data Studio Pro.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/about-pro)

A team workspace is a shared location for team collaboration. Team workspaces let Data Studio Pro users share and manage access to Data Studio assets at scale.

A team workspace can be shared with individuals or with Google Groups users. To share a team workspace, add members and assign roles to them. Members of a team workspace automatically have access to the content within that team workspace.

Team workspaces can be useful in the following scenarios:

  * You can use team workspaces and folders in team workspaces to onboard a group of new hires by granting all of them access to your team content at once, instead of having to grant access individually.
  * You can use team workspaces to manage access to environments like prod and pre-prod, which helps you to ensure that the right teams have granular access and sharing permissions.



## Access team workspaces

To use team workspaces, you'll need the following:

  * Your organization must purchase Data Studio Pro.
  * Your organization's Google Cloud administrator must [link Data Studio to a Google Cloud project](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/link-pro-to-a-google-cloud-project-mau-version).



Once your organization has purchased Data Studio Pro and linked a Google Cloud project, you will be able to access existing team workspaces from the left navigation. For instructions for creating a new team workspace in a project that you belong to, see the [Create, edit, and delete team workspaces](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/create-edit-and-delete-team-workspaces) article.

To access an existing team workspace, follow these steps:

  1. [Sign in to Data Studio.](https://datastudio.google.com)
  2. In the left navigation, expand the listing for your project, or click the project ID to open the content list.
  3. From the list, choose the existing team workspace that you want to access.



![Data Studio left navigation showing a Pro project named looker-studio-pro, a highlighted team workspace named A workspace, and Sandbox. Owned by me is also available.](https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/data-studio/images/a-workspace-w-owned-by-me.png)

You can also organize and manage assets in folders and subfolders in team workspaces. See the [Organize assets with folders in team workspaces](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/organize-assets-with-folders-in-team-workspaces) page for more information about folders in team workspaces.

## Difference between a Sandbox and a team workspace

In Data Studio, [Owned by me](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/owned-by-me) includes assets that you create or own. As a Data Studio Pro subscriber, your organization's administrator can turn off access to **Owned by me**. You can create content in your [Sandbox](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/sandbox) or in a team workspace instead.

A Sandbox is a location that can be used to develop personal content that includes Data Studio Pro features. A Sandbox includes assets that you have created or that have been transferred to your ownership.

**Note:** Content within your Sandbox is not visible to other users unless you share it or move it into a team workspace. However, organization administrators and project owners can see the content within your Sandbox.

### Team workspace roles

In a Sandbox, in **Owned by me** , or in **Shared with me** , there are three basic roles that govern what you can do with the assets that you have access to:

  * **Viewers** can view the asset, see the data, and interact with viewer-level controls.
  * **Editors** can modify the asset.
  * **Owners** have complete control over the asset.



Team workspace roles grant additional permissions to all assets in a workspace. You can grant the following roles at the team workspace level:

  * **Viewers** can view all assets and folders in the workspace. All members of a team workspace have the capabilities that are provided by the Viewer role.
  * **Contributors** can view and modify all the assets in the workspace.
  * **Content managers** can add members to and remove members from the workspace.
  * **Managers** have complete control over the assets in the workspace and can add, remove, and define roles for all the members of the workspace.



Folder permissions are inherited from a user's team workspace role.

Learn more about [roles and permissions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/roles-and-permissions). Learn more about [organizing assets in folders](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/organize-assets-with-folders-in-team-workspaces).
