#  Connect to Firebase (via BigQuery) Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Firebase is Google's web and mobile app analytics platform. Firebase lets you share users, data, and analytics across platforms, meaning your users have the same experience whatever device they're on and you can manage performance all in one place.

[Learn more about Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/?gclid=Cj0KEQiApqTCBRC-977Hi9Ov8pkBEiQA5B_ipQcC0HWqCSBpx0oeSKO57rZGxmQZFKz7bwD5ZBzPk-8aAh1C8P8HAQ).

You can use Data Studio to turn your raw Firebase Analytics data into informative reports that are quick to read, quick to share, and fully customizable. It does this by leveraging its BigQuery connector: after you [link your Firebase app to BigQuery](https://support.google.com/firebase/answer/6318765#zippy=%2Cin-this-article), your BigQuery dataset can be visualized beautifully in Data Studio.

To create BigQuery data sources in Data Studio, refer to the [BigQuery connector](https://docs.cloud.google.com/data-studio/connect-to-google-bigquery) article.
