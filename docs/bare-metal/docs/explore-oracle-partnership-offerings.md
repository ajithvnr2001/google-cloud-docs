# Explore Oracle and Google Cloud partnership offerings

[Oracle and Google Cloud have partnered](https://cloud.google.com/solutions/oracle) to provide a seamless multicloud experience, letting you run Oracle database services natively within the Google Cloud.

The Oracle and Google Cloud partnership delivers a fully integrated experience for deploying and managing Oracle Database instances directly in Google Cloud. Our key offering [Oracle Database@Google Cloud](https://docs.cloud.google.com/oracle/database/docs/overview) brings Oracle database services, including Oracle Exadata Database Service and Oracle Autonomous Database, to Google Cloud.

This collaboration lets you do the following:

  * Run Oracle database workloads with low latency and high performance.
  * Simplify operations with a single, consolidated bill for both Oracle and Google Cloud services.
  * Innovate by combining your Oracle data with Google's AI and analytics tools, such as Gemini and Vertex AI.



For a majority of Oracle deployments, our new strategic partnership offering, [Oracle Database@Google Cloud](https://docs.cloud.google.com/oracle/database/docs/overview), provides a more integrated and efficient experience than Bare Metal Solution. Oracle Database@Google Cloud offers simplified management and direct access to Google Cloud's AI and analytics tools. For more information, discuss your requirements with your Google Cloud account team to find the ideal solution for your business.

For more information about Oracle and Google Cloud partnership offerings and how to get started, see [Oracle on Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/solutions/oracle).
