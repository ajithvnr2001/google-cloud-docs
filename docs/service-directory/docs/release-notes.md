## August 03, 2022

Feature

[Configuring an internal TCP/UDP load balancer](https://docs.cloud.google.com/service-directory/docs/configuring-ilb-in-sd) and [network load balancer](https://docs.cloud.google.com/service-directory/docs/configuring-netlb-in-sd) in Service Directory is available in [**GA**](https://cloud.google.com/products/#product-launch-stages).

## February 15, 2022

Feature

[Service Directory integration with Traffic Director](https://docs.cloud.google.com/service-directory/docs/sd-td-overview) is available in [**Preview**](https://cloud.google.com/products/#product-launch-stages) . 

After you register a service with Service Directory, the integration makes services in the service registry available to the applications in your mesh and to gateways configured by Traffic Director. Your service mesh and self- managed gateways can then send traffic to these services

## January 24, 2022

Feature

[Private network access in Service Directory](https://docs.cloud.google.com/service-directory/docs/private-network-access-overview) is available in [**GA**](https://cloud.google.com/products/#product-launch-stages). 

Private network access helps supported Google Cloud services to connect directly to VPC networks.
