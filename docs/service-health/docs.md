# Personalized Service Health documentation

[Read product documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/service-health/docs/overview)

Personalized Service Health is a one-stop view of Google Cloud [service health events](https://docs.cloud.google.com/service-health/docs/overview#service_health_event), where you can manage active events and review past events. It identifies how Google Cloud service health events are relevant to your project and filters out events that are not relevant. Personalized Service Health provides the following options to access service health events: 

  * **View active and past Google Cloud incidents** that are relevant to your project in the Service Health dashboard in the Google Cloud console.
  * **Request service health events using an API** on a per-project or per-organization basis.
  * **Configure alerts or export logs through Cloud Logging** across channels such as [Cloud Monitoring channels](https://cloud.google.com/monitoring/support/notification-options#creating_channels) including email, Pub/Sub, webhooks, Slack, and PagerDuty.
