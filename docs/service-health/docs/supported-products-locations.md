## Locations

This section contains a list of public Google Cloud locations. Some Google Cloud products may not be available in the given location or may not use all location types. For example, zonal service may not use multi-regions.

See [Google Cloud product availability by locations](https://cloud.google.com/about/locations) for up-to-date information.

### Regions

Format: `continent-subcontinent#`

Examples:

`asia-east1` | `australia-southeast2` | `europe-west8` | `us-east4`  
---|---|---|---  
`asia-east2` | `europe-central2` | `europe-west9` | `us-east5`  
`asia-northeast1` | `europe-north1` | `me-west1` | `us-east7`  
`asia-northeast2` | `europe-southwest1` | `northamerica-northeast1` | `us-south1`  
`asia-northeast3` | `europe-west1` | `northamerica-northeast2` | `us-west1`  
`asia-south1` | `europe-west2` | `southamerica-east1` | `us-west2`  
`asia-south2` | `europe-west3` | `southamerica-west1` | `us-west3`  
`asia-southeast1` | `europe-west4` | `us-central1` | `us-west4`  
`asia-southeast2` | `europe-west5` | `us-central2` |   
`australia-southeast1` | `europe-west6` | `us-east1` |   
  
### Zones

Format: `continent-subcontinent#-letter`

Examples:

`asia-east1-a`  
---  
`asia-east1-b`  
`asia-east1-c`  
`asia-east2-a`  
`asia-east2-b`  
`asia-east2-c`  
  
### Multi-regions

Format: `multiregion` or `multiregion#`

Examples:

`asia` | `eur6` | `nam13` | `nam8`  
---|---|---|---  
`asia1` | `europe` | `nam3` | `nam9`  
`eu` | `nam-eur-asia1` | `nam4` | `us`  
`eur3` | `nam10` | `nam5` |   
`eur4` | `nam11` | `nam6` |   
`eur5` | `nam12` | `nam7` |
