#  Manage emerging incidents Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Personalized Service Health publishes emerging incidents for specific Google Cloud products.

These incidents are detected by Google and under early investigation by Google engineers. They appear as **Emerging** in the Service Health dashboard until they are confirmed.

Some ways to handle emerging incidents are the following: 

  * To be notified of emerging incidents as early as possible, include them in your alert policies.
  * To wait for Google to confirm an incident, omit these incidents from alerts. See an [example alert condition](https://docs.cloud.google.com/service-health/docs/example-alerting-policies#alert_on_any_update_on_confirmed_incidents) you can use.



## Supported Google Cloud products

See the list of [supported products](https://docs.cloud.google.com/service-health/docs/supported-products-locations), which indicate the Google Cloud products that support emerging incidents.
