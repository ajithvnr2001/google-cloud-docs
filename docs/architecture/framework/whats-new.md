#  What's new in the Well-Architected Framework Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Last reviewed 2026-10-13 UTC

This document lists significant changes to the Google Cloud Well-Architected Framework.

## October 13, 2026

  * Added information about [Well-Architected Framework agent skills](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework#agent-skills).



## October 2, 2026

  * Operational excellence pillar: Updated the [Plan and manage capacity](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/operational-excellence/operational-readiness-and-performance-using-cloudops#plan_and_manage_capacity) recommendation with the following guidance: 
    * Design for flexibility and efficiency by using future reservations and Flex-start VMs.
    * Configure managed instance groups (MIGs) with instance and location flexibility.



## September 11, 2026

  * [AI and ML perspective](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/ai-ml): 
    * Replaced references to deprecated Vertex Explainable AI across the perspective with guidance to use tabular explanations and general explainability techniques.
    * Updated the [Optimize self-managed serving](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/ai-ml/performance-optimization#optimize-self-managed-serving) section in the performance optimization pillar to recommend multi-host inferencing using Pathways.



## August 18, 2026

  * [Cost optimization pillar](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/cost-optimization): Updated [Align cloud spending with business value](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/cost-optimization/align-cloud-spending-business-value#promote_a_value-driven_and_tco-aware_mindset) with recommendations to adopt unit economics to measure unit margins and balance cloud spending against revenue or business metrics.



## August 12, 2026

  * [Security, privacy, and compliance pillar](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/security): Updated [Implement zero trust](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/security/implement-zero-trust#secure_your_network) with expanded guidance on the following topics: 
    * Segmenting networks by using multiple Virtual Private Cloud (VPC) networks and Shared VPC.
    * Preventing data exfiltration by using VPC Service Controls and strict egress firewall rules.



## July 13, 2026

  * [Reliability pillar](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/reliability): Added information in [Perform testing for recovery from failures](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/reliability/perform-testing-for-recovery-from-failures#simulate_failure_scenarios) about how Google Cloud conducts periodic zone and region disconnect testing in a production-equivalent environment.



## July 6, 2026

  * [Financial services (FS) perspective](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/fsi): Updated [FS perspective: Cost optimization](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/fsi/cost-optimization#take_advantage_of_discounts) to note that Google Cloud enables resource-based committed use discount (CUD) sharing across projects by default for most Cloud Billing accounts.



## June 15, 2026

  * [Sustainability pillar](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/sustainability): Updated [Continuously measure and improve sustainability](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/sustainability/continuously-measure-improve) with the following guidance: 
    * Align resource hierarchy and annotations with business units before exporting Carbon Footprint data.
    * Validate cost-based carbon assumptions against location-based and market-based metrics.
    * Track the removal of unattended projects through IT service management (ITSM) workflows.
    * Integrate monthly BigQuery carbon exports into FinOps dashboards such as Looker.



## May 4, 2026

  * [AI and ML perspective](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/ai-ml): Updated [AI and ML perspective: Operational excellence](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/ai-ml/operational-excellence#prepare_for_peak_events) to recommend Memorystore for Valkey, Memorystore for Redis, and Memorystore for Redis Cluster for caching frequently accessed data, replacing deprecated Memorystore for Memcached.



## February 13, 2026

  * [AI and ML perspective: Performance optimization](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/ai-ml/performance-optimization): Major update to expand the performance-optimization recommendations.



## January 28, 2026

  * [Sustainability pillar](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/sustainability): Expanded the sustainability guidance as a full-fledged pillar.



## November 26, 2025

  * [AI and ML perspective: Security](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/ai-ml/security): Major update to expand the security recommendations in the AI and ML perspective.



## August 7, 2025

  * [AI and ML perspective: Reliability](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/ai-ml/reliability): Major update to expand the reliability recommendations in the AI and ML perspective.



## July 28, 2025

  * Added the [Financial services industry (FSI) perspective](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/fsi). These documents describe principles and recommendations that are specific to FSI, aligned to each pillar of the Architecture Framework.



## May 28, 2025

  * [AI and ML perspective: Cost optimization](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/ai-ml/cost-optimization): Major update to expand the cost optimization recommendations in the AI and ML perspective.



## April 28, 2025

  * [AI and ML perspective: Operational excellence](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/ai-ml/operational-excellence): Major update to expand the operational excellence recommendations in the AI and ML perspective.



## February 5, 2025

  * [Security, privacy, and compliance pillar](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/security): Major update to align the recommendations with core principles of security, privacy, and compliance.



## December 30, 2024

  * [Reliability pillar](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/reliability): Major update to align the recommendations with core principles of reliability.



## December 6, 2024

  * [Performance optimization pillar](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/performance-optimization): Major update to align the recommendations with core principles of performance optimization.



## October 31, 2024

  * [Operational excellence pillar](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/operational-excellence): Major update to align the recommendations with core principles of operational excellence.



## October 11, 2024

  * Added the [AI and ML perspective](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/perspectives/ai-ml). These documents describe principles and recommendations that are specific to AI and ML, aligned to each pillar of the Architecture Framework.



## September 25, 2024

  * [Cost optimization pillar](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/cost-optimization): Major update to align the recommendations with core principles of cost optimization.



## August 27, 2024

  * The System Design category of the Architecture Framework has been removed. The guidance in this category is available in other Google Cloud documentation.



## June 4, 2024

  * Cost optimization category: 
    * Added information about [monitoring](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/cost-optimization/databases#monitor_usage_5) the cost of Dataflow resources.
    * Add recommendations for [optimizing](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/cost-optimization/databases#optimize_resources_5) cost by using the at-least-once streaming mode and the dynamic thread scaling feature.



## May 31, 2024

  * Cost optimization category: 
    * Added information about the [FinOps hub](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/cost-optimization/finops#finops-hub).



## May 30, 2024

  * System design category: 
    * Added a new design principle, [Design for change](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/system-design/principles#design_for_change).



## May 23, 2024

  * Cost optimization category: 
    * Added guidance in [Optimize cost: Compute, containers, and serverless](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/cost-optimization/compute#reclaim_idle_resources) about reducing cost by suspending idle Compute Engine VMs .



## May 16, 2024

  * System design category: 
    * Added information about the Batch service in [Choose and manage compute](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/system-design/compute#platform).



## May 9, 2024

  * Cost optimization category: 
    * Updated guidance about Dataflow Shuffle in [Optimize cost: Databases and smart analytics](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/cost-optimization/databases#dataflow).



## April 22, 2024

  * Cost optimization category: 
    * Added a recommendation about considering storage cost when configuring the log-retention period in [Optimize cost: Cloud operations](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/cost-optimization/cloudops#logging).



## March 29, 2024

  * Reliability category: 
    * Reorganized the guidance related to [SLOs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/reliability/choose-slos) and [SLIs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/reliability/choose-slis) to improve usability and alignment with SRE guidance.



## January 4, 2024

  * Performance optimization category: 
    * Updated the guidance for [Network Service Tiers](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/performance-optimization/networking).



## November 28, 2023

  * Reliability category: 
    * Reorganized the content to improve readability and consistency:
    * [Define SLOs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/reliability/defining-SLOs): Moved "Terminology" section to new page: [Terminology](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/reliability/terminology)
    * [Adopt SLOs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/reliability/adopting-slos): Moved "SLOs and Alerts" section to new page: [SLOs and Alerts](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/reliability/slo-and-alerts)



## November 9, 2023

  * System design category: 
    * Added guidance to help cloud architects to [choose deployment archetypes](https://docs.cloud.google.com/architecture/framework/system-design/archetypes) for workloads in Google Cloud.
