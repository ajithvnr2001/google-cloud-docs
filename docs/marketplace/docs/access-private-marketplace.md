#  Access Google Cloud Private Marketplace Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Google Cloud Private Marketplace lets you deploy pre-approved products that run on Google Cloud. Your organization's administrators curate products available on Google Cloud Marketplace that meet your organization's internal procurement standards, and then share the products with you.

Your membership in an organization, folder, or project determines which products are available for your use in Google Cloud Private Marketplace.

If you don't see products that you expect to have access to from within your project, see [Troubleshoot Google Cloud Private Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/governance/troubleshoot-private-marketplace).

## Deploy and maintain products

To deploy and maintain products from Private Marketplace, follow the [same steps](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/manage-deployments) that you would for products from the public marketplace.

## Request products that you want to use

If your organization's administrators have turned on Cloud Marketplace Product Requests, you can [ask your administrators](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/governance/requesting-procurement) to review and approve Google Cloud Marketplace products and offer them in your organization's private marketplace.

**Note:** You can't request certain types of products, such as products with private pricing, or Google products.
