#  Add products to a collection Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

After you've created a collection, you can add additional products to it.

**Note:** Make sure that Google Cloud Private Marketplace supports the products that you want to add to your collection. For details, see [Supported products](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/governance/private-marketplace#supported-products).

## Add products

To add products to a collection that you've already created, complete the following steps:

  1. Ensure that you have the [required IAM roles](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/governance/private-marketplace#permissions).

  2. In the Cloud Marketplace resource selector, select your organization.

  3. In the navigation menu, click **Marketplace Governance**.

[Go to Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace)

  4. In the navigation menu, click **Private Marketplace**.

  5. Select the collection that you want to add products to.

  6. In **Add products** , click **\+ Add**.

  7. In the field that you see, enter the URL of the product's product details page, and then click **Add**. Cloud Marketplace retrieves the product's information.

  8. To add the product to the collection, click **Save**.

  9. Continue adding products until your collection is complete.

  10. Click **Save** to save your changes to your collection.
