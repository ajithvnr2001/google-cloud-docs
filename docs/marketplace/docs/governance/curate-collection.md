#  Create a collection Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Before you [turn on Google Cloud Private Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/governance/enable-private-marketplace), you must [create and publish a default collection](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/governance/create-default-collection), called `organization-collection`. This page describes how to create additional collections.

If you want to create additional collections, choose which products you want to include in those collections and understand how you want to share them. You can share a collection with specific folders or projects. When users access your private marketplace from projects that you've shared a collection with, or projects that belong to folders or organizations that you've shared a collection with, they can view and access the products that you added to that collection within Private Marketplace.

## Before you begin

  * Ensure that you have the [required IAM roles](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/governance/private-marketplace#permissions).



## Create a collection

To create additional collections, complete the following steps:

  1. In Cloud Marketplace, click **Marketplace Governance**.

[Go to Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace)

  2. On the **Private Marketplace** page, click **Create collection**.

  3. Enter your collection information. The **Name** must be unique from other collections in your organization.

The **Name** and **Details** are visible to collection administrators.

  4. In **Add products** , click **\+ Add**.

  5. In the field that you see, enter the URL of the product's product details page, and then click **Add**. Cloud Marketplace retrieves the product's information.

  6. To add the product to the collection, click **Save**.

  7. Continue adding products until your collection is complete.

  8. You can [share the collection](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/governance/share-collection), or click **Create** to create the collection without sharing it. If you create a collection without sharing it, you can share it later.
