When you [use Google Cloud Private Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/access-private-marketplace), your membership in an organization, folder, or project determines which products are available to you.

If you don't see products that you expect to have access from within your project, take the following steps to troubleshoot your access:

  1. Verify that you've selected the correct project.

  2. If [Marketplace User Access Restrictions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/governance/strict-user-access) is turned on for your organization, verify that you've been granted the [required permissions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/governance/strict-user-access#required-permissions) to access Google Cloud Marketplace.
