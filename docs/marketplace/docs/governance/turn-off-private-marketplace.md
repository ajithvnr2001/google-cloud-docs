#  Turn off Private Marketplace Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

This page describes how to turn off Google Cloud Private Marketplace for your organization's users.

After you turn off Private Marketplace, your organization's users see the public marketplace as their default landing experience when they access Google Cloud Marketplace.

Turning off Private Marketplace doesn't alter any existing collections that you previously created. While Private Marketplace is turned off, governance administrators can continue to manage those collections, or to create new collections. Private Marketplace preserves any changes that administrators make to collections or to other Private Marketplace settings while Private Marketplace is turned off. When you [turn Private Marketplace back on](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/governance/enable-private-marketplace) for your organization, Private Marketplace applies these settings.

To turn off Private Marketplace for your organization, complete the following steps:

  1. Ensure that you have the [required IAM roles](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/governance/private-marketplace#permissions).

  2. In Cloud Marketplace, click **Marketplace governance**.

[Go to Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace)

  3. Click the **Governance settings** tab.

  4. Under **Private Marketplace** , click the toggle to turn off the Private Marketplace experience.
