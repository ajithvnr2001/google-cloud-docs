You can get AI models from Google Cloud Marketplace and use them with Model Garden.

## Before you begin

  * Verify that you have the required Identity and Access Management (IAM) roles to purchase or deploy products. For details, see [IAM roles for purchasing and managing products](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/access-control#roles_for_purchasing_and_managing_products).
  * Verify that you're in the Google Cloud project in which you want to purchase or deploy the product.



## Get AI models from Cloud Marketplace

To get AI models from Cloud Marketplace, complete the following steps:

  1. In Cloud Marketplace, visit the product page for the AI model that you want to get.

  2. From the product page, click **Enable**.

  3. For some AI models, you might be asked to complete a form that appears by providing details of your intended usage. If you encounter a form, complete the required fields and click **Next**.

  4. Review the relevant terms and agreements for the AI model that appear as part of the purchasing or deployment flow.

**Note:** The terms and agreements that you see when you purchase or deploy a product represent the current versions at that time. If you previously viewed the terms and agreements from an AI model's **Product details** page, we recommend that you verify the current terms and agreements when you accept them during purchase or deployment.
  5. To accept the relevant terms and agreements and finish enabling the AI model, click **Agree**.




### Regionalized pricing for AI models

Some AI models on Cloud Marketplace have pricing that changes depending on where you use the AI model. In these cases, Cloud Marketplace determines the pricing based on the location of the endpoint to which you're sending your requests.

If an AI model's pricing varies based on location, you can see the pricing by location in the **Pricing** section of the AI model's Cloud Marketplace listing. To view pricing for different locations, use the **Location** drop-down.

If you're using Google Cloud across multiple regions, verify that you're sending your requests to an endpoint in your preferred location.

**Note** : If you have purchased a Gemini Enterprise Flexible Savings Plans (FSP), eligible third-party AI models will drawn against your organization's [Flexible Savings Plans](https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/cuds-flexible-savings-plans#third-party-models-private-offers). 

## Use AI models with Vertex AI

After you've enabled an AI model, complete the following steps to use it in Model Garden:

  1. In Cloud Marketplace, visit the product page for the AI model that you want to use.

  2. From the AI model's product page, click **Manage on Vertex AI**.




For more information about using Model Garden, visit the [Model Garden documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/docs/start/explore-models).

## What's next

For information about managing your usage of AI models, see [Manage AI models](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/manage-ai-models).
