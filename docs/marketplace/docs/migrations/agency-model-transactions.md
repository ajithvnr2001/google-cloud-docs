#  Migration to transacting in the agency model Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Beginning in October 2023, Google Cloud Marketplace supports the agency transaction model. The agency model is designed to support increased flexibility and control for partners as Google continues to develop Cloud Marketplace.

Under the agency model, Google acts as an agent for the vendor who's offering the product through Cloud Marketplace. Google facilitates and processes the transaction, but the vendor of the product is the Merchant of Record for the transaction. For a comparison of the available transaction models, see [Transaction models](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/transaction-models).

If you make a transaction through Cloud Marketplace that meets the requirements of the agency model (described later in this document), Cloud Marketplace uses the agency model for that transaction. Otherwise, Cloud Marketplace uses the Merchant of Record model for that transaction. Cloud Marketplace notifies you during the transaction process whether it's using the agency model or the Merchant of Record model for that transaction.

**Note:** Starting around February 1, 2025, for customers in the United Kingdom, France, or Germany who make transactions under the agency model, Google invoices purchases of Cloud Marketplace products offered by ISVs separately from purchases of first-party Google products and usage of Google Cloud services. Starting June 1, 2025, this also applies to customers in Israel who make transactions under the agency model. Starting June 1, 2026, this applies to customers in Switzerland. Starting July 1, 2026, this applies to customers in Hong Kong. For details, see [Migration to split invoicing for agency model transactions](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/migrations/split-invoicing-for-agency).

## Requirements to transact through the agency model

For a transaction to occur through the agency model, the following requirements must be met:

  * Both you and the vendor who's offering the product must be located in a region that Cloud Marketplace supports for the agency model. For a list of supported regions, visit [Agency jurisdictions](https://cloud.google.com/terms/marketplace-agency-jurisdictions).

  * Both you and the customer must have verified your identity to Google, if you were requested to by Google. For more information, visit [Verify your identity](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/verify-your-identity).




## Additional information

For answers to frequently asked questions (FAQ), see the external [Introduction to Agency model PDF](https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/google_cloud_marketplace_agency_model_customer_faq.pdf).
