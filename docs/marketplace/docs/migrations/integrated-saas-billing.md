#  Migrating subscriptions to the integrated software as a service (SaaS) billing platform Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

On or after **June 13, 2022** , your integrated software as a service (SaaS) subscriptions will be migrated to a new billing platform within Google Cloud Marketplace.

The migration happens automatically, so you aren't required to take any action.

## What to expect during the migration to integrated SaaS billing

During the migration, you might see the following changes when managing your subscriptions in the Google Cloud console:

  * A new Google Cloud project in your [Cloud Billing reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/reports). The new Google Cloud project has an auto-generated name and ID. After the migration is complete, the current project for your subscription will be replaced by the new project.

  * An active and inactive order for your subscription on the **Your Orders** page. To modify your subscription, use the active order.
