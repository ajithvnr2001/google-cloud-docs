Google Cloud Marketplace uses AI-powered features to help you discover software packages, services, and AI agents for your specific business needs more effectively. These features use Google's machine learning capabilities to understand your goals and identify relevant products for your use cases.

## AI-powered search

Cloud Marketplace includes an AI-powered search experience to help you discover products. Unlike conventional keyword search, the AI-powered search experience lets you describe your goals, use cases, or technical problems using natural language. The system then identifies relevant products and provides a summarized explanation of how they meet your requirements.

You can access these features from the search bar on the [Cloud Marketplace page](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace) in the Google Cloud console.

The AI-powered search experience includes several key features, such as the following:

  * **Instant Search** : As you type, the search bar instantly displays autocomplete suggestions, relevant categories, and specific products or partners. You can select an instant match to navigate directly to it.
  * **AI Overviews** : When you run a standard search, an AI Overview summarizes how the marketplace inventory addresses your query. This includes suggested products and providers with a summary of why each matches your intent, along with follow-up buttons that let you jump directly into AI Mode.
  * **AI Mode** : For complex inquiries, you can engage in a conversation in AI Mode. Enter AI Mode by clicking **AI Mode** in the search bar, clicking **Continue the conversation** from an AI Overview, or selecting any of the follow-up buttons in an AI Overview. This dedicated interface supports multi-turn conversations, letting you ask successive follow-up questions and refine your search iteratively. To restart a conversation, reload the page in AI Mode.



For more details on using AI-powered search, see [Discover products with AI search](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/discover-products-ai-search).

## AI agent discovery

Cloud Marketplace includes a dedicated page for AI agents. AI agents are software systems that use AI to pursue goals and complete tasks on behalf of users. You can use AI-powered search, in combination with filters for industry and business function, to find AI agents that can automate tasks, answer questions, or provide specialized domain knowledge for your business needs.

For more information, see [Explore AI agents on Cloud Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/explore-ai-agents).

## Access to AI models

You can get AI models from Cloud Marketplace and use them with Model Garden. Model Garden is a platform for discovering, testing, and deploying Google and partner AI models on Vertex AI. Cloud Marketplace hosts a wide variety of AI models, including foundation models and models trained for specific tasks, that you can enable for use in your projects.

For more information, see [Get AI models from Cloud Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/enable-ai-models).
