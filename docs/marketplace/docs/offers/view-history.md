#  View the history of an offer Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

To view the history of an offer, you must have the [Consumer Procurement Events Viewer](https://console.cloud.google.com/iam-admin/roles/details/roles%3Cconsumerprocurement.eventsViewer) (`roles/consumerprocurement.eventsViewer`) role for your Google Cloud project. If you don't have the role, request access from a Project Owner (`roles/owner`).

To view a record of key events for an offer that you've accepted, visit the offer's purchase page. In the purchase page, you see a **Key Events** widget that lists a historical record of statuses for that offer.

To download a copy of the offer at a particular step, click **Download PDF** from that particular step.
