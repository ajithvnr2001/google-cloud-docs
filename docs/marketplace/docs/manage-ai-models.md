#  Manage AI models Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

You use Model Garden to manage the AI models that you get from Google Cloud Marketplace. For information about getting AI models from Cloud Marketplace, see [Get AI models from Google Cloud Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/enable-ai-models).

## Manage AI models on Vertex AI

To manage AI models that you've procured from Cloud Marketplace, complete the following steps:

  1. In Cloud Marketplace, visit the product page for the AI model that you want to manage.

  2. From the AI model's product page, click **Manage on Vertex AI**.
