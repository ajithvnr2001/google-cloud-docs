#  Explore AI agents on Cloud Marketplace Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Google Cloud Marketplace includes a dedicated page for AI agents, allowing you to discover, evaluate, and deploy conversational agents tailored to your business needs.

This page explains how to leverage the new AI search specifically for finding AI agents, how to browse the available listings, and how to use the available filters.

## The dedicated AI agents page

The AI agents page on [Cloud Marketplace](https://console.cloud.google.com/marketplace) provides a curated view of the AI agents available for deployment. It highlights agents that can automate tasks, answer questions, or provide specialized domain knowledge.

## Leverage AI search for finding AI agents

You can use the conversational AI search experience to find AI agents that solve specific business problems. For more details on using the AI search experience, see [Discover products with AI search](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/discover-products-ai-search).

To find an AI agent using AI search:

  1. In the search bar, enter a query that describes the task.
  2. In the **AI Overview** section of the results, review the suggested AI agents and their descriptions. To enter AI Mode for a multi-turn conversation that further narrows your results, click follow-up buttons in the AI Overview. AI Mode multi-turn conversations are only available to logged-in users.



## Browse and evaluate AI agent listings

When you search for AI agents or browse the dedicated page, you can evaluate the available options using the provided product cards and AI-generated summaries.

  1. Review the **AI Overview** or engage in a multi-turn conversation in AI Mode for a summary of how the suggested AI agents address your query. To launch AI Mode with contextual follow-up inquiries, click follow-up buttons in the AI Overview.
  2. Examine the product cards for each AI agent, which display key metadata, such as the provider and a brief description.
  3. Click the link to an AI agent's details page to review its features, pricing, and deployment options.



### Use filters for AI agents

To help you refine your search, you can use the horizontal filters on the search page. When you apply filters, both the standard search results and the AI Overviews respect your active filters.

For AI agents, you can use the following new and updated filters to narrow down the available options:

  * **Category** : Filter AI agents by their general classification or domain.
  * **Industry** : Filter AI agents that are designed or specialized for specific industries, such as healthcare, finance, or retail.
  * **Business function** : Filter AI agents based on the organizational function they serve, such as customer support, sales, marketing, or human resources.
  * **Compatibility** : Filter AI agents based on their compatibility, for example A2A and Gemini Enterprise.



By combining the conversational AI search with these filters, you can quickly find the most relevant AI agents for your specific use cases.
