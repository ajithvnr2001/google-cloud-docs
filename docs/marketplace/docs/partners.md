# Google Cloud Marketplace Partners documentation

[Read product documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/get-started)

Google Cloud Marketplace is one of the fastest ways for your customers to get started on Google Cloud. 

You can offer production-ready stacks, products, datasets, and services to accelerate development, so that your users spend less time installing your software. You can offer products with pricing features that you're used to, such as subscriptions, pay-as-you-go, and custom pricing. You can also receive reports that enable you to track how your customers are interacting with your products and marketing campaigns. 

For most configurations, your customers receive one bill for all of your products and services, as well as the Google Cloud services that they use.
