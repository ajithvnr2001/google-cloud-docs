#  Offer professional services Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

You can use Google Cloud Marketplace to list and sell professional services that you offer to customers. These professional services must support existing commercial Cloud Marketplace products.

To sell your professional services to a customer, you (or a Google Cloud reseller) use _Private Offers_ to create a custom quote and send it to your customer.

You can offer professional services with subscription plans, usage-based pricing, or a combination of pricing models.

**Note:** Customers can't buy professional services directly from Cloud Marketplace in the way that they can buy most other types of Cloud Marketplace products. Customers can buy professional services only by accepting a private offer from you or from a reseller of Google Cloud products. If a customer wants to buy professional services that you offer, and you've [turned on sales lead management](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/enable-customer-contact), the customer can send you a message requesting more information.

## Before you begin

  * If you're new to offering software on Cloud Marketplace, [sign up to become a vendor](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offer-products#initiate-onboarding). 
  * For your first (and all subsequent) listings you must complete the [Cloud Marketplace Onboarding Validation Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSesllyRR-HvLFZthYA8Cv4nQRUTOthHGgs-76DY9o2IntUL3A/viewform). 
  * To get access to Producer Portal, complete the _Cloud Marketplace Project Info Form_ , provided by the Cloud Marketplace team. 



## Product-specific requirements

All products that you offer through Cloud Marketplace must comply with the Cloud Marketplace [listing requirements](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/get-started). If you offer professional services, you must meet the following requirements:

  * Cloud Marketplace must be able to use the agency transaction model for your private offers that include professional services products. For more details, see [Agency model requirements](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/transaction-models#agency-requirements).
  * You and your customer must both be located in the United States.
  * The professional services that you offer must link to at least one current commercial Cloud Marketplace product listing.

**Note:** Professional services are only available for third-party Cloud Marketplace product listings.
  * The professional services that you offer must fall under one of the following categories:

    * **Assessment** : Evaluation of the customer's current operating environment to find the right solutions for their organization.
    * **Implementation** : Help with configuration, setup, and deployment of third-party software.
    * **Premium support** : Access to guidance and assistance from experts, designed for the customer's needs.
    * **Managed services** : End-to-end environment management on the customer's behalf.
    * **Training** : Tailored workshops, programs, and educational tools provided by experts to help customers' employees learn best practices.
  * The professional services that you offer aren't specifically regulated under applicable laws, such as legal, pharmaceutical, financial investment, or accounting services.

  * The professional services that you offer must be restricted to remote delivery. On-site engagements, and any associated expenses (for example, travel or hospitality), are strictly prohibited.




## Overview of onboarding professional services

To offer professional services through Cloud Marketplace, use Producer Portal to create a professional services product listing for Cloud Marketplace. This involves the following steps:

  * [Create your product](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/professional-services/product-details) in Producer Portal.
  * [Add product details](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/professional-services/product-details) for your professional services product's Cloud Marketplace listing.
  * [Choose and submit your pricing model](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/professional-services/choose-pricing).
  * [Complete your technical integration](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/professional-services/technical-integration).
  * [Publish your product to Cloud Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/professional-services/publish).



After you click to publish your product, it might take up to two days for your product's listing to be publicly accessible in Cloud Marketplace.

While your product's listing is publicly accessible in Cloud Marketplace, for customers to be able to purchase your professional services, you (or a reseller) must [create private offers](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers) for your customers.

## Create your workspace for Cloud Marketplace products

We recommend that you create a new project in the [Google Cloud console](https://console.cloud.google.com/) primarily for your Cloud Marketplace products. For the project ID, use the format `PARTNER_NAME-public`. For information about creating projects, see [Creating and managing projects](https://docs.cloud.google.com/resource-manager/docs/creating-managing-projects).

If you already have a project for your current Cloud Marketplace products, you can re-use that project.

## Submit information about your product

Before you begin setting up and integrating your product, you must complete the [Cloud Marketplace Project Info Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfddn4mwKnqtLNQ-m7IgRZ-bgTz4BOsrEDWCf3XBjc_ogKNnA/viewform).

After you complete the form, your Partner Engineer gives you access to Producer Portal.
