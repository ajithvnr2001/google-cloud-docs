#  Operational requirements for specific product types Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

If you want to offer products on Google Cloud Marketplace, then in addition to meeting the [listing requirements](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/get-started) for Cloud Marketplace, you must also maintain the following operational best practices, which depend on the types of products that you're offering:

  * [Operational requirements for Kubernetes apps](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/kubernetes#product-specific_requirements)
  * [Operational requirements for VM products](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/vm#product-specific_requirements)
  * [Operational requirements for SaaS products](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/integrated-saas#product-specific_requirements)
  * [Operational requirements for container image products](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/container#product-specific_requirements)
  * [Operational requirements for data products](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/data#requirements)
  * [Operational requirements for professional services](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/professional-services#requirements)
  * [Operational requirements for AI agents](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/ai-agents#a2a-onboard)



To use Private Offers to send custom offers for products, you must ensure that your offers meet the [operational requirements for private offers](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers#requirements)
