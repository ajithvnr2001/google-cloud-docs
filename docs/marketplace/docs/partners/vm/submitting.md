This page explains how to submit your product for review and publish the product to Google Cloud Marketplace.

## Before you begin

  * Make sure that you have reviewed and met all [the requirements for listing your product](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/vm#before-you-begin).
  * Make sure that you have reviewed and met all [the requirements for packaging your product](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/vm/build-vm-image).
  * [Configure payments](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/receive-payments) so that you can be paid for your product's usage.
  * [Test your product](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/vm/test-product) end-to-end.



## Complete reviews of your product's components

Before publishing your product, you must submit all of its components for review. When you submit your product, the Cloud Marketplace team reviews your VM, which includes verifying that your image deploys and uninstalls successfully, running unit tests, and scanning your VM image for vulnerabilities. You can submit the following reviews in any order to the Cloud Marketplace team:

  * Product details
  * Pricing
  * Deployment package

**Note:** Cloud Marketplace automatically performs the required steps to validate your deployment package after you upload it, and each time that you make a subsequent update to the package. After Cloud Marketplace has validated your package successfully, it marks this review as complete in Producer Portal. To ensure successful deployment for your customers, we recommend that you thoroughly test your product after validation is complete, before you submit your product for publication.



## Submit your product for final review

After Google has approved your product's component reviews, you must publish your product privately for final testing and review.

To publish your product privately, complete the following steps:

  1. Go to your product's **Overview** page in Producer Portal.

  2. Click **Publish**. This notifies Google that your product is ready for final review before publication.




### Make changes to your product after submitting it for review

If you find a mistake in your product, or if your contact at Google requests changes to it:

  1. Navigate to the page you'd like to make changes to.

  2. Click **Acknowledge** to acknowledge that you must re-submit the corresponding review for your product if you change it.

  3. Make any intended changes to your product.

  4. Re-submit the affected review for approval.




## Publish your product publicly

After all reviews have been approved, you can launch your product publicly within a few minutes by completing the following steps:

  1. Go to your product's **Overview** page in Producer Portal.

  2. Click **Enable public display**.

**Note:** If you don't see this button, verify that you've published your product privately. For more information, see Submit your product for final review, previously on this page.
  3. Click **Make public**.
