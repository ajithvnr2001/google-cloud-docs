#  Add your data product in Producer Portal Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

To offer a BigQuery sharing (formerly Analytics Hub) listing as a data product in Google Cloud Marketplace, you create a data product in Producer Portal that corresponds to the Sharing listing.

## Before you begin

  * To offer a data product through Cloud Marketplace, you must first create a listing in Sharing. To learn more, see [Create a listing](https://docs.cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs/analytics-hub-manage-listings#create_a_listing).
  * To get access to Producer Portal, you must have completed the _Cloud Marketplace Project Info Form_ , provided by the Google Cloud Marketplace team.



## Create your workspace for Cloud Marketplace products

We recommend that you create a new project in the [Google Cloud console](https://console.cloud.google.com/) primarily for your Cloud Marketplace products. For the project ID, use the format `PARTNER_NAME-public`. For information about creating projects, see [Creating and managing projects](https://docs.cloud.google.com/resource-manager/docs/creating-managing-projects).

If you already have a project for your current Cloud Marketplace products, you can re-use that project.

## Create your product in the portal

To add your product information, which includes your marketing information, pricing plan, and integrated data, use [Producer Portal](https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal).

**Note:** If you can't access Producer Portal, verify that you've selected the correct project. If you still can't access it, use the [Partner Support Desk](https://g.co/cloud/psd-partner) to request assistance, and include the word "Marketplace" in your description. For more information about Partner Support Desk, visit [Request assistance with Cloud Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/get-support).

To create your product, complete the following steps:

  1. Click **Add product**.

  2. Under **Product type** , select **Data**.

  3. For **Sharing listing** , enter the URL or the resource name of the Sharing listing that you want to use to create your Cloud Marketplace data product listing.

  4. Specify your product's **Product ID** and **Config ID**. These are unique identifiers for your product, used only for referencing your changes, and customers can't see them.

  5. Certify that your product complies with the Cloud Marketplace [listing requirements](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/get-started) and the [requirements for data products](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/data#requirements_for_data_products).

  6. Click **Create**. It might take a few seconds to create your product.




## What's next

After you add your product in Producer Portal, you create and prepare one or more _configs_ for publication.

  * Learn about [configs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/data#configs) for Cloud Marketplace data products.
  * Prepare a config for release by [adding product details](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/data/product-details) for your data product's Cloud Marketplace listing.
  * Review the available pricing options, and [select a pricing model](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/data/choose-pricing) for your config.
