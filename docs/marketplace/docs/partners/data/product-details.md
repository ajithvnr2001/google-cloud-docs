#  Add product details to a config Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

After you've [added your data product](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/data/add-product) in Producer Portal, you create one or more _configs_ and add product details to prepare them for publication, such as your product's title, icon, marketing information, and links to documentation.

**Note:** Configs are editable draft representations of how you plan to offer your product through Cloud Marketplace, including product and pricing details. For more details, see the [overview of configs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/data#configs).

## Add product details

To add product details to a config, use [Producer Portal](https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal).

**Note:** If you can't access Producer Portal, verify that you've selected the correct project. If you still can't access it, use the [Partner Support Desk](https://g.co/cloud/psd-partner) to request assistance, and include the word "Marketplace" in your description. For more information about Partner Support Desk, visit [Request assistance with Cloud Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/get-support).

In Producer Portal, select your data product and complete the following steps:

  1. A list of configs appears. Click the name of the config that you want to edit.

  2. Add the title, icon, and description for your data product's Cloud Marketplace listing.

  3. Select up to two categories from the **Category** drop-down. This helps customers to find your product under select categories or filters as they search Cloud Marketplace. 

  4. Under **Product overview and documentation** , add an overview and documentation for your product.




To save your progress at any point, or after completing this section, click **Save**.

## What's next

  * Review the available pricing options, and [select a pricing model](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/data/choose-pricing) for your config.
  * Submit your config for review and [publish your product](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/data/publish) to Cloud Marketplace.
