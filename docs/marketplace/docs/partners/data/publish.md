To publish your data product to Google Cloud Marketplace, you must submit a _config_ for review in Producer Portal. After you submit the config for review, you can't edit it.

**Note:** Configs are editable draft representations of how you plan to offer your product through Cloud Marketplace, including product and pricing details. For more details, see the [overview of configs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/data#configs).

If the config passes the review process, you can publish it to Cloud Marketplace. If the review fails, you can clone the config, modify it as needed, and then submit that new config for review.

After you click to publish your product, it might take up to two days for your product's listing to be publicly accessible in Cloud Marketplace.

## Before you begin

Before you submit a config for review, you must complete the following steps:

  * [Add product details](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/data/product-details) for your data product's Cloud Marketplace listing to the config.
  * [Add pricing information](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/data/choose-pricing) to the config.



## Submit a config for review

To submit your config for review, use Producer Portal. The direct link to Producer Portal is:
    
    
    https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal?project=YOUR_PUBLIC_PROJECT_ID
    

**Note:** If you don't see the link, or can't access the URL, verify that you've selected the correct project. If you still can't access the URL, use the [Partner Support Desk](https://g.co/cloud/psd-partner) to request assistance, and include the word "Marketplace" in your description. For more information about Partner Support Desk, visit [Request assistance with Cloud Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/get-support).

In Producer Portal, select your data product and complete the following steps:

  1. In the list of configs that you see, click the name of the config that you want to submit for review.

  2. Click **Submit for review**.




### Cancel the review of a config

If you submitted a config for review, but the review is still in-progress, you can cancel the review to return the config to an editable draft state. To cancel a review, complete the following steps:

  1. In Producer Portal, navigate to the config that you previously submitted for review.

  2. Under **More actions** , select **Cancel review**.




## Publish your data product

After your config passes the review process, you can publish the config (and your data product) to Cloud Marketplace.

To publish your data product, complete the following steps:

  1. In Producer Portal, navigate to the config that you previously submitted for review.

  2. Click **Publish**.


**Note:** After you click to publish your product, it might take up to two days for your data product's Cloud Marketplace listing to be publicly available.
