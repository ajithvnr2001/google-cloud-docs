#  Private offer plans for Cloud Marketplace resellers Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

If you resell Google Cloud Marketplace products offered by independent software vendors (ISVs), the ISVs can create _private offer plans_ that let you create private offers for your customers to buy their Cloud Marketplace products. Private offer plans include discounts that the ISV defines when creating the plan, which aren't visible to your customers. When you use a private offer plan to create a private offer for a customer, you specify how much of a discount to offer to the customer.

## Before you begin

  * Verify that you and your organization meet the [requirements for Google Cloud resellers](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/resellers/resell#requirements).
  * To access the Partner Sales Console, you and your team members must have the following Identity and Access Management (IAM) roles: 
    * [Billing Account Administrator](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/billing-access#billing.admin), or a custom role with both `billing.accounts.get` and `billing.accounts.update` permissions on your reseller Cloud Billing accounts. This role must be assigned at the [Organizational level](https://docs.cloud.google.com/resource-manager/docs/access-control-org).
    * The _Google Cloud Reseller Admin_ role in the Partner Sales Console. For more information, see [Assign Google Cloud reseller roles and permissions](https://support.google.com/channelservices/answer/9805524).



## Accept a private offer plan from an ISV

When an ISV creates a private offer plan, you receive a notification in the Partner Sales Console. For the private offer plan to take effect, you must use the Partner Sales Console to accept the private offer plan:

  1. Sign in to the [Partner Sales Console](https://channelservices.cloud.google.com/).
  2. Click **Private offer plans** , or navigate to your pending private offer plan from the **Alerts** section of the Partner Sales Console.
  3. For **Pending acceptance** , click a private offer plan to review its details.
  4. To accept the plan, click **Accept** , and then **Confirm acceptance**.



After you accept the plan, you use the Partner Sales Console to create private offers for your customers.

## Request a private offer plan from an ISV

To contact an ISV and request a private offer plan, see [Contact a Cloud Marketplace vendor](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/contact-vendor) in the documentation for Cloud Marketplace customers.
