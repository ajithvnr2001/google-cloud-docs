#  Resell Cloud Marketplace products from ISVs Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

As a Google Cloud Marketplace reseller, you can resell Cloud Marketplace products offered by independent software vendors (ISVs) to your customers. To resell Cloud Marketplace products, you use the _Partner Sales Console_ to do the following:

  * Accept _private offer plans_ from ISVs, which let you create private offers for your customers to buy the ISVs' products. For more information, see [Private offer plans for Cloud Marketplace resellers](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/resellers/reseller-private-offer-plans).
  * Create private offers for your customers to buy products offered by ISVs.
  * Manage your customers' orders of Cloud Marketplace products.



For more information about the Partner Sales Console, see the [Partner Sales Console help center](https://support.google.com/channelservices/answer/9789535).

ISVs can offer discounts as incentives to partners who resell their Cloud Marketplace products. When a customer purchases products that you resell, they don't see the amount of any reseller discount that you receive from the ISV. For more information, see [Request a reseller discount from an ISV](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/resellers/request-incentive).

If products listed on Google Cloud Marketplace aren't eligible for reselling, you can't resell them to your customers or use Cloud Billing accounts linked to your parent Cloud Billing account to purchase them. For a product to be eligible for reselling, the ISV offering the product must turn on reselling for their products. For more information, see [Turn on reselling for your products](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/resell/set-up-reselling#enable-reselling).

## Requirements

To resell Cloud Marketplace products, you must meet the following requirements:

  * You've been accepted in the _Google Cloud Partner Network program_. For more information, see [Join Google Cloud Partner Network](https://partners.cloud.google.com).
  * You're authorized in Google Cloud Partner Network's _Resale Subprogram_.

**Note:** As a reseller, you can't use your reseller Cloud Billing account to buy Cloud Marketplace products. To purchase products from Cloud Marketplace, you must use the resold customer Cloud Billing subaccounts that you set up for your customers. For more information, see [Get started: New Google Cloud resellers](https://support.google.com/channelservices/answer/10223784).

  * [Process requests from customers](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/resellers/process-entitlement-transfers) to change details of their orders.
