#  Available reports for your Cloud Marketplace products Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Depending on the type of products that you offer on Cloud Marketplace, you can receive the following reports:

  * [Charges and Usage reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/reports/report-charges-usage): Details of your software's usage.

  * [Disbursements reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/reports/report-disbursement): Details about the amount paid to you.

  * [Detailed disbursements reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/reports/report-detailed-disbursement): A detailed breakdown of the amount paid to you, for accounting and financial reconciliation purposes. We strongly recommend that you turn on this report.

  * [Customer (incremental) insights reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/reports/report-customer-insight): Information about who your customers are and how they're using your software, such as the number of virtual machines (VMs) that they've deployed your products on.

  * [VM customer tracking reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/reports/report-VM-customer-tracking): Contact information for customers who've purchased your VM products and consented to share this information with you.




To access your reports, visit the destination that you specified when you [set up to receive reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/reports/set-up-reports).
