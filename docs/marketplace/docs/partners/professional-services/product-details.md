#  Add product details for your professional services product Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

This document describes how to add product details for professional services that you offer through Cloud Marketplace. Product details include your product's title, image, marketing information, and links to documentation.

## Add product details

You add your product details in Producer Portal. The direct link to Producer Portal is:
    
    
    https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal?project=YOUR_PROJECT_ID
    

**Note:** If you don't see the link, or can't access the URL, verify that you've selected the correct project. If you still can't access the URL, use the [Partner Support Desk](https://g.co/cloud/psd-partner) to request assistance, and include the word "Marketplace" in your description. For more information about Partner Support Desk, visit [Request assistance with Cloud Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/get-support).

To add product details, complete the following steps:

  1. In Producer Portal, select your professional services product.

  2. From the **Overview** page of your product, go to the **Product details** section and click **Edit**.

  3. Add the title, tagline, product image, and description for your Professional services product's Cloud Marketplace listing.

  4. Select up to two categories from the **Category** drop-down. This helps customers find your product under select categories or filters as they search Cloud Marketplace.

  5. Under **Supported product listing** , paste the URL of the Cloud Marketplace listing for each of the products that your professional services product supports.

  6. Click **Next**.

  7. Under **Tutorials & documentation**, add documentation to support your professional services product.

  8. Optionally, under **Add more license agreements (Optional)** , add a custom license agreement for customers of your professional services product.

  9. Click **Next**.

  10. Under **Product metadata** , customize the product's details page URL by specifying a custom segment under **URL segment**.

  11. Optionally, add search metadata or keywords, separated by commas.

  12. Under **Show product in Marketplace search result** , use the toggle to decide if you want your professional services product to appear in public Cloud Marketplace search results.




After you've completed this section, click **Submit** to submit your product details for review.
