#  Publish your professional services product Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

To publish your professional services product on Google Cloud Marketplace, you must have submitted the following categories for review, and had them approved by Google:

  * **Product details**
  * **Pricing**
  * **Technical integration**



You can submit the **Product details** and **Pricing** categories to the Cloud Marketplace team for review in any order. You must submit the **Technical integration** review after your **Pricing** review has been approved.

## Verify your information

Before you submit your professional services product for publication, verify the information for your product in Producer Portal.

The direct link to Producer Portal is:
    
    
    https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal?project=YOUR_PUBLIC_PROJECT_ID
    

## Publish your product

To publish your product on Cloud Marketplace, you must have already completed the following requirements:

  * Complete the **Product details** , **Pricing** , and **Technical integration** reviews.

  * Be signed up as a [Google Cloud Marketplace vendor](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/get-started#vendor-agreement).




After you verify that you've met the preceding requirements, complete the following steps:

  1. Go to your product's **Overview** page in Producer Portal.

  2. Click **Publish**.
