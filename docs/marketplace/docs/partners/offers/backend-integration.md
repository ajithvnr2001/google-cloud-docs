#  Configure your SaaS product's backend for private offers Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

This document explains how to configure your software as a service (SaaS) product's backend for [Private Offers](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers).

You don't need to complete these steps for virtual machine (VM) or Kubernetes products.

For additional instructions about using entitlements to track usage, see [Configure your SaaS product's backend](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/integrated-saas/backend-integration).

## Before you begin

  * Your product must be integrated with, and listed on, Cloud Marketplace.

  * At least one of your pricing plans must be publicly available.

  * You must have onboarded the product to Producer Portal and configured your product's backend for the pricing plan that you're using for the offer. For steps, see [Configure your SaaS product's backend](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/integrated-saas/backend-integration).




## Manage entitlements for your private offers

For each SaaS product that you offer through Cloud Marketplace, you can choose whether or not to [turn on automatic offer approval](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/automatic-approval). You receive different Pub/Sub messages depending on whether or not your product has automatic offer approval turned on.
