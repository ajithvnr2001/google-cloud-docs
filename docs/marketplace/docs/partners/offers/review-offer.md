#  Review and publish your offer Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

This article describes how to review and publish a [private offer in Producer Portal](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/create-private-offers).

## Before you begin

Before you review and publish your offer, you must complete the following steps in Producer Portal:

  1. [Create a new offer](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/create-private-offers).

  2. [Add customer and product details](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/add-offer-details) to the offer.

  3. [Set up pricing](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/set-up-pricing-model) for your offer.

  4. [Specify the legal terms](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/select-eula) for your offer.




## Review your offer

To review your offer:

  1. In Producer Portal, navigate to the private offer that you're creating.

  2. Confirm the details of your offer. You don't see a calculated value for **Deal Vendor Net Revenue Percentage** , but the calculated value appears after you publish the offer.

  3. To see how the offer displays to the customer, click **More actions**. In the menu that appears, click **See customer's view**.




## Publish your offer

**Note:** You can't delete or modify an offer after you publish it. If you want to delete or modify a published offer, you must instead create a new offer to replace it.

For some offers, Cloud Marketplace requires that Google review and approve the offer before you publish it. This might take up to 24 hours. To see whether Google must review your offer, navigate to the offer in Producer Portal. If the [Vendor Net Revenue Schedule](https://cloud.google.com/terms/marketplace-revenue-share-schedule) applies to the offer, then Google must review and approve the offer if the following criteria are true:

  * The total contract value (TCV) is greater than US$10,000,000.
  * When the offer is created, the deal type field has one of the following values: 
    * `native renewal`
    * `channel shift`
    * `migration`

**Note:** Starting April 21, 2025, Google Cloud Marketplace calculates revenue share percentage for Cloud Marketplace transactions in accordance with the [Vendor Net Revenue Schedule](https://cloud.google.com/terms/marketplace-revenue-share-schedule), where applicable. For more information, see [Overview of the Vendor Net Revenue Schedule](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/migrations/variable-revenue-share).

If Cloud Marketplace requires that Google review and approve your offer, you see a **Continue** button. To submit your offer for review, click the **Continue** button. After you've clicked the button, the offer's status changes to `Pending Google Approval` until Google reviews the offer. After Google has reviewed and approved your offer, the offer's status changes to `Created`.

If Cloud Marketplace doesn't require that Google review and approve your offer, you see a **Publish** button. To publish your offer, click the **Publish** button. The offer publishes immediately after you click the button.

### View the offer's revenue share

After you publish the offer, the applicable revenue share appears in the **Deal Vendor Net Revenue Percentage** field. For offers with auto-renew turned on, the **Auto-renew** section shows the revenue share that applies to the renewal term of the offer after the offer automatically renews.

**Note:** In some cases, the revenue share might be marked as an estimate. This happens for offers with start dates based on customer acceptance that have proration turned on. After the customer accepts the offer, the revenue share stops being an estimate.

## What's next

  * You can now copy the generated URL and [send the offer to your customer](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/send-offer).
  * View [sample revenue share calculations for Cloud Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/revenue-share-scenarios).
