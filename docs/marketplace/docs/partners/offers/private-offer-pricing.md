#  Pricing for private offers Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

When you're working directly with a customer or reseller, you can customize the pricing of your Cloud Marketplace product by using [Private Offers](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers) to create a private offer.

The pricing of a private offer consists of the following components:

  * [Pricing model](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/select-pricing-model): The basis for how you customize your product's pricing plan.

  * [Billing frequency](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/select-payment-schedule): The frequency and timing for how the customer pays for the offer, and the offer's start and end dates or duration.

  * [Auto-renew](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/auto-renew) (optional): If you turn this on for an offer, and the customer has it turned on at the end of the offer's current term, the offer renews automatically.




## What's next

  * Select a [pricing model](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/select-pricing-model) for your private offer.

  * Select a [billing frequency](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/select-payment-schedule) for your private offer.

  * For information about auto-renew, visit [Automatic renewal for private offers](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/auto-renew).
