This article describes steps to download a [private offer](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers) as a PDF.

## Download an offer as a PDF

If you want to save an offer to share with others in your organization, you can download the offer as a PDF. The PDF includes:

  * The offer ID.

  * The state of the offer at the time the PDF was downloaded.

  * Information about the billing account for the offer.




Optionally, you can add the end user license agreement (EULA) included with the offer and any internal notes.

On the **Private Offers** page, click **Manage your offers** more_vert to download the offer as a PDF.

## Download a PDF of a previous version of an offer

You can use the **Key Events** widget to download a PDF of an offer at various steps in its history. For more information, visit [View the history of an offer](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/view-offer-status#key-events).
