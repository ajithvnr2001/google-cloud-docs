#  Reporting for private offers Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

The reports that you receive for your Cloud Marketplace products include information about your private offers. To access your reports, visit the destination (or destinations) that you specified when you [set up to receive reports](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/reports/set-up-reports).

In your reports, the following fields appear for your private offers:

  * **Quote_id:** If you've generated revenue from a private offer, the quote ID associated with the offer's product and billing account.
  * **Quote_creator:** If you've generated revenue from a private offer, the email address of the offer creator.
  * **Payment schedule** : The schedule for when the customer pays for a private offer, either Prepay or Postpay.
  * **Prepay credits** : For private offers that use a prepay payment schedule, the amount of prepay credits burndown.



For additional fields and information about reporting, visit [Reports for your Google Cloud Marketplace products](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/reporting).

**Note:** When you view reports for your Cloud Marketplace products, for prepay offers with the [committed use discounts (CUDs) pricing model](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/select-pricing-model#CUD), you don't see whether commitment expires or rolls over between installments. To see this information for an offer, view the offer in Producer Portal.
