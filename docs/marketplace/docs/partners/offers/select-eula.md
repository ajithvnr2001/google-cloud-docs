#  Specify your end user license agreement (EULA) Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

This document describes how to specify the end user license agreement (EULA) for a [private offer in Producer Portal](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/create-private-offers).

## Before you begin

Before you specify your EULA, you must complete the following steps in Producer Portal:

  1. [Create an offer](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/create-private-offers).

  2. [Add customer and product details](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/add-offer-details) to the offer.

  3. [Set up pricing](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/set-up-pricing-model) for your offer.




## Specify your EULA

In Producer Portal, navigate to the private offer you're creating. Under **Legal terms** , specify whether you want to use the usual EULA for your product or provide a custom EULA for this offer.

### Standard EULA

To review a standard EULA or other applicable agreement, see the following links. To use one or more of the following standard agreements, download and attach them to the private offer as described in the Custom EULA section.

  * [Bonterms Standard End User Agreement](https://console.cloud.google.com/tos?id=marketplace-standard-end-user-agreement)

  * [Bonterms Standard Reseller Agreement (for Marketplaces)](https://console.cloud.google.com/tos?id=marketplace-standard-reseller-agreement)

  * [Bonterms Professional Services Agreement](https://console.cloud.google.com/tos?id=standard-professional-services-agreement)




These are standard agreements created by [Bonterms](https://bonterms.com/), which are free to use and licensed under the open-source document license CC BY-ND.

### Custom EULA

If you want to provide a custom EULA:

  1. Use the **Attach a custom EULA** field to select and upload your custom EULA. Your custom EULA file must be:

     * In PDF format.

     * Less than or equal to the maximum file size of 4 MB.

  2. In the **Custom display name** field, specify the name that you want your customers to see for your custom EULA when they view the offer.

  3. Click **Next**.




## What's next

After you've specified your private offer's EULA, you're ready to [review and publish](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offers/review-offer) your offer.
