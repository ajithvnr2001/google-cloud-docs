To publish your Terraform Kubernetes app to Google Cloud Marketplace, you submit aspects of your app for review in Producer Portal. You can submit the following reviews to the Cloud Marketplace team in any order:

  * **Product details**
  * **Pricing**



After your pricing review is approved, you can submit the **Deployment configuration** review.

After you complete all of the preceding reviews, use Producer Portal to publish your app to Cloud Marketplace and then, optionally, make it public to all Cloud Marketplace users.

## Verify your information

Before you publish your Terraform Kubernetes app, verify your app's information in Producer Portal.

The direct link to Producer Portal is:
    
    
    https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal?project=YOUR_PUBLIC_PROJECT_ID
    

### Make changes to your product after submitting it for review

If you find a mistake in your product, or if your contact at Google requests changes to it, complete the following steps:

  1. In Producer Portal, navigate to the page that you'd like to make changes to.
  2. To acknowledge that you must re-submit the corresponding review for your product if you change it, click **Acknowledge**.
  3. Make the changes to your product.
  4. Re-submit the affected review for approval.



## Publish your app privately

After you complete the **Product details** , **Pricing** , and **Deployment configuration** reviews, complete the following steps to publish your Terraform Kubernetes app to Cloud Marketplace:

  1. In Producer Portal, navigate to your product's **Overview** page.
  2. Click **Publish**.



This publishes your app _privately_ to Cloud Marketplace, which means that it isn't publicly accessible to all Cloud Marketplace users.

## Make your published app publicly accessible

To make your published Terraform Kubernetes app publicly accessible on Cloud Marketplace, complete the following steps:

  1. In Producer Portal, go to your app's **Overview** page.

  2. Click **Enable public display**.

**Note:** If you don't see this button, verify that you've published your product privately. For more information, see Publish your app, in this document.
  3. Click **Make public**.
