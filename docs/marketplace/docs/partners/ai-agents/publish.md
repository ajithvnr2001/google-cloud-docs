To publish your AI agent to Google Cloud Marketplace, you must have completed the following steps in Producer Portal:

  * Complete the **Product details** , **Pricing** , and **Technical integration** reviews for your AI agent.
  * Validate your AI agent's Agent Card.



You can submit the **Product details** and **Pricing** tabs to the Cloud Marketplace team for review in any order. However, you must have successfully completed the pricing review before you submit your **Technical integration** tab for review.

After you've successfully completed the preceding reviews, you can submit your AI agent to Cloud Marketplace for validation. Submitting your AI agent doesn't publish it publicly immediately, but allows Google operations to start validation and testing. After validation is complete, you are provided with a `gcloud` command to make your listing publicly visible.

## Before you begin

  * To access Producer Portal, you must have completed the _Cloud Marketplace Project Info Form_ , provided by the Google Cloud Marketplace team. For more information about becoming a Cloud Marketplace vendor, see [Sign up as a Cloud Marketplace vendor](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/offer-products#initiate-onboarding).

If you can't access Producer Portal, verify that you've selected the correct project. If you still can't access it, use the [Google Cloud Support portal](https://g.co/cloud/psd-partner) to request assistance, and include the word "Marketplace" in your description. For more information about the Google Cloud Support portal, visit [Request assistance with Cloud Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/get-support).

  * Verify that you've successfully completed the required reviews and validation in Producer Portal. For details, see [Overview of onboarding an AI agent](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/ai-agents#onboard).




## Publish your AI agent

To submit your AI agent for review, use [Producer Portal](https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal) to complete the following steps:

  1. In Producer Portal, go to your product's **Overview** page.

  2. To preview your product's listing, click **Preview**.

  3. To submit your product for review, click **Submit for review**. If you have already published your product and are making updates, click **Publish changes** instead.

After your product is published publicly, its listing becomes visible in both Cloud Marketplace and Gemini Enterprise. You can manage updates to both pages directly from Producer Portal.




### View your AI agent's issue tracker

After your AI agent launches, your Partner Engineer creates a public issue tracker for your product, similar to the [Google Cloud issue trackers](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/issue-trackers), to track bugs and feature requests from your users.

## Make changes to your AI agent after you've submitted sections for review

If you find a mistake in your product, or if your contact at Google requests changes to it:

  1. Navigate to the page you'd like to make changes to.

  2. Click **Acknowledge** to acknowledge that you must re-submit the corresponding review for your product if you change it.

  3. Make any intended changes to your product.

  4. Re-submit the affected review for approval.
