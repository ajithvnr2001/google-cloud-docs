#  Test your SaaS product integration in Producer Portal Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

Before you publish a SaaS product, you use Producer Portal to preview and test it.

## Before you begin

To avoid unexpected costs when testing your products, use a Test Billing Account, which has 100% discounted usage. For steps to set up a Test Billing Account, see [Create a Test Billing Account](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/create-test-billing-account).

## Overview

Testing your SaaS product integration with Producer Portal includes the following steps:

  * [Preview your product in Producer Portal](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/integrated-saas/test-preview), to see how it appears to Google Cloud Marketplace users.
  * [Preview your product's pricing](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/integrated-saas/test-preview-pricing).
  * [Test your product's usage reporting](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/integrated-saas/test-usage-reporting).

**Note:** If you use App Lifecycle Manager to deploy and operate your SaaS product, you can also take advantage of streamlined validation and tenant onboarding. For more information, see [Integrate with App Lifecycle Manager](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/integrate-app-lifecycle-manager).
