#  Publish your SaaS product Stay organized with collections  Save and categorize content based on your preferences. 

This page describes the steps you need to take after you have completed [integrating your app with Cloud Marketplace](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/integrated-saas/technical-integration-setup).

**Note:** If you use App Lifecycle Manager to deploy and operate your SaaS product, you can also take advantage of streamlined validation and tenant onboarding. For more information, see [Integrate with App Lifecycle Manager](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/integrate-app-lifecycle-manager).

## Verify your information

Before you submit your SaaS product for review, verify the information for your product in Producer Portal.

The direct link to Producer Portal is:
    
    
    https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal?project=YOUR_PUBLIC_PROJECT_ID
    

After your product is launched, your Partner Engineer creates a public issue tracker for your product, similar to the [Google Cloud issue trackers](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/issue-trackers), to track bugs and feature requests from your users.

## Submit your product for review

To submit your product for review, you use Producer Portal.

The direct link to Producer Portal is:
    
    
    https://console.cloud.google.com/producer-portal?project=YOUR_PUBLIC_PROJECT_ID
    

You can submit the following reviews to the Cloud Marketplace team in any order:

  * Product details

  * Pricing




You must submit the following review after your pricing review is approved:

  * Technical integration



### Make changes to your product after submitting it for review

If you find a mistake in your product, or if your contact at Google requests changes to it:

  1. Navigate to the page you'd like to make changes to.

  2. Click **Acknowledge** to acknowledge that you must re-submit the corresponding review for your product if you change it.

  3. Make any intended changes to your product.

  4. Re-submit the affected review for approval.




## Publish your product

To publish your product on Cloud Marketplace, you must:

  * Complete the **Product details** , **Pricing** , and **Technical integration** reviews.

  * Be signed up as a [Google Cloud Marketplace vendor](https://docs.cloud.google.com/marketplace/docs/partners/get-started#vendor-agreement).




After you verify that you've met the preceding requirements:

  1. Go to your product's **Overview** page in Producer Portal.

  2. Click **Publish**.




### View your product's issue tracker

After your product is launched, your Partner Engineer creates a public issue tracker for your product, similar to the [Google Cloud issue trackers](https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/docs/issue-trackers), to track bugs and feature requests from your users.
